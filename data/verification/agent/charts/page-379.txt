ROOT [Indo-European] Pa, Pa-ter, Poti, Potiš : Pa: to protect, to feed. Pa-ter: protector, feeder (of the family), father ☞ Ter 3. Poti, Potiš: protector, husband, power, also self. This has probably started as a nursery word and became a usual term in the Indo-European languages. Compare with the root "Baba".  (refs POK:787,842; SYN:103; KAS:10)  PART 1
