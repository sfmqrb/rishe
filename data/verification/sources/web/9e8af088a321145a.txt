 Etymology 1
 [ edit ] 

 Etymologically related to שָׁלָה ( šālâ , “ to be quiet, tranquil ” ) . Compare also שָׁלַו ( šālaw , “ to be quiet, at ease ” ) . Common Northwest Semitic , Phoenician 𐤔𐤋𐤌 ( šlm /⁠šalōm⁠/ ) , Aramaic שְׁלָמָא ( šlāmā ) / ܫܠܡܐ ( šlāmā ) , Ugaritic 𐎌𐎍𐎎 ( šlm /⁠šalāmu⁠/ ) . Cognate with Arabic سلام ( salām ) .

 Usage notes
 [ edit ] 

 Only the first sense ("peace") is used productively as a standalone noun in Modern Hebrew. The second sense ("rest") only appears in fixed expressions, and the third sense ("well-being") is almost exclusively used in the construct state, e.g. אני דואג ל שלומו ( aní do'ég le- shlomó , “ I am worried about his well-being ” ) .
