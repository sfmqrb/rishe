 This Proto-Indo-European entry contains reconstructed terms and roots . As such, the term(s) in this entry are not directly attested , but are hypothesized to have existed based on comparative evidence.

 Proto-Indo-European
 [ edit ] 

 Root
 [ edit ] 

 *h₁ers- [ 1 ] [ 2 ] [ 3 ] 

 to flow 

 to roam , to err [ 4 ] 

 Reconstruction notes
 [ edit ] 

 Differently, a root *h₁reh₁s- ( “ to rush ” ) is reconstructed for Ancient Greek ἐρωή ( erōḗ , “ rush, impulse ” ) , Proto-Germanic *rēsō ( “ running, rush (of water) ” ) and Latin rōrāriī ( “ skirmishers ” ) . [ 5 ]
