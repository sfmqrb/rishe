 Middle Persian 𐭧𐭥𐭫𐭱𐭩𐭲 ( ḥwlšyt /⁠xwar(x)šēd⁠⁠/ ) 
 Persian خورشید 

 Inherited from Middle Persian 𐭧𐭥𐭫𐭱𐭩𐭲 ( ḥwlšyt /⁠xwar(x)šēd⁠⁠/ ) . Compare Avestan 𐬵𐬎𐬎𐬀𐬭𐬆-𐬑𐬱𐬀𐬉𐬙𐬀- ( huuarə-xšaēta- , “ bright sun (an epithet) ” ) . By surface analysis , خور ( xwar  / xor , “ sun ” ) +‎ شید ( šēd  / šid , “ shine ” ) .

 The first part is related to خراسان ( xurāsān  / xorâsân ) , the name of a province in eastern Iran (since the sun rises in the east), and the second part with Old Armenian աշխէտ ( ašxēt ) . [ 1 ]
