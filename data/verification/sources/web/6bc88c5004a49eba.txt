 This Proto-Turkic entry contains reconstructed terms and roots . As such, the term(s) in this entry are not directly attested , but are hypothesized to have existed based on comparative evidence.

 Proto-Turkic
 [ edit ] 

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 *kadïŋ 

 birch tree
