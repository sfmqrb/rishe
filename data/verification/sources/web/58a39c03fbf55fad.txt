 Etymology 1
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian [Book Pahlavi needed] ( gwd /⁠gōy⁠/ , “ ball ” ) . Cognate with Sanskrit गोल ( gola ) , Persian گلوله ( golule , “ pellet; bullet ” ) , Northern Luri گولٛاْ ( goła , “ pellet ” ) , Central Kurdish گولە ( gule ) and گۆ ( go , “ ball ” ) , Northern Kurdish gog ( “ ball ” ) , Zazaki gudi ( “ ball; round ” ) and an Iranian loanword in Classical Syriac ܓܘܝܐ ( gōyāʾ ) .
