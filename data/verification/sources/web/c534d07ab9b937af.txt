 Irish uisce 

 From Old Irish uisce , [ 1 ] from Proto-Celtic *udenskyos , from Proto-Indo-European *wódr̥, *udéns . [ 2 ] Cognate with Scottish Gaelic uisge , Manx ushtey .

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 IPA ( key ) : /ˈiʃcə/ [ 3 ] [ 4 ] , [ˈiʃcɪ] [ 5 ] 

 Audio : ( file ) 

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 uisce   m ( genitive singular uisce , nominative plural uiscí ) 

 water 
 Synonym: dobhar 
 2015 [ 2014 ], Will Collins, translated by Proinsias Mac a' Bhaird, edited by Maura McHugh, Amhrán na Mara (fiction; paperback), Kilkenny, County Kilkenny; Howth, Dublin: Cartoon Saloon; Coiscéim, translation of Song of the Sea (in English), →ISBN , page 2: Tá rónta ag bogadaíl ar bharr an uisce . [ original: Seals bob up and down in the water . ]
