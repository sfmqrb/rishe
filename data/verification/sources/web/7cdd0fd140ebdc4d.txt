 This Proto-Indo-European entry contains reconstructed terms and roots . As such, the term(s) in this entry are not directly attested , but are hypothesized to have existed based on comparative evidence.

 Proto-Indo-European
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 The root is unknown. This etymology is incomplete. You can help Wiktionary by elaborating on the origins of this term. 

 The Tocharian descendants point to a variant *ǵʰn̥dwéh₂- , which has a metathetical relationship to *dn̥ǵʰwéh₂- , but it is unknown whether the metathesis occurred in the branch leading to Tocharian or that leading to the rest of Nuclear Indo-European. On the other hand, Proto-Germanic *tungǭ could point to a different formation from the same root, *dn̥ǵʰ-éh₂-(m̥/n̥) , rather than being a direct descendant from here.

 Noun
 [ edit ]

 tongue 

 Alternative reconstructions
 [ edit ] 

 *dn̥ǵʰ-uh₂ [ 2 ] [ 3 ] 

 *d(é)nǵʰ-uh₂ ~ *dn̥ǵʰ-wéh₂s [ 4 ] 

 Reconstruction notes
 [ edit ] 

 None of the descendants reflect the form reconstructed here exactly by regular sound laws only; Old Latin dinguā comes the closest (only lacking nominative *-s ). Onomatopoeic effects and parallel contaminations from *leyǵʰ- ( “ to lick ” ) explain many but not all of these irregularities.
