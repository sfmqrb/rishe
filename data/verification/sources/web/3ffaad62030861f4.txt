 Middle Persian
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Inherited from Old Persian *gauθrā , borrowed from Old Median *gauθrā , from Proto-Iranian *gawθraH ( “ jewel ” ) , related to *gawθrám ( “ family, lineage, growth ” ) . Cognate with Northern Kurdish gewher , Bactrian γωυριγο ( gōurigo ) , Pashto غوره ( ǧwëra ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 gwhl • ( gōhr, gōhar ) 

 substance , essence , nature 
 Wizīdagīhā ī Zādspram , chapter 3, line 28
 PWN 1000 ŠNT gwhl Y kwp BYN zmyk' YHBWNt YKOYMWNʾt 
 pad hazār sāl gōhr ī kōf andar zamīg dād ēstād
 for a thousand years the substance of the mountains had been created in the earth. 

 jewel 

 stock , lineage
