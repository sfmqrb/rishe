 Persian
 [ edit ] 

 Alternative forms
 [ edit ] 

 پائیدن ( pâ'idan ) 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian p'dn, p'y- ( /⁠pādan, pāy-⁠/ , “ to protect, guard; to wait, stand ” ) , from Old Persian 𐎱𐎠𐎮𐎹 ( pā- , “ to protect ” ) , from Proto-Iranian *paH- , from Proto-Indo-Iranian *paH- , from Proto-Indo-European *peh₂- ( “ to protect ” ) .

 Verb
 [ edit ] 

 پاییدن • ( pāyīdan  / pâyidan ) ( present stem پای ( pāy  / pây ) , Tajik spelling поидан ) 

 to protect , guard 

 to stand firm ; to be firm 

 to be constant ; to be steady , fixed , established 

 to watch , see , surveil ; to mind 
 بپا ! ― bepâ ! ― Watch out ! 

 to police 

 to subsist independently of anything

 to fix the look steadily on any object

 to wait , expect
