 Arabic
 [ edit ] 

 Root 

 س ك ن ( s k n ) 

 21 terms 

 Etymology 1
 [ edit ] 

 An Arabic formation from the root س ك ن ( s k n ) , but religious meanings are a semantic loan from Hebrew שְׁכִינָה ( shəḵīná ) .

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 IPA ( key ) : /sa.kiː.na/ 

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 سَكِينَة • ( sakīna )  f ( plural سَكَائِن ( sakāʔin ) ) 

 inner peace , tranquility , calmness 

 ( religion ) presence of God , shechinah 
 609–632 CE , Qur'an , 48:4 : هُوَ ٱلَّذِىٓ أَنزَلَ ٱلسَّكِينَةَ فِى قُلُوبِ ٱلْمُؤْمِنِينَ He is the One Who sent down serenity upon the hearts of the believers.
