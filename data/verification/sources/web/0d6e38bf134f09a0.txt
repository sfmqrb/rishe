 Uyghur
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Proto-Turkic *top .

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 IPA ( key ) : /top/ 

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 توپ • ( top ) ( plural توپلار ( toplar ) ) 

 ball 
 پەي توپ ― pey top ― a shuttlecock; badminton (the game) 

 masses , people 
 كىشىلەر توپى ― kishiler topi ― the mass of people, the masses , the people 

 a flock , swarm , crowd 
 بىر توپ چىكەتكە ― bir top chiketke ― a swarm of locusts 

 bolt 
 بىر توپ رەخت ― bir top rext ― a bolt of cloth 

 in a large quantity, in bulk
 توپ ساتماق ― top satmaq ― to sell wholesale 

 a measure word for flock, etc.
 بىر توپ پادا قوي ― bir top pada qoy ― a flock of sheep 

 cannon ; artillery 
 توپ ئاتماق ― top atmaq ― to fire a cannon , to shell 

 ( chess ) rook
