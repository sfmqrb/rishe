 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Sanskritization of Pali वेळुरिय ( veḷuriya , “ beryl ” ) or Prakrit वेलुरिअ ( veluria ) , derived from the name of a Dravidian mining town. See there for more.

 Traditionally explained (in Aṣṭādhyāyī 4.3.84 by Pāṇini ) as a mineral named after the city of विदूर ( vidūra ) , corresponding to (modern-day Bēlūr ).

 The original meaning of the South Indian word was "white crystals of quartz and beryl" (preserved in Arabic and Persian borrowing), and later "coloured (blue and green) beryl", as retained in Pali and Sanskrit.

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 ( Classical Sanskrit ) IPA ( key ) : /ʋɑi̯.ɖuːɾ.jɐ/ 

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 वैडूर्य • ( vaiḍūrya ) stem ,  n or m 

 cat's eye ( AdbhB. , MBh. , Kāv. etc.)

 ( at the end of a compound ) a jewel ; anything excellent of its kind

 name of a particular mountain (also वैडूर्यपर्वत ( vaiḍūryaparvata ) ) ( MBh. , VarBṛS. etc.)

 ( Buddhist Hybrid Sanskrit ) lapis lazuli
