 Etymology 1
 [ edit ] 

 PIE word 
 *wáy 

 From Proto-Iranian *wáy , from Proto-Indo-Iranian *wáy , eventually from Proto-Indo-European *wáy .

 Cognate with Latin vae , English woe . Similar words also exist in Semitic, compare Aramaic וָי ( wāy ) , Arabic وَيْل ( wayl ) .

 Interjection
 [ edit ] 

 وای • ( wāy  / vây ) ( Tajik spelling вой ) 

 woe !, alas !, oh dear !
