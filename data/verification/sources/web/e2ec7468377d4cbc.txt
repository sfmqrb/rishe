 This Proto-Indo-European entry contains reconstructed terms and roots . As such, the term(s) in this entry are not directly attested , but are hypothesized to have existed based on comparative evidence.

 Proto-Indo-European
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From earlier *ḱwón-s . Hamp has suggested derivation from *péḱu [ 1 ] +‎ *-ō , perhaps with a semantic shift from "he of the sheep" to "sheepdog." However, according to the EIEC, this theory is unlikely, as there is no trace of initial *p- in any of the descendants and *péḱu probably did not only refer to sheep. [ 2 ] The term has also been compared with Old Chinese 犬 ( *kʰʷeːnʔ , “ dog ” ) , [ 3 ] from Proto-Sino-Tibetan *d-kʷəj-n .
