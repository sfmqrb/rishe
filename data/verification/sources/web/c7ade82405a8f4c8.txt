 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Of Pre-Greek origin, [ 1 ] possibly Semitic (often thought to be Phoenician for “man from the east”), from Proto-Semitic *q-d-m ( “ to precede, come before ” ) , with influence from κέκασμαι ( kékasmai , “ to have excelled, shined ” ) . Compare Classical Syriac ܩܕܡ ( “ to come before ” ) , Arabic قدم , Hebrew קדם .
