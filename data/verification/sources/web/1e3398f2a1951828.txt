 Etymology 1
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology tree

 Akkadian 𒁇 ( ṣalāpu ) bor. 
 Aramaic צְלִיבָא ( ṣəlīḇā ) bor. 
 Arabic صليب 

 Borrowed from Aramaic צְלִיבָא ( ṣəlīḇā ) , borrowed from Akkadian 𒁇 ( ṣalāpu , “ to cross out, to pervert; to give difficulty or be crooked, not be easy or straight ” ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 صَلِيب • ( ṣalīb )  m ( plural صُلْبَان ( ṣulbān ) or صُلُب ( ṣulub ) ) 

 cross ( geometrical figure ) 
 اَلصَّلِيب اَلْجَنُوبِيّ ― aṣ-ṣalīb al-janūbiyy ― Southern Cross 

 ( also Christianity ) cross , Cross , crucifix ( post for crucifixion ) 
 7th century CE , Ṣaḥīḥ al-Buḵāriyy , 46:37 : لَا تَقُومُ السَّاعَةُ حَتَّى يَنْزِلَ فِيكُمُ ٱبْنُ مَرْيَمَ حَكَمًا مُقْسِطًا، فَيَكْسِرَ الصَّلِيبَ ، وَيَقْتُلَ الْخِنْزِيرَ، وَيَضَعَ الْجِزْيَةَ، وَيَفِيضَ الْمَالُ حَتَّى لَا يَقْبَلَهُ أَحَدٌ lā taqūmu s-sāʕatu ḥattā yanzila fīkumu bnu maryama ḥakaman muqsiṭan, fayaksira aṣ-ṣalība , wayaqtula l-ḵinzīra, wayaḍaʕa l-jizyata, wayafīḍa l-mālu ḥattā lā yaqbalahu ʔaḥadun The hour will not arise until the son of Mary comes down upon you as a just ruler, and he will destroy the cross , and he will kill the swine, and abolish the jizya, and fortunes will overflow so that nobody will accept them as gifts.
