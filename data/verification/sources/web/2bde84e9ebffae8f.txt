 Persian Wikipedia has an article on: اردشیر 

 Wikipedia fa 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Inherited from Middle Persian 𐭠𐭥𐭲𐭧𐭱𐭲𐭥 ( Ardaxšīr ) , from Old Persian 𐎠𐎼𐎫𐎧𐏁𐏂𐎠 ( Artaxšaçā ) , which was borrowed into Ancient Greek , Latin , and then English as Artaxerxes ; and into Sanskrit as ऋतक्षयर्ष ( ṛtakṣayarṣa ) and अर्तक्षयर्ष ( artakṣayarṣa ) .
