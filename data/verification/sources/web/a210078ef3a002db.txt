 Abtin ( Persian : آبتین ), or Athwya , is a character in Shahnama (national epic of Iran ), who is the father of Fereydun . He is mentioned as the father of Fereydun in the Avesta , having been the "second man" to prepare Ahura-Mazda for the "corporeal world" (the first being Jamshid , and the third being Thrita ). [ 1 ] His name comes from the same origin as " Āptya ", a title for water-born deities or heroes in the Rigveda . According to the Shatapatha Brahmana , that the first to bear this title was Agni , and that he subsequently created three Aptyas, Trita , Dvita, and Ekata, when he spat on the waters in anger. [ 2 ]
