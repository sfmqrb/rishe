 This Proto-Indo-European entry contains reconstructed terms and roots . As such, the term(s) in this entry are not directly attested , but are hypothesized to have existed based on comparative evidence.

 Proto-Indo-European
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Likely from *dwi- , [ 1 ] with *d dropped, or possibly shifted to a *h₁ sound due to the Kortlandt effect, which proposes that *d became *h₁ in certain positions.

 Adverb
 [ edit ] 

 *wí [ 1 ] 

 apart , asunder 

 away from, against 

 Alternative reconstructions
 [ edit ] 

 *h₁wi- [ 2 ] ( laryngeal deduced from long scansion of augment in अविधत् ( ávidhat ) , see *h₁weydʰh₁- )
