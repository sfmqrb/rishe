 Ottoman Turkish
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Borrowed from Classical Persian دَنْدَان ( dandān , “ tooth ” ) , from Middle Persian 𐫅𐫗𐫅𐫀𐫗 ( dndʾn /⁠dandān⁠/ ) , ultimately from Proto-Indo-Iranian *Hdántā ( “ tooth ” ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 دندان • ( dendân ) ( definite accusative دندانی ( dendânı ) , plural دندانلر ( dendânlar ) ) 

 tooth , a hard, calcareous structure present in the mouth of humans and many animals 
 Synonyms: دیش ( diş ) , سن ( senn ) 

 serrature , a notching , like that between the teeth of a saw , in the edge of anything

 Derived terms
 [ edit ] 

 دندان مزد ( dendân müzd , “ exaction ” ) 
 دندانه ( dendâne , “ anything shaped like a tooth ” )
