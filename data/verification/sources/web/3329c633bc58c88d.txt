 3 terms 

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 IPA ( key ) : /ʔatˤ.las/ 

 Adjective
 [ edit ] 

 أَطْلَس • ( ʔaṭlas ) ( feminine طَلْسَاء ( ṭalsāʔ ) , masculine plural طُلْس ( ṭuls ) ) 

 worn-out , having the quality of a rag 

 filthy , having a dusty hue 

 having a skin of dark pigmentation .
