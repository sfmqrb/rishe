 Noun

 Jawi spelling of stor .‎

 Persian
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian stwl ( /⁠stōr⁠/ , “ horse, mount; large cattle ” ) , from Avestan 𐬯𐬙𐬀𐬊𐬭𐬀 ( staora , “ large cattle ” ) , from Proto-Iranian *stáwrah , from Proto-Indo-Iranian *stáwras ( “ large domestic animal, cattle ” ) , from Proto-Indo-European *(s)táwros ( “ wild bull, aurochs ” ) . Probably related to obsolete تاول ( tâvel , “ bull, ox ” ) .
