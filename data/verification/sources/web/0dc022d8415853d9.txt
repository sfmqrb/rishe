 This Proto-Indo-European entry contains reconstructed terms and roots . As such, the term(s) in this entry are not directly attested , but are hypothesized to have existed based on comparative evidence.

 Proto-Indo-European
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Of uncertain origin; [ 1 ] [ 2 ] possibly from *h₂uks-ḗn , from *h₂weks- ( “ to become strong ” ) [ 3 ] [ 4 ] [ 5 ] +‎ *-ḗn , or perhaps from *ugʷ-s-ḗn ( “ inseminator ” ) , from *wegʷ- ( “ to moisten, make wet ” ) . [ 6 ] Alternatively borrowed from Proto-Kartvelian *usx- ( “ sacrificial bull ” ) with metathesis [ 7 ] [ 8 ] , or a Wanderwort , whence also Proto-Turkic *öküŕ ( “ ox, bull ” ) . [ 9 ] Compare Proto-Nakh *ustu ( “ bull, ox ” ) .
