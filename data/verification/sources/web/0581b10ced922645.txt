 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 کنگره • ( kongore ) ( plural کنگره‌ها ) 

 merlon , battlement 

 notch , tooth ( of a tool ) 
 Synonym: دندانه ( dandâne ) 

 pinnacle , turret

 Etymology 2
 [ edit ] 

 Borrowed from French congrès , from Latin congressus . As a proper noun, a calque of English Congress .

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ]
