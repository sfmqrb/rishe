 Arabic
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Tool noun derivative of إِقْلِيد ( ʔiqlīd , “ key ” ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 مِقْلَاد • ( miqlād )  m ( plural مَقَالِيد ( maqālīd ) ) 

 key 
 Synonym: مِفْتَاح ( miftāḥ ) 
 609–632 CE , Qur'an , 42:12 : لَهُ مَقَالِيدُ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضِ يَبْسُطُ الرِّزْقَ لِمَن يَشَاءُ وَيَقْدِرُ إِنَّهُ بِكُلِّ شَيْءٍ عَلِيمٌ lahu maqālīdu s-samāwāti wālʔarḍi yabsuṭu r-rizqa liman yašāʔu wayaqdiru ʔinnahu bikulli šayʔin ʕalīmun To Him pertain the keys of the heavens and the earth. He extends provision for whom He wills and restricts [it]. Indeed He is of all things knowing. 

 switch 
 Synonym: مِفْتَاح ( miftāḥ ) 
 أَغْلَقَ/فَتَحَ مِقْلَادًا وَشَغَّلَ/أَوْقَفَ الْجِهَازَ‎ ʔaḡlaqa/fataḥa miqlādan wašaḡḡala/ʔawqafa l-jihāza (please add an English translation of this usage example) 

 يَسْمَحُ مِقْلَادٌ بِمُرُورِ التَّيَّارِ الْكَهْرَبَائِيِّ عِنْدَ إِغْلَاقِهِ وَيَمْنَعُ الْمُرُورَ عِنْدَ فَتْحِهِ yasmaḥu miqlādun bimurūri t-tayyāri l-kahrabāʔiyyi ʕinda ʔiḡlāqihi wayamnaʕu l-murūra ʕinda fatḥihi A switch allows the passage of electric current when it is turned on and prevents its passage when it is turned off.
