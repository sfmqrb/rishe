 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology tree

 Arabic يَمَنَ ( yamana ) der. 
 Arabic مَيْمُون ( maymūn ) bor. 
 Ottoman Turkish میمون ( maymun ) 
 Turkish maymun 

 Inherited from Ottoman Turkish میمون ( maymun , “ monkey ” ) , borrowed from Arabic مَيْمُون ( maymūn , “ baboon, mandrill ” ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 maymun ( definite accusative maymunu , plural maymunlar ) 

 monkey , ape , primate , simian , any mammal in the order Primates except hominids 
 Synonym: primat
