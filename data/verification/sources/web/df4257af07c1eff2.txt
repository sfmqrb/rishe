 Persian
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian [Book Pahlavi needed] ( hstn' /⁠xastan⁠/ , “ to wound, injure ” ) , [ 1 ] itself from Proto-Iranian *xad- ( “ to beat, strike, inflict a wound, hurt ” ) , of uncertain further origin. Tentatively compared with Sanskrit कदन ( kadana , “ destruction ” ) and Ancient Greek κέκᾰδον ( kékădon , “ robbing ” ) , though these come with phonetic difficulties. [ 2 ] 
Within Iranian, cognate with Northern Kurdish xistin ( “ to hit, put, get, drop; causative of ketin ” ) , Central Kurdish and Southern Kurdish خستن ( xistin , “ to throw, hit ” ) , Gurani ۋستەی , Khotanese khasta- ( “ beaten, pained ” ) , Parthian xd- ( xad- , “ to injure ” ) , xdm ( xaδm , “ wound ” ) , etc.

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 IPA ( key ) : [xæsˈtæn]
