 Persian

 Alternative forms
 [ edit ] 

 افراختن ( afrâxtan ) 

 افرازیدن ( afrâzîdan , “ present stem infinitive ” ) 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian [script needed] ( ʾplʾstn' /⁠abrāštan⁠/ , “ to lift, raise ” ) , is a compound verb, so the elements are *af- from Proto-Iranian *Hápa ( “ off, away, from ” ) or Proto-Indo-European *upó ( “ under, below, towards ” ) + *rāš- from Proto-Indo-Iranian *Hraštás ( “ straightened, right ” ) .

 Verb
 [ edit ] 

 افراشتن • ( afrâštan ) ( present stem افراز ( afrâz ) ) 

 to raise , to elevate , to lift , to exalt , to erect 
 c. 1260s , Jalāl ad-Dīn Mohammad Rūmī , translated by Reynold A. Nicholson , مثنوی معنوی [ Masnavi-ye-Ma'navi ], volume I, verse 3008: چیست تعظیم خدا افراشتن / خویشتن را خوار و خاکی داشتن čîst ta'zîm-i-xudâ afrâštan / xwêš-atân râ xwâr u xaki dâštan What is (the meaning of) to exalt and glorify God? / To deem yourself despicable and (worthless) as dust.
