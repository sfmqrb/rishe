 Alternative forms

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian [Book Pahlavi needed] ( lwstʾk' /⁠rōstāg⁠/ ) , whence Classical Syriac ܪܘܣܬܩܐ ( rustāqā ) , Arabic رُسْتَاق ( rustāq ) . Akin to Old Armenian ռոտաստակ ( ṙotastak ) borrowed from the Parthian cognate. This etymology is incomplete. You can help Wiktionary by elaborating on the origins of this term.
