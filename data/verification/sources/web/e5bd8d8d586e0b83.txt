 Aramaic
 [ edit ] 

 Verb
 [ edit ] 

 עקב • ( transliteration needed ) 

 to search , to investigate 

 to trip (literally "to hold by the heel")

 Hebrew
 [ edit ] 

 Root 

 ע־ק־ב ( ʿ-q-b ) 

 3 terms 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Related to Arabic عاقب ( “ to guard, follow at the heel ” ) . [ 1 ]

 Usage notes
 [ edit ] 

 The plural עֲקֵבִים is used for heels and high heels, while עֲקֵבוֹת refers to footprints.

 to follow , tail , track , trace 

 to watch , observe
