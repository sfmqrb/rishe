 Persian
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Borrowed from Arabic جَاه ( jāh ) , from Middle Persian [Book Pahlavi needed] ( gʾs ) , 𐫃𐫀𐫍 ( gʾh /⁠gāh⁠/ , “ place, throne, bed; time ” ) . Doublet of گاه ( gâh ) , see there for more.
