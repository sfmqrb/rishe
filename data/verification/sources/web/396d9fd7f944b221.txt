 Ottoman Turkish
 [ edit ] 

 پیاله 
 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Borrowed from Classical Persian پیاله ( piyāla , “ glass, cup ” ) , from Middle Persian [script needed] ( pygʾl /⁠*paygāl⁠/ , “ cup, goblet ” ) , possibly from Ancient Greek φιάλη ( phiálē , “ cup, bowl ” ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 پیاله • ( piyale ) ( definite accusative پیاله‌یی ( piyaleyi ) , plural پیاله‌لر ( piyaleler ) ) 

 winecup , wineglass , any vessel from which wine is drunk
 Synonym: ناجود ( nacud )

 Persian
 [ edit ] 

 Alternative forms
 [ edit ] 

 پیغاله ( payġâla ) 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Inherited from Middle Persian [script needed] ( pygʾl /⁠*paygāl⁠/ , “ cup, goblet ” ) ; [ 1 ] compare Mazanderani پیلکا ( pilëkā ) , Sogdian [script needed] ( ptγ'δ , “ cup ” ) , Khotanese [script needed] ( phalau , “ flat dish, bowl ” ) . [ 2 ] Further origin is disputed:

 A borrowing from or otherwise cognate form of Avestan 𐬞𐬀𐬙𐬌𐬔𐬁𐬭𐬀 ( patigāra , “ a cup for drinking ” ) , from Proto-Iranian *pati- + a reflex of Proto-Indo-European *gʷerh₃- ( “ to swallow, devour ” ) . [ 3 ] 

 A borrowing relationship with Ancient Greek φιάλη ( phiálē , “ cup, bowl ” ) has been suggested, [ 2 ] though the Greek is itself thought to be a loanword, making the ultimate direction unclear.

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 پیاله • ( piyāla / piyâle ) ( plural پیاله‌ها ( piyāla-hā / piyâle-hâ ) , Tajik spelling пиёла ) 

 a glass cup or chalice 

 beaker
