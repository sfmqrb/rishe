 Ottoman Turkish
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Borrowed from Classical Persian نِیَاز ( niyāz , “ supplication, invocation ” ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 نیاز • ( niyâz ) ( definite accusative نیازی ( niyâzı ) , plural نیازلر ( niyâzlar ) ) 

 invocation , supplication , entreaty , the act of calling for the assistance or presence of a superior being 
 Synonym: دعا ( duʼâ ) 

 Derived terms
 [ edit ] 

 نیاز ایتمك ( niyâz etmek , “ to use entreaty ” ) 
 نیازكار ( niyâzkar , “ supplicant ” ) 
 نیازمند ( niyâzmend , “ supplicant ” ) 
 نیازی ( niyâzi , “ pertaining to entreaty ” ) 

 Descendants
 [ edit ] 

 Turkish: niyaz
