 Noun

 port , harbour

 Etymology 2
 [ edit ] 

 Contracted from Old Hindi बांदर ( bāṃdara ) , from Sauraseni Prakrit 𑀯𑀸𑀡𑀭 ( vāṇara ) , from Sanskrit वानर ( vānara ) . 
The -d- is excrescent between -n- and -r- , for which compare پَنْدَرَہ ( pandara ) .

 [ edit ]

 Rhymes: -əɾ
