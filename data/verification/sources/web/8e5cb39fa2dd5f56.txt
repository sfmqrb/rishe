 This Proto-Indo-European entry contains reconstructed terms and roots . As such, the term(s) in this entry are not directly attested , but are hypothesized to have existed based on comparative evidence.

 Proto-Indo-European
 [ edit ] 

 Root
 [ edit ] 

 *wert- ( perfective ) [ 1 ] [ 2 ] [ 3 ] [ 4 ] 

 ( intransitive ) to turn , to rotate , spin 

 Reconstruction notes
 [ edit ] 

 The reconstruction of an athematic root present *wért-ti ~ *wr̥t-énti [ 5 ] is based on misinterpreting Sanskrit passive aorist वर्ति ( varti ) as a present verb वर्त्ति ( vartti ) [ 6 ] (written identically in many manuscripts). [ 7 ]
