 This Proto-Indo-Iranian entry contains reconstructed terms and roots . As such, the term(s) in this entry are not directly attested , but are hypothesized to have existed based on comparative evidence.

 Proto-Indo-Iranian
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Unknown; possibly borrowed from the BMAC substrate , compare the lack of similarity with Western Indo-European *peysk- ( “ fish ” ) and Eastern Indo-European *dʰǵʰu- ( “ fish ” ) . [ 1 ] [ 2 ] Alternative theories link the term to Proto-Germanic *matiz ( “ food ” ) [ 3 ] , ultimately from Proto-Indo-European *meh₂d- ( “ to drip, ooze; grease, fat ” ) .
