 Pronunciation

 Verb
 [ edit ] 

 خَزَنَ • ( ḵazana ) I ( non-past يَخْزُنُ ( yaḵzunu ) , verbal noun خَزْن ( ḵazn ) ) 

 to store , to stock , to lay up , to hoard , to amass , to accumulate 

 to contain , to conceal (oil, minerals etc) ( of discovery sites ) 

 to keep secret 

 to put in safekeeping
