 Etymology 1
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian [script needed] ( bwl /⁠bōr⁠/ , “ reddish-brown, bay [horse] ” ) , from Proto-Iranian *buHráh , from Proto-Indo-Iranian *bʰruHrás , ultimately from Proto-Indo-European *bʰerH- ( “ brown ” ) . 

 Cognate with Ossetian бур ( bur , “ yellow ” ) , Northern Kurdish bor ( “ grey ” ) , Shughni vůr ( “ brown, grey ” ) , and the Old Armenian loanword բորենի ( boreni , “ hyena ” ) .
