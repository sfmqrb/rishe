 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian [script needed] ( gwšk' /⁠gōšag⁠/ , “ corner ” ) , which appears to be a suffixed formation from the word found now as گوش ( gōš , “ ear ” ) , in the sense of a lappet or protruding end of something. [ 1 ]
