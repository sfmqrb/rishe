 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From ealier آوام ( āwām  / âvâm ) , inherited from Middle Persian [Book Pahlavi needed] ( ʾpʾm /⁠abām⁠/ , “ loan, debt ” ) . Compare Parthian [script needed] ( ʾbʾmyh ) . [ 1 ]
