 Verb
 [ edit ] 

 هَاجَرَ • ( hājara ) III ( non-past يُهَاجِرُ ( yuhājiru ) , verbal noun مُهَاجَرَة ( muhājara ) or هِجَار ( hijār ) ) 

 to migrate 

 ( Classical ) to separate from one's own, emigrate from one's tribe

 ( Classical ) to abandon the nomadic life of the desert to live in the city

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 هَاجِر • ( hājir )  m 

 something excellent in its class; one who walks feebly, as though weak or heavily burdened

 one who rambles, who talks nonsense

 one who is delirious

 [ edit ] 

 From Hebrew הגר ( hāgār ) .

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 IPA ( key ) : /haː.d͡ʒar/ 

 Proper noun
 [ edit ] 

 هَاجَر • ( hājar )  f 

 Hagar , mother of Ishmael by Abraham 

 a female given name , Hajar , equivalent to English Hagar or Hager
