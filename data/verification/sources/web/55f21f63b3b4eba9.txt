 Etymology 1
 [ edit ] 

 Inherited from Proto-Indo-European *kʷey- ( “ pile, stow, to gather ” ) . Cognates include Northern Kurdish çandin ( “ to plant, cultivate ” ) , çinîn ( “ to reap ” ) , nijinandin / nijandin ( “ to pile ” ) , Sanskrit चिनोति ( cinóti , “ he gathers, collects ” ) , Old Church Slavonic чинити ( činiti , “ commit, perform ” ) and Ancient Greek ποιέω ( poiéō , “ make, create ” ) .

 چیدن • ( čidan ) ( present stem چین ( čin ) ) 

 to gather , to arrange , to lay out 
 صندلی‌ها را مثل سینما چیدند . sandali-hâ râ mesl-e sinemâ čidand . They arranged the chairs like in a movie theater.
