 كتن ( keten ) 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Borrowed from Arabic كَتَّان ( kattān , “ flax ” ) , from Classical Syriac ܟܶܬܳܢܳܐ ( kettānā , “ flax; linen ” ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 كتان • ( ketân or keten ) ( definite accusative كتانی ( ketânı, keteni ) , plural كتانلر ( ketânlar, ketenler ) ) 

 flax , a plant of the species Linum usitatissimum , cultivated as a food and fiber crop
