 This Proto-Indo-European entry contains reconstructed terms and roots . As such, the term(s) in this entry are not directly attested , but are hypothesized to have existed based on comparative evidence.

 Proto-Indo-European
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology 1
 [ edit ] 

 Unknown.

 Root
 [ edit ] 

 *tews- [ a 1 ] [ a 2 ] 

 to be empty ; to drain

 Etymology 2
 [ edit ] 

 Mallory suggests that the term may be an extension of a root *tew- ( “ to favor ” ) , which he also sees in Latin tueor . [ b 1 ] De Vaan, however, connects the Latin term with the root *tewh₂- . [ 1 ] 

 Root
 [ edit ] 

 *tews- [ b 2 ] [ b 3 ] 

 to be calm , content , happy

 [ edit ]
