 This Proto-Indo-European entry contains reconstructed terms and roots . As such, the term(s) in this entry are not directly attested , but are hypothesized to have existed based on comparative evidence.

 Proto-Indo-European
 [ edit ] 

 Adverb
 [ edit ] 

 *h₁én [ 1 ] [ 2 ] [ 3 ] 

 in 

 Reconstruction notes
 [ edit ] 

 Traditionally reconstructed with leading *h₁, but several Greek and Sanskrit compounds, if the analysis is correct, rule this out, for example Ancient Greek ἀκαρός ( akarós ) , a variant of ἔγκαρος ( énkaros , “ brain ” ) , [ 4 ] or Sanskrit हेमन् ( héman , “ in winter ” ) < *ǵʰeym én . [ 5 ] [ 6 ] [ 7 ]
