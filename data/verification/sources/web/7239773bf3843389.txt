 Ottoman Turkish
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Borrowed from Persian مغز ( maġz ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 مغز • ( mağz ) 

 brain , the organ which controls the central nervous system 
 Synonyms: بینی ( beyni ) , دماغ ( demağ ) , مخ ( muh ) 

 kernel , marrow , pith , core , essence of a thing 
 Synonyms: اوز ( öz ) , لب ( lubb ) , مخ ( muh ) 

 ( by extension ) mind , sense , understanding , intellect

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian [Book Pahlavi needed] ( mzg /⁠mazg⁠/ , “ brain, marrow ” ) , from Proto-Iranian *mazgáh , from Proto-Indo-Iranian *mazgʰás , from Proto-Indo-European *mosgʰos . Cognate of Russian мозг ( mozg ) , Serbo-Croatian мозак ( mozak ) and English marrow .
