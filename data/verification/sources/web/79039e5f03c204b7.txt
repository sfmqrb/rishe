 Old Persian
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Proto-Iranian *Áryah , from Proto-Indo-Iranian *Áryas . Cognate with Avestan 𐬀𐬌𐬭𐬌𐬌𐬋 ( a i riiō ) , Sanskrit आर्य ( ārya ) .

 Adjective
 [ edit ] 

 𐎠𐎼𐎡𐎹 ( a-r-i-y / Aryaʰ /) 

 Iranian
