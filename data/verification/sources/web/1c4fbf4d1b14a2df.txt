 Persian
 [ edit ] 

 Alternative forms
 [ edit ] 

 ستاییدن , ستائیدن 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian stwtn' ( /⁠stūdan⁠/ ) , stʾdytn' ( /⁠stāyīdan⁠/ , “ to praise ” ) , from Proto-Iranian *stā́wti ( “ to praise ” ) , from Proto-Indo-Iranian *stā́wti ( “ to praise, laud ” ) . Cognate with Sanskrit स्तौति ( stauti , “ to praise, extol ” ) , Old Punjabi ਉਸਤਤਿ ( usatati ) , Ancient Greek στεῦται ( steûtai , “ to promise or threaten that one will ” ) , Hittite [script needed] ( istuwa- , “ to become public ” ) .

 Iranian Persian

 Tajik

 ستودن • ( setudan ) ( present stem ستای ( setây ) ) 

 to praise , to commend 

 to extol , to eulogize 

 to glorify 

 to adore 

 to worship
