 Etymology 1
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian [script needed] ( twmbk' /⁠tumbag⁠/ , “ drum ” ) , according to Watkins, of imitative origin. [ 1 ] Akin to Old Armenian թմբուկ ( tʻmbuk ) , and plausibly also Arabic دَرْبُوكَة ( darbūka ) and the Aramaic forms listed there.
