 This Proto-Indo-European entry contains reconstructed terms and roots . As such, the term(s) in this entry are not directly attested , but are hypothesized to have existed based on comparative evidence.

 Proto-Indo-European
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 The term has been interpreted as the dual of a stem *(H)oḱto- ( “ four fingers ” ) . This erstwhile singular may appear in Avestan 𐬀𐬱𐬙𐬌- ( ašti- , “ breadth of four fingers ” ) and was perhaps borrowed into Proto-Kartvelian as *otxo- ( “ four ” ) . Sihler, however, argues that—in Sanskrit—the dual endings -āu usually only occur about one-seventh as frequently as the alternative ending -ā . However, in the case of this numeral, अ॒ष्ट ( aṣṭá ) appears around a third as often as the form अ॒ष्टौ ( aṣṭáu ) . [ 1 ]

 Reconstruction notes
 [ edit ] 

 Schrijver suggests a reconstruction with initial *h₃e- is perhaps preferable as the other numerals from *tréyes ( “ three ” ) to *déḱm̥ ( “ ten ” ) showcases the e-grade. However, also according to the Schrijver, the lack of initial *h- in the Armenian descendants suggests a pre-form beginning with *Hoḱ- or *Hḱ- . [ 5 ] Martirosyan suggests that the vocalism of the Armenian term could derive from a zero-grade sequence *h₃ḱ- , though it may also have emerged due the influence of an ordinal number. [ 6 ]
