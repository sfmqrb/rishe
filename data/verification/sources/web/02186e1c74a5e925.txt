 English giraffe 

 From Middle French giraffe , from Italian giraffa , from Arabic زُرَافَة ( zurāfa ) , from Classical Syriac ܙܪܝܦܐ ( zārīp̄ā ) , from Classical Persian زُرْنَاپَا ( zurnāpā , literally “ flute-leg ” ) . Displaced camelopard .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 giraffe ( plural giraffes or giraffe ) 

 A ruminant in the genus Giraffa of the African savannah with long legs, a highly elongated neck, yellow fur patterned with dark spots typically in the form of a network , and two or more horn-like projections called ossicones ; the tallest living animal.
 Synonym: camelopard 
 2019 June 6, “A gaggle, a confusion and a conspiracy - bizarre animal collective group names”, in BBC ‎ [1] , archived from the original on 27 August 2026 : A group of giraffes is called a tower. 

 A giraffe unicycle .

 ( Cockney rhyming slang ) A laugh .
 Synonyms: bubble bath , bubble 
 Are you having a giraffe ?! 

 ( colloquial ) A very tall individual .
 ( especially, derogatory ) A very tall woman .
