 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Borrowed from French véhicule , from Latin vehiculum ( “ a carriage, conveyance ” ) , from vehō, vehere ( “ to carry ” ) .

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 ( Received Pronunciation , Scotland ) IPA ( key ) : /ˈviː.ɪ.kəl/ , /ˈvɪə̯.kəl/ 

 ( Standard Southern British ) IPA ( key ) : /ˈvɪjɪk(ə)l/ , /ˈvɪjək(ə)l/ , /ˈvɪːk(ə)l/ 

 ( General American ) IPA ( key ) : /ˈvi.ə.kəl/ , /ˈviˌ(h)ɪ.kəl/

 A conveyance : a spacious device for carrying or transporting substances , objects , people or animals .

[ … ] Rock-filled torrents smashed vehicles and homes, burying victims under rubble and sludge. 

 2020 January 1, Adam Hadhazy, “Achieving autonomy”, in Aerospace America ‎ [2] , archived from the original on 22 April 2025 : Both vehicle types will feed that data into artificial intelligence systems that, having been trained through so-called machine learning, will identify, characterize and evaluate external phenomena. 

 A guided or aimed mobile object or apparatus , especially if intended or used as a weapon .
 kinetic kill vehicle 

 2013 June 7, Ed Pilkington , “‘Killer robots’ should be banned in advance, UN told”, in The Guardian Weekly ‎ [3] , volume 188 , number 26 , archived from the original on 13 August 2025 , page 6: In his submission to the UN, [Christof] Heyns points to the experience of drones. Unmanned aerial vehicles were intended initially only for surveillance, and their use for offensive purposes was prohibited, yet once strategists realised their perceived advantages as a means of carrying out targeted killings, all objections were swept out of the way. 

 A medium for expression of talent or views .
 supporting actors in a Steven Spielberg vehicle 

 A liquid content (e.g., oil) which acts as a binding and drying agent in paint.

 ( pharmaceuticals ) The main excipient (such as an oil or gel) that conveys the active ingredient of a drug.
 an ointment with a petrolatum vehicle 

 An entity to achieve an end.
 mission service as a vehicle for development of better-rounded worldviews 

 ( Buddhism ) A mode or method of spiritual practice ; a yana .

 ( Hinduism ) An animal or (rarely) a plant on which a Hindu deity rides or sits.

 To transport in a vehicle, or as if in a vehicle.
