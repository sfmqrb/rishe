 Ottoman Turkish
 [ edit ] 

 باروت 
 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Borrowed from Arabic بَارُود ( bārūd , “ saltpetre; gunpowder ” ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 باروت • ( barut ) ( definite accusative باروتی ( barutu ) , plural باروتلر ( barutlar ) ) 

 gunpowder , an explosive mixture of saltpetre , charcoal and sulphur , formerly used in gunnery
