 Old Persian
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Proto-Indo-Iranian *Hr̥štikás , from *Hr̥štíš ( “ spear ” ) , from Proto-Indo-European *h₁r̥stís (whence 𐎠𐎼𐏁𐎫𐎡𐏁 ( r̥štiš , “ spear ” ) ). Compare Avestan 𐬀𐬭𐬱𐬙𐬌 ( aršti ) , Sanskrit ऋष्टि ( ṛṣṭi ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 𐎠𐎼𐏁𐎫𐎡𐎣 ( a-r-š-t-i-k / r̥štikaʰ /) [ 1 ] 

 spearman 
 DNb, 44 : 𐏐 𐎠𐎿𐎲𐎠𐎼 𐏐 𐎠𐎼𐏁𐎫𐎡𐎣 𐏐 𐎠𐎷𐎡𐎹 𐏐 𐎢𐎺𐎠𐎼𐏁𐎫𐎡𐎣 𐏐 : a-s-b-a-r : a-r-š-t-i-k : a-mi-i-y : u-v-a-r-š-t-i-k : As a spearman , I am a good spearman, on horseback [ … ]
