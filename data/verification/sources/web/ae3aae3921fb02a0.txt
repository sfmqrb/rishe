 Egyptian Arabic
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Persian بس ( bas ) , from Middle Persian ws ( /⁠was⁠/ ) .

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 IPA ( key ) : /bass/ 

 Conjunction
 [ edit ] 

 بس • ( bass ) 

 but 
 1996 [ 1963 ], صلاح جاهين , رباعيات [ Quatrains ]‎ [1] , مكتبة الأسرة , page 56 : وكنت عاوز أقتله .. بس خُفت I wanted to kill him... but I got scared. 

 only , just 
 1994 , يوسف القعيد , لبن العصفور [ Bird's Milk ]‎ [2] , دار الهلال , page 7: دول بس اللي بيروحوا الجنة حتف Only those go to heaven when they die. 

 Interjection
 [ edit ] 

 بس • ( bass ) 

 stop !, enough !
 1923 , بيرم التونسي , السيد ومراته في باريس [ The Gentleman and His Wife in Paris ]‎ [3] , مطبعة الشباب , page 33 : يوه ! بس ياشيخ اتحدت بلسانك بلاش ايدك Oh! Enough , old man, speak with your tongue, not with your hands. 

 References
 [ edit ] 

 Hinds, Martin ; Badawi, El-Said ( 1986 ), “ بس ”, in A Dictionary of Egyptian Arabic ‎ [4] , Beirut: Librairie du Liban , page 74 

 Gulf Arabic
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Persian بس ( bas ) , from Middle Persian ws ( /⁠was⁠/ ) .

 Conjunction
 [ edit ] 

 بس • ( bas ) 

 but , though 
 رِحْتْ اِلْمَطْعَمْ بَسْ كَانْ مْسَكَّر ― I went to the restaurant but it was closed. 

 only , just 
 سِعِر اِلْوَجْبَة بَسْ دِينَار ― The meal costs just one dinar. 

 enough 
 بَسِّكْ كَمْيُوتَر ― Enough computer (time) for you!

 Iraqi Arabic
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Borrowed from Persian بس ( bas ) .

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 IPA ( key ) : /basː/ 

 Particle
 [ edit ] 

 بس ( bass ) 

 only , just 
 بس أريد أعرف شصار. bass arīd aʿraf š-ṣār. I just want to know what happened. 

 except , but 
 ما بقى أحد بالبيت بس آني. mā bqa aḥad bil-bēt bass āni. No one stayed at home except me. 

 Conjunction
 [ edit ] 

 بس ( bass ) 

 but , though 
 رحت للسوگ بس ما لگيت اللي أريده. riḥt lis-sūg bass mā ligēt illi arīda. I went to the market, but I didn't find what I wanted. 

 as soon as , once 
 بس توصل، دزلي رسالة. bass tōṣal, dazz-li risāla. As soon as you arrive, send me a message. 

 Interjection
 [ edit ] 

 بس ( bass ) 

 stop !, enough !
 Synonym: كافي ( kāfi ) 
 بس! لا تحچي بهالموضوع بعد. bass! lā tiḥči b-hal-mawḍūʿ baʿad. Enough! Don't talk about this topic anymore.
