 Etymology

 Adjective
 [ edit ] 

 پیروز • ( pīrōz / piruz ) ( Tajik spelling пирӯз ) 

 victorious , triumphant 
 c. 1260s , Jalāl ad-Dīn Mohammad Rūmī , translated by Reynold A. Nicholson , مثنوی معنوی [ Masnavi-ye-Ma'navi ], volume III, verse 4092: با سپاهی همچو استاره‌ی اثیر، انبه و پیروز و صفدر ملک‌گیر [ … ] bâ sepâhi hamčow estâre-ye asir, anboh o piruz o safdar molk-gir [ … ] With an army like the stars of heaven, numerous and victorious , one that pierces the ranks [of the enemy] and takes possession of empire [ … ]

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 پیروز • ( pīrōz / piruz ) ( plural پیروزها ( pīrōz-hā / piruz-hâ ) , or پیروزان ( pīrōzān / piruzân ) , Tajik spelling пирӯз ) 

 victor , winner 

 Proper noun
 [ edit ] 

 پیروز • ( pīrōz  / piruz ) ( Tajik spelling Пирӯз ) 

 a male given name , Piruz , Pirooz , Pirouz , and Peroz , from Middle Persian
