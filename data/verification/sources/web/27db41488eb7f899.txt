 Latin
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology 1
 [ edit ] 

 Unknown, thought to be a loanword, perhaps from Etruscan . [ 1 ] Compare bārō ( “ dunce, lout ” ) .

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 ( Classical Latin ) IPA ( key ) : [ˈbar.dʊs] 

 ( modern Italianate Ecclesiastical ) IPA ( key ) : [ˈbar.dus] 

 Adjective
 [ edit ] 

 bardus ( feminine barda , neuter bardum , comparative bardior ) ; first / second-declension adjective 

 ( rare ) stupid , oafish , dull of apprehension 

 Usage notes
 [ edit ] 

 Neither the superlative ( *bardissimus ) nor the adverbial ( *bardē ) is attested in Classical or Late sources.

 Etymology 2
 [ edit ] 

 Attested since Lucan , from Proto-Celtic *bardos ( “ bard ” ) , from Proto-Indo-European *gʷerH- ( “ to approve, praise ” ) , whence grātus .

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 ( Classical Latin ) IPA ( key ) : [ˈbar.dʊs] 

 ( modern Italianate Ecclesiastical ) IPA ( key ) : [ˈbar.dus] 

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 bardus   m ( genitive bardī ) ; second declension 

 a bard ( a poet and singer among the Gauls )

 Norwegian Bokmål
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Borrowed from Middle Low German pardues , perduuz , pardus , perdus , ultimately likely onomatopoeic 

 Cognate with German bardaus , pardauz , Dutch pardoes , perdoes , Swedish burdus , and Danish bardus .

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 IPA ( key ) : /baˈɖʉːs/ , /ˈbaʁˈdʉːs/ 

 This entry needs an audio pronunciation . If you are a native speaker with a microphone, please record this word . The recorded pronunciation will appear here when it's ready.

 Rhymes: -ʉːs 

 Hyphenation: bar‧dus 

 Adjective
 [ edit ] 

 bardus ( neuter singular bardust , definite singular and plural barduse , comparative mer bardus , superlative mest bardus ) 

 sudden , unexpected ; headlong ( rash, blunt, without deliberation ) 
 Synonyms: brå , uventet , plutselig 
 han stilte mange barduse spørsmål ― he asked a lot of blunt questions 
 1893 , Jonas Lie , Niobe , page 252 : den … unge piges krasse og barduse forestillinger the … young girl's harsh and blunt ideas 

 1906 , Hans E. Kinck , Livsaanderne , page 173 : han var … vant til dette barduse væsen hos sin søn he was ... used to this sudden behavior in his son 

 2005 , Jan Kjærstad , Kongen av Europa : han ba om unnskyldning for den barduse henvendelsen he apologized for the blunt inquiry 

 Adverb
 [ edit ] 

 bardus 

 suddenly , unexpectedly ; without warning ( happening quickly and with little or no warning ) 
 Synonyms: brått , hodekulls , plutselig 
 å komme bardus (på noen) ― to suddenly hit you; to dawn on (you) 
 1882 , Alexander L. Kielland , Skipper Worse , page 95 : der [var] begende mørkt … nede paa bryggerne, hvor man gik lige bardus paa hovedet i søen, naar man var fuld eller fremmed it [was] pitch dark ... down on the docks, where you could abruptly fall headfirst into the lake if you were drunk or unfamiliar with the area. 

 1852 , Nicolai Ramm Østgaard , En Fjeldbygd , page 180 : det [hendte] en enkelt gang, at rytteren bardus kom ned igjen paa den anden side it [happened] once, that the rider suddenly came down again on the other side 

 (Can we date this quote?) , Henrik Wergeland , Samlede Skrifter I , page 349 : Ola Høiland, … tog Norges bank ret listig … Herud paa jagt, bardus! Ola Høiland, ... took Norway's bank by surprise ... Here we go hunting, out of the blue! 

 1993 , Knut Faldbakken , Ormens år : en så levende, bardust sjarmerende og intellektuelt uforferdet kvinne such a lively, abruptly charming and intellectually fearless woman 

 2010 , Aksel Selmer , Bedringens vei : Remi setter seg bardust ned i sofaen ved siden av Svanhild Remi plops down on the sofa next to Svanhild 

 2010 , Tor Edvin Dahl , Hodet ved døra : det kom bardus på meg [da hun sa at moren hennes var forelsket i meg] it hit me suddenly [when she said that her mother was in love with me] 

 2011 , Odd Klippenvåg , Ljublju : grovheten hans kom så bardus at jeg ikke fikk fram et ord his rudeness took me by suprise so that I couldn't get a word out. 

 2015 , Lars Saabye Christensen , Magnet , page 681 : vi ville ikke komme bardus på og nærmet oss langsomt og forsiktig we didn't want to come on too strong, so we approached slowly and cautiously
