 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Deverbal noun of فرهیختن ( farhixtan ) . From Middle Persian plʾhng ( /⁠frahang⁠/ , “ education, knowledge; dictionary ” ) , ultimately from Proto-Iranian *θanǰáyati ; see there for further information. Compare Old Armenian հրահանգ ( hrahang ) , an Iranian loanword.

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 فرهنگ • ( farhang ) ( plural فرهنگ‌ها ( farhang-hā / farhang-hâ ) , Tajik spelling фарҳанг ) 

 culture 
 نشان فرهنگ و هنر ― nešân-e farhang o honar ― Order of Culture and Art 
 فرهنگ و تمدن ایران ― farhang o tamaddon-e irân ― the culture and civilization of Iran 

 knowledge , education 
 Synonyms: دانش ( dāniš  / dâneš ) , تربیت ( tarbiyyat  / tarbiyat ) 
 c. 1260s , Jalāl ad-Dīn Mohammad Rūmī , translated by Reynold A. Nicholson , مثنوی معنوی [ Masnavi-ye-Ma'navi ], volume VI, verse 3781: تکیه بر عقل خود و فرهنگ خویش / بودمان تا این بلا آمد به پیش takye bar 'aql-e xod o farhang -e xiš / bud-emân tâ in balâ âmad be piš We relied on our own intelligence and wisdom , so that this tribulation has come to pass. 

 civility , courtesy 
 Synonym: ادب ( adab ) 

 dictionary 
 Synonyms: see Thesaurus: لغت‌نامه
