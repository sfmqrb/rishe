 This Proto-Germanic entry contains reconstructed terms and roots . As such, the term(s) in this entry are not directly attested , but are hypothesized to have existed based on comparative evidence.

 Proto-Germanic
 [ edit ] 

 Alternative forms
 [ edit ] 

 *wunskō   f [ 1 ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 A nominal formation from Proto-Indo-European *wnh₁-sḱé-ti ( “ to desire ” ) , an sḱé -present from *wenh₁- ( “ to wish, love ” ) . [ 2 ] 

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 IPA ( key ) : /ˈwun.skɑz/ 

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 *wunskaz   m [ 1 ]
