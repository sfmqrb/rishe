 Proper noun
 [ edit ] 

 اسحاق • ( ishāq  / eshâġ ) ( Tajik spelling Исҳоқ ) 

 ( Islam ) Ishaq , Isaac 

 a male given name , Eshaq , Ishaq , and Eshagh , from Arabic إِسْحَاق ( ʔisḥāq ) [in turn from Hebrew יצחק ], equivalent to English Isaac
