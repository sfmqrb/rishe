 This Proto-Indo-European entry contains reconstructed terms and roots . As such, the term(s) in this entry are not directly attested , but are hypothesized to have existed based on comparative evidence.

 Proto-Indo-European
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology 1
 [ edit ] 

 Possibly a non-Indo-European loanword. [ 1 ] Perhaps onomatopoeic or sound-symbolic ; compare *gʰabʰ- ( “ to seize ” ) [ 2 ] as so-called "chiming roots". [ 3 ] 

 Root
 [ edit ] 

 *kap- [ 4 ] [ 5 ] [ 3 ] 

 to seize , to grab 
 Synonyms: *gʰed- , *gʰeh₁bʰ- , *gʰreb⁽ʰ⁾(h₂)- , *h₁ep- , *h₁rep- , *teh₂g- , *tek- 

 ( stative ) to hold 
 Synonyms: *kagʰ- , *gʰed- , *twerH-

 [ edit ] 

 *keh₂p- [ 6 ] [ 7 ] 

 *kh₂ep- [ 8 ]

 Etymology 2
 [ edit ] 

 Uncertain; may be the same as the verbal root in *kap- ( etymology 1 ) above, particularly if it developed figuratively from the sense “cup, bowl” as retained in Proto-Celtic *kaɸukos and perhaps Sanskrit कपाल ( kapā́la ) . [ 1 ] Otherwise, sound-symbolic or borrowed from a substrate , as evidenced by the a-grade. [ 2 ] [ 3 ]

 head
