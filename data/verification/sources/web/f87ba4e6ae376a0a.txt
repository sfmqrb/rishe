 Marathi
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Borrowed from Sanskrit नाथ ( nātha ) . First attested as Old Marathi 𑘡𑘰𑘞 ( nātha ) .

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 IPA ( key ) : /nat̪ʰ/ 

 Rhymes: -at̪ʰ 

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 नाथ • ( nāth )  m [ 1 ] [ 2 ] [ 3 ] 

 master , lord , protector 
 Synonyms: see Thesaurus: मालक 

 ( literary , archaic ) husband 
 Synonyms: see Thesaurus: पती 

 ( chiefly dramatic ) a respectful term of address for one's husband 
 Synonym: स्वामी ( svāmī )

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Proto-Indo-European *h₃neh₂- ( “ to help, support ” ) . Cognate with Ancient Greek ὀνί̆νημῐ ( oní̆nēmĭ , “ to help; to please ” ) . The Sanskrit root is नाथ् ( nāth , “ to seek aid, ask ” ) .

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 ( Vedic ) IPA ( key ) : /nɑː.tʰɐ́/ 

 ( Classical Sanskrit ) IPA ( key ) : /n̪ɑː.t̪ʰɐ/ 

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 ना॒थ • ( nāthá ) stem ,  n 

 refuge , help , resort 
 c. 1200 BCE – 1000 BCE , Atharvaveda 9.2.7 : अध्यक्षो वाजी मम काम उग्रः कृणोतु मह्यम् असपत्नम् एव ।
विश्वे देवा मम नाथं भवन्तु सर्वे देवा हवम् आ यन्तु म इमम् ॥ adhyakṣo vājī mama kāma ugraḥ kṛṇotu mahyam asapatnam eva.
viśve devā mama nāthaṃ bhavantu sarve devā havam ā yantu ma imam. May Kāma, the mighty one, my potent warder, make me unrivalled.
May all the Gods be my protection , all Gods come closer to this invocation of mine.

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 ना॒थ • ( nāthá ) stem ,  m 

 ( often at the end of compounds, esp. in names of gods and men ) protector , patron , possessor , owner , ward , guardian , lord 
 c. 500 BCE – 100 BCE , Rāmāyaṇa Bālakāṇḍa.76.19: न चेयं मम काकुत्स्थ व्रीडा भवितुम् अर्हति ।
त्वया त्रैलोक्य नाथेन यद् अहं विमुखीकृत: ॥ na ceyaṃ mama kākutstha vrīḍā bhavitum arhati.
tvayā trailokya nāthena yad ahaṃ vimukhīkṛta:. I have nothing to be ashamed of, O Descendant of Kakustha, the lord of the three worlds.
Since I have been defeated by you. 

 husband (especially in vocative)

 a rope passed through the nose of a draft ox 

 name of several authors
