 Etymology 1
 [ edit ] 

 Inherited from Middle Persian [Book Pahlavi needed] ( lwd ) , 𐫡𐫇𐫏 ( rwy /⁠rōy⁠/ , “ copper, brass ” ) , Northern Luri ڕوویی ( rruyi ) , from Proto-Indo-Iranian *Hrawdáh ( “ copper, brass ” ) , from Proto-Indo-Iranian *Hrawdʰás . [ 1 ]

 Etymology 2
 [ edit ] 

 Inherited from Middle Persian [Book Pahlavi needed] ( lwd ) , 𐫡𐫇𐫏 ( rwy /⁠rōy⁠/ , “ face ” ) , Northern Luri ڕی ( rri ) , from Proto-Iranian *rauda- ( “ appearance ” ) (with semantic shift "growth, development" > "appearance"), from Proto-Iranian *Hraud- ( “ to grow (bigger) ” ) , from Proto-Indo-European *h₁lewdʰ- ( “ to grow ” ) .
