 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian pstk' ( /⁠pistag⁠/ , “ pistachio nut ” ) , from Old Persian *pistaka , attested as Elamite [script needed] ( pi-iš-tuk-ka₄ /⁠pistaka⁠/ , “ pistachio nut ” ) . Related to Persian بسته ( busta  / boste , “ nut ” ) , cognate with Sanskrit बुस्त ( busta , “ husk or shell of fruit ” ) .
