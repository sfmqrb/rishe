 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 farang ( plural farangs ) 

 ( Thailand ) A foreigner in Thailand who is of Western ancestry ; one who comes to Thailand from a Western country .
 2017 March 30, " Farang jumps to his death at Suvarnabhumi Airport ", Coconuts Bangkok , Coconuts Media Limited:
 Farang jumps to his death at Suvarnabhumi Airport: A foreign man is believed to have committed suicide when he fell from the fourth floor of Suvarnabhumi Airport this morning. 

 2017 April 20, " Crazy farang tied up by Pattaya locals and police after he jumped from roof to roof ", Coconuts Bangkok , Coconuts Media Limited:
 Crazy farang tied up by Pattaya locals and police after he jumped from roof to roof: A foreign man was tied up by locals and police Wednesday morning... At midnight, locals alerted the police after the farang went on a rampage and destroyed other people's property... Witnesses said they had not seen the man before. The farang continued to let out curses as he was moved onto the police's truck... 

 2017 May 9, " Farang couple has sex on Samui beach for audience of stray dogs ", Coconuts Bangkok , Coconuts Media Limited:
 Farang couple has sex on Samui beach for audience of stray dogs: A local on Koh Samui shared a video of a couple he called " farang scums" having sex on the beach for an audience of confused stray dogs.

 Norwegian Nynorsk

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Most likely from fara +‎ -ang . The suffix -ang is a rare, unproductive suffix found in some other terms like berang ( “ gait ” ) and årang ( “ cohort ” ) . Probably related to Icelandic farangur ( “ luggage ” ) 

 First attested in 1758 by Søren Fermann in Tinnmål dialect . Later also attested by Søren Richart Hagerup (probably in Surnadal dialect, ca. 1770) and Jacob Nicolai Wilse ( Spydeberg dialect, 1780). Wilse gives definition " a sudden sickness " (probably same as bråmein ).

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 farang   m ( definite singular farangen , indefinite plural farangar , definite plural farangane ) 

 an epidemy 
 Synonyms: epidemi , sott

 ( colloquial ) clipping of magefarang ( “ norovirus ” ) 
 Synonyms: norovirus , omgangssjuke
