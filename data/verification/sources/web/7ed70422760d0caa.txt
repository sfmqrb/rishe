 [ edit ] 

 From Persian انبار ( anbâr ) .

 Alternative forms
 [ edit ] 

 أَنْبار ( ʔanbār ) 

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 عَنْبَر • ( ʕanbar )  m ( plural عَنَابِر ( ʕanābir ) ) 

 storeroom , stowage , storehouse , magazine 
 Synonym: مَخْزَن ( maḵzan ) 

 ward , section (e.g. of a hospital)
 2021 April 1, Hani Mohammed, “ بحثاً عن زنزانة لـ” الترانس ”:حكايات عن “جحيم” العبور الجنسي في مصر [ Searching for a Cell for Trans People : Stories of the "Hell" of Gender Transition in Egypt ]”, in Daraj Media ‎ [2] , archived from the original on 16 August 2026 : انكسر ذراع ملك بعد حادث، وانتقلت إلى المستشفى، الوجه والشعر والجسد لأنثى، لكن المستشفى حجز ملك في عنبر الرجال. فوجئ مدير المستشفى، وسأل: لماذا تحجزونها هنا؟ فعاجله معاونوه بالإجابة: تحمل بطاقة “ذكر”. Malak's arm was broken after the incident and she was transported to the hospital. Her face, hair, and body all read female, yet the hospital addmited her to the men's ward . The hospital director was surprised, and asked "why have you put her here?" to which the nurses quickly replied: her ID card says "male."

 Ottoman Turkish
 [ edit ] 

 عنبر 
 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Borrowed from Arabic عَنْبَر ( ʕanbar , “ ambergris ” ) , itself from Middle Persian ʾmbl ( ambar , “ ambergris ” ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 عنبر • ( ʼanber ) ( definite accusative عنبری ( ʼanberi ) , plural عنبرلر ( ʼanberler ) ) 

 ambergris , a solid , waxy , flammable substance produced in the intestines of the sperm whale 

 ( by extension ) sperm whale , the largest of the toothed whales ( Physeter macrocephalus )
 Synonyms: عنبر بالغی ( ʼanber balığı ) , قادرغه بالغی ( kadırga balığı ) , قاشالو ( kaşalo ) 

 ( in general ) perfume , fragrance , anything delicious , fragrant , or exquisite to the sense of smell
