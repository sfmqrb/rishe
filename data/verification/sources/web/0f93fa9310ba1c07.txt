 Alternative forms

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 ( US ) IPA ( key ) : /pəˈd͡ʒæmə/ , /pəˈd͡ʒɑːmə/ 

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 pajama 

 ( US ) Attributive form of pajamas .
 We were greeted by an old, pajama -clad man. 

 Let's have a pajama party. 

 pajama top and bottoms 

 ( rare ) A pair of pajamas .
 2004 December 1, Kenya National Assembly Official Record (Hansard) : (Mr. Kibwana): On a point of order, Mr. Deputy Speaker, Sir. Is it in order for Mr. Omingo, who seems to relish in calling me names, to suggest that the national dress of Kenya is a pajama ?

 Makasar
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From pa- +‎ jama .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 pajama ( Lontara spelling ᨄᨍᨆ or 𑻣𑻪𑻥 , definite pajamaya ) 

 worker ; craftsman ; someone who works on something

 Derived terms
 [ edit ] 

 compounds

 pajama baraʼ

 Verb
 [ edit ] 

 pajama ( Lontara spelling ᨄᨍᨆ or 𑻣𑻪𑻥 ) 

 ( transitive , causative ) to make someone work ; to put to work; to employ
