 بَتَّ • ( batta ) I ( non-past يَبُتُّ ( yabuttu ) or يَبِتُّ ( yabittu ) , verbal noun بَتّ ( batt ) ) 

 to cut off, to sever 
 Synonyms: قَطَعَ ( qaṭaʕa ) , بَتَرَ ( batara ) , جَزَمَ ( jazama ) 

 to complete , to finish , to achieve , to accomplish 

 to fix , to settle , to determine , to decide 

 to adjudge , to adjudicate

 Persian
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology 1
 [ edit ] 

 Inherited from Middle Persian [Book Pahlavi needed] ( bwt' /⁠but⁠/ , “ Buddha, idol ” ) , ultimately from Sanskrit बुद्ध ( buddha ) . In the earliest New Persian sources of the ninth century, the word already has the extended meaning of “beautiful person; the beloved” (whom one worships instead of God). Doublet of بد ( bud  / bod , “ Buddha ” ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 بت • ( but / bot ) ( plural بت‌ها ( but-hā / bot-hâ ) , or بتان ( butān / botân ) , Tajik spelling бут ) 

 idol 
 Synonym: صنم ( sanam ) 
 c. 1011 , Abu'l-Qāsim Firdawsī , “The reign of Gushtāsp”, in شاهنامه [ Book of Kings ]‎ [4] : چو آگه شدند از نکو دین اوی
گرفتند آن راه و آیین اوی
 بتان از سر کوه میسوختند
بجای بت آذر برافروختند ču āgah šudand az nēkū dīn-i ōy
giriftand ān rāh u āyīn-i ōy
 butān az sar-i kōh mē-sōxtand
ba jāy-i but āḏar bar-afrōxtand When they learned of the good religion of his,
They took up the ways and customs of his;
They burned the idols from the mountaintops,
And in the idol' s place, they lit the sacred flame. (Classical Persian romanization) 

 ( Classical Persian ) beauty ; beautiful person ; the beloved 
 Synonyms: صنم ( sanam ) , جانان ( jānān  / jânân ) , دلبر ( dilbar  / delbar ) , دلستان ( dilsitān  / delsetân ) 
 c. 950 , Abū‘l-Hasan Ali Munjīk, گنج سخن , 4th edition, volume 1, Tehran: Ibn-i Sīnā, published 1961 , page 50 : ما را جگر بتیر فراق تو خسته گشت
ای صبر بر فراق بتان نیک جوشنی mā rā jigar ba tīr-i firāq-i tu xasta gašt
ay sabr bar firāq-i butān nēk jawšan-ī My heart [lit. liver] has been wounded by the arrow of separation from you;
O patience! You are a fine breastplate against separation from beauties . (Classical Persian transliteration)
