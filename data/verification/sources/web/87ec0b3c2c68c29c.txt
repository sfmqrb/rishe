 Old Persian
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Proto-Iranian *dáhyuš , from Proto-Indo-Iranian *dásyuš , from Proto-Indo-European *des- ( “ enemy, foreigner ” ) . [ 1 ] 

 Cognate with Old Avestan 𐬛𐬀𐬒𐬌𐬌𐬎 ( dax́iiu , “ country ” ) , Younger Avestan 𐬛𐬀𐬣𐬵𐬎 ( daŋ́hu ) , Sanskrit दस्यु ( dásyu , “ stranger , foreigner ” ) . [ 2 ] 

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 𐎭𐏃𐎹𐎠𐎢 ( d-h-y-a-u / dahyau /)  f 

 land , country 
 c. 640 BCE – 600 BCE , Ariaramnes , AmH 5: 𐎡𐎹𐎶 𐏐 𐎭𐏃𐎹𐎠𐎢𐏁 𐏐 𐎱𐎠𐎼𐎿𐎠 𐏐 𐎫𐎹 𐏐 𐎠𐎭𐎶 𐏐 𐎭𐎠𐎼𐎹𐎠𐎷𐎡𐎹 i-y-m : d-h-y-a-u-š : p-a-r-s-a : t-y : a-d-m : d-a-r-y-a-mi-i-y This is the Persian land which I hold.
