 Persian
 [ edit ] 

 Alternative forms
 [ edit ] 

 لغت ( laġat ) ( dialectal ) 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Inherited from Proto-Indo-Iranian [Term?] , a suffixed form of Proto-Indo-European *lek- ( “ to bend, leg ” ) . [ 1 ] Cognate with Ancient Greek λάξ ( láx , “ with the leg/heel ” ) , Ancient Greek λᾰκτῐ́ζω ( lăktĭ́zō , “ to kick ” ) ; Sanskrit लकुट ( lakuṭa , “ stick, club ” ) ; Sauraseni Prakrit 𑀮𑀢𑁆𑀢𑀸 ( lattā , “ kick ” ) , the latter probably from an unattested Sanskrit word for "to kick". [ 2 ]

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 لگد • ( lagad ) 

 kick 
 1166 , Niẓāmī of Ganja , chapter 34, in مخزن‌الاسرار [ Treasury of Mysteries ]‎ [2] : آتش در خرمن خود می‌زنی
دولت خود را به لگد می‌زنی ātaš dar xarman-i xwad mē-zanī
dawlat-i xwad rā ba lagad mē-zanī You are setting fire to your own harvest,
You are kick ing away your own fortune. (Classical transliteration)
