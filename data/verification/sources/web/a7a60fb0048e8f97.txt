 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian 𐭪𐭥𐭯 ( kōf , “ hill, mountain ” ) , from Old Persian 𐎣𐎢𐎳 ( kaufa , “ mountain ” ) , [ 1 ] from Proto-Iranian *káwfš , from Proto-Indo-European *kewHp- ( “ heap; to heap ” ) .
