 Ottoman Turkish
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Borrowed from French budget , itself from English budget .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 بودجه • ( büdce ) 

 budget , the amount of money or resources earmarked for a particular activity 

 Descendants
 [ edit ] 

 Turkish: bütçe 

 → Azerbaijani: büdcə 

 Further reading
 [ edit ]
