 Persian زن 

 Inherited from Middle Persian [script needed] ( zan ) , from Proto-Iranian *ǰánHs , from Proto-Indo-Iranian *ǰánHs , from Proto-Indo-European *gʷénh₂s , from *gʷḗn .

 Cognates include English queen , Northern Kurdish jin , Greek γυναίκα ( gynaíka ) , Russian жена ( žena ) , etc.

 زن • ( zan ) ( plural زنان ( zanān / zanân ) , or زن‌ها ( zan-hā / zan-hâ ) , Tajik spelling зан ) 

 woman 
 Synonym: ضعیفه ( za'īfa  / za'ife ) 
 Antonym: مرد ( mard ) 
 زن، زندگی، آزادی ― zan , zendegi, âzâdi ― Woman , Life, Freedom 

 wife 
 برادر زن م یک هفته با ما می‌مونه. barâdar-e zan am yek hafte bâ mâ mi-mune. My wife' s brother is staying with us for a week. 

 female
