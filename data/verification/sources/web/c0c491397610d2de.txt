 Verb
 [ edit ] 

 أَرْغَنَ • ( ʔarḡana ) IV ( non-past يُرْغِنُ ( yurḡinu ) , verbal noun إِرْغَان ( ʔirḡān ) ) 

 to comply with

 to ease , to facilitate
