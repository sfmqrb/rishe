 Sanskrit तुच्छ्य 

 From Proto-Indo-European *tus-sḱ-yós , from *tews- ( “ to be empty ” ) . Cognate with Lithuanian tùščias ( “ empty ” ) , Middle Persian [script needed] ( twhyg /⁠tuhīg⁠/ ) (whence Persian تهی ( tohi ) ).

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 ( Vedic ) IPA ( key ) : /tut.t͡ɕʰjɐ́/ , [tut̚.t͡ɕʰjɐ́] 

 ( Classical Sanskrit ) IPA ( key ) : /t̪ut̪.t͡ɕʰjɐ/ , [t̪ut̪̚.t͡ɕʰjɐ] 

 Adjective
 [ edit ] 

 तु॒च्छ्य • ( tucchyá ) stem 

 empty , vain 
 c. 1500 BCE – 1000 BCE , Ṛgveda 5.42.10 : य ओहते रक्षसो देववीतावचक्रेभिस्तं मरुतो नि यात ।
यो वः शमीं शशमानस्य निन्दा त्तुच्छ्या न्कामान्करते सिष्विदानः ॥ ya ohate rakṣaso devavītāvacakrebhistaṃ maruto ni yāta.
yo vaḥ śamīṃ śaśamānasya nindā ttucchyā nkāmānkarate siṣvidānaḥ. With wheelless chariots drive down him, O Maruts, who at the feasts of Gods regards the demons.
May he, though bathed in sweat, form empty wishes, who blames his sacred rite who toils to serve you.
