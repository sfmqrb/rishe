 Alternative forms

 Zach , Zakariah 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Hebrew זְכַרְיָה ( Zəḵaryā , “ Yahweh has remembered ” ) under influence from Latin Zacharias and Koine Greek Ζαχαρίας ( Zakharías ) . Doublet of Zach , Zachary , Zacharias , Zechariah , Zakariya , and Jakariya .

 Proper noun
 [ edit ] 

 Zachariah 

 A male given name from Hebrew , alternative form of Zechariah and Zachary .
