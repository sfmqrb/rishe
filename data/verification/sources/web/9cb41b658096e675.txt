 Persian

 Persian Wikipedia has an article on: بانو 

 Wikipedia fa 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Inherited from Middle Persian [Book Pahlavi needed] ( bʾnwk' ) , [Manichaean needed] ( bʾnwg /⁠bānūg⁠/ ) , from Old Persian *bānūka- . [ 1 ] [ 2 ] Akin to Old Armenian բանուկան ( banukan ) , an Iranian borrowing.

 This etymology is missing or incomplete. Please add to it, or discuss it at the Etymology scriptorium . 
Particularly: “Related to Ancient Greek δέσποινα ( déspoina , “ mistress, lady ” ) ? Proto-form might be something like Proto-Iranian *bāt-nūka- , with the "dems" part dropped off.”

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 بانو • ( bānū, bānō / bânu, bânu ) ( plural بانوان ( bānūān, bānōān / bânuân, bânuân ) , or بانوها ( bānū-hā, bānō-hā / bânu-hâ, bânu-hâ ) , Tajik spelling бону , or бонӯ ) 

 lady 
 Synonym: خانم ( xānum  / xânom )
