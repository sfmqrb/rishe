 Alternative forms

 chæta 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Ancient Greek χαίτη ( khaítē , “ crest, mane, flowing hair, foliage ” ) .

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 ( Received Pronunciation ) IPA ( key ) : /ˈkiːtə/ 

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 chaeta ( plural chaetae ) 

 A chitinous bristle of an annelid worm .

 Such a bristle on an arthropod .
