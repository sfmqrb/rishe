 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian [Book Pahlavi needed] ( ʾsyʾp̄ /⁠āsyāb⁠/ ) . By surface analysis , آس ( ās  / âs , “ millstone ” ) +‎ ـیـ ( -y- , linking element ) +‎ آب ( āb  / âb , “ water ” ) . Compare آسباد ( âsbâd , “ windmill ” ) .
