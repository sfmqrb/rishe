 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 كَعْب • ( kaʕb )  m ( plural كِعَاب ( kiʕāb ) or كُعُوب ( kuʕūb ) ) 

 knot , knob , node 

 joint , articulation 

 ( anatomy ) ankle , anklebone 
 609–632 CE , Qur'an , 5:6 : وَٱمْسَحُوا بِرُؤُوسِكُمْ وَأَرْجُلَكُمْ إِلَى ٱلْكَعْبَيْنِ wamsaḥū biruʔūsikum waʔarjulakum ʔilā l-kaʕbayni (please add an English translation of this quotation) 

 ( anatomy , proscribed ) heel 

 high heels 

 stub , counterfoil , coupon 

 ferrule 

 die , cube 

 fame , glory , honor , rank
