 Middle Persian
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From unattested Old Persian *pistaka , hence Elamite [script needed] ( pi-iš-tuk-ka₄ /⁠pistaka⁠/ , “ pistachio nut ” ) , probably from Proto-Iranian *pištakáh ( “ crushed ” ) , from Proto-Indo-Iranian *pištás + *-ikas , from Proto-Indo-European *pis-tó-s , from *peys- ( “ to grind, crush ” ) . [ 1 ] [ 2 ] The name perhaps refers to the ease of crushing pistachios and the culinary value of crushed pistachios.

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 pstk' • ( pistag ) 

 ( Book Pahlavi ) pistachio nut
