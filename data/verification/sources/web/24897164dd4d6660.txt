 Persian گاو 

 From Middle Persian [Book Pahlavi needed] ( TWRA /⁠gāw⁠/ ) , from Old Persian *gāuš, 𐎥𐎢 ( “ g-u ” ) , from Proto-Iranian *gā́wš , from Proto-Indo-Iranian *gā́wš , [ 1 ] from Proto-Indo-European *gʷṓws .

 Cognate to English cow , Urdu گائے ( gāe ) / Hindi गाय ( gāy ) , Avestan 𐬔𐬀𐬊 ( gao ) , Ancient Greek βοῦς ( boûs ) , Latin bōs .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 گاو • ( gāw / gâv ) ( plural گاوها ( gāw-hā / gâv-hâ ) , Tajik spelling гов ) 

 cow , bull , cattle 
 c. 1260 , Rumi , “ 5:3500 ”, in The Masnavi : او اگر دیوانه است و فتنه‌کاو، داروی دیوانه باشد کیر گاو. u agar divâne ast o fetne-kâv, dâru-ye divâne bâšad kir-e gâv . If he is mad and bent on mischief, the cure for a madman is an ox -hide whip. ( Reynold A. Nicholson translation) 

 ( astronomy ) the constellation Taurus 
 1192 , Nizami Ganjavi , “ Layla and Majnun ”, in Khamsa : گاو فلکی چو گاو دریا، گوهر بگلو در از ثریا. gâv -e falaki čo gâv -e daryâ, gowhar be-galu dorr az sorayyâ. the celestial Bull , like the cow of the sea, has on its neck gems, and pearls from the Pleiades.
