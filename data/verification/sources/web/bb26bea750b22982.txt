 Arabic
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology 1
 [ edit ] 

 Root 

 ء م ر ( ʔ m r ) 

 22 terms 

 Cognate to Hebrew אמר ( amár , “ to say ” ) . Compare also Ugaritic 𐎀𐎎𐎗 ( ảmr ) and Akkadian 𒀀𒈠𒀀𒀸 ( amārum , “ to see, to watch ” ) .

 Verb
 [ edit ] 

 أَمَرَ • ( ʔamara ) I ( non-past يَأْمُرُ ( yaʔmuru ) , verbal noun أَمْر ( ʔamr ) ) 

 to order , to command , to bid , to instruct 

 to commission , to charge , to entrust

 م ر ر ( m r r )

 Elative of مُرّ ( murr , “ bitter , severe , sharp , painful ” ) and مَرِير ( marīr , “ firm , strong ” ) .

 Adjective
 [ edit ] 

 أَمَرّ • ( ʔamarr ) ( feminine مُرَّى ( murrā ) ) 

 elative degree of مَرِير ( marīr ) :
 firmer , stronger ; firmest , strongest 

 elative degree of مُرّ ( murr ) :
 bitterer ; bitterest 

 more severe ; most severe 

 sharper ; sharpest 

 more painful ; most painful
