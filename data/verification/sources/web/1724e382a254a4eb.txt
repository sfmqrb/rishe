 Alternative forms
 [ edit ] 

 لگ‌لگ ( lag-lag ) , لقلق ( laqlaq ) 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Probably ultimately from Semitic . Compare Akkadian 𒀀𒊩𒄷 ( laqlaqqu , “ stork ” ) , Arabic لَقْلَق ( laqlaq , “ stork ” ) , Assyrian Neo-Aramaic ܠܲܩܠܵܩܵܐ ( laqlāqā , “ stork ” ) .

 Pronunciation
