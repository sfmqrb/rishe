 Prakrit वेरुलिअ ( vērulia ) bor. 
 Ancient Greek βήρυλλος 

 Borrowed from Middle Indic forms like Pali veḷuriya or Prakrit 𑀯𑁂𑀭𑀼𑀮𑀺𑀅 ( verulia ) , from Dravidian , probably named after a city (modern day Bēlūr ) in southern India in which the mineral was mined. [ 1 ] 

 The variants βηρύλλιον ( bērúllion ) and βηρύλλιος ( bērúllios ) reflect the source veruḷiya more faithfully than βήρυλλος ( bḗrullos ) .
