 Arabic
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology 1
 [ edit ] 

 From Classical Syriac ܡܪܝ ( mār(ī) ) , the first-person singular possessed form of ܡܪܐ ( mārā , “ lord, master ” ) .

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 IPA ( key ) : /maːr/ 

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 مَار • ( mār )  m 

 ( Christianity ) Lord ; Saint ; Mar ( honorific title for a man of religion, most often a saint ) 
 Matthew 7:21:
 لَيْسَ كُلُّ مَنْ يَقُولُ لِي: يَا مَار! يَا مَار! يَدْخُلُ مَلَكُوتَ السَّمَاوَاتِ، بَلْ مَنْ يَعْمَلُ بِإِرَادَةِ أَبِي الَّذِي فِي السَّمَاوَاتِ laysa kullu man yaqūlu lī: yā mār! yā mār! yadḵulu malakūta s-samāwāti, bal man yaʕmalu bi-ʔirādati ʔabī llaḏī fī s-samāwāti Not everyone who says to me, O Lord! O Lord! will enter the kingdom of heaven, but only the one who does the will of my Father who is in heaven.
