 Etymology 2
 [ edit ] 

 Root 

 ه ر ي ( h r y ) 

 1 term 

 Derived from the active participle of هَرَى ( harā ) .

 Adjective
 [ edit ] 

 هَار • ( hār ) 

 active participle of هَرَى ( harā ) 

 reeling, tottering, unsteady
 609–632 CE , Qur'an , 9:109 : أَفَمَنْ أَسَّسَ بُنْيَانَهُ عَلَىٰ تَقْوَىٰ مِنَ اللَّهِ وَرِضْوَانٍ خَيْرٌ أَم مَّنْ أَسَّسَ بُنْيَانَهُ عَلَىٰ شَفَا جُرُفٍ هَارٍ فَانْهَارَ بِهِ فِي نَارِ جَهَنَّمَ ʔafaman ʔassasa bunyānahu ʕalā taqwā mina l-lahi wariḍwānin ḵayrun ʔam man ʔassasa bunyānahu ʕalā šafā jurufin hārin fānhāra bihi fī nāri jahannama (please add an English translation of this quotation)

 Noun
