 This Proto-Indo-European entry contains reconstructed terms and roots . As such, the term(s) in this entry are not directly attested , but are hypothesized to have existed based on comparative evidence.

 Proto-Indo-European
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Perhaps this term emerged from *h₁gr- , the zero-grade of another otherwise unattested root. The original root may have undergone depalatalization in the zero-grade. [ 1 ] 

 Root
 [ edit ] 

 *h₁ger- [ 2 ] [ 3 ] 

 to wake up , awaken 
 Synonyms: *bʰewdʰ- , *weǵ- 
 Antonyms: *ses- , *swep- 

 to keep watch 

 Alternative reconstructions
 [ edit ] 

 *ger- [ 4 ] 

 *h₁ǵer- [ 5 ] [ 6 ] 

 Reconstruction notes
 [ edit ] 

 Structurally, this root is somewhat unusual in that it begins with a laryngeal followed by a plosive, whereas most laryngeal-initial roots began with a laryngeal followed by a resonant. However, it does not necessarily violate any rules of Proto-Indo-European phonotactics. [ 7 ] The root *h₃sleydʰ- may showcase a similar irregularity. [ 8 ]
