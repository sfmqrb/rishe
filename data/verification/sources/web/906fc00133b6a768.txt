 English

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Borrowed from Sanskrit मुक्ति ( mukti ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 mukti ( uncountable ) 

 Feminine synonym of masculine substantive noun मोक्ष ( mokṣa , “ liberation ” ) .
 Synonym: moksha
