 Sanskrit पञ्चनद ( pañcanada ) calq. 

 Persian پنجاب 

 Compound of پنج ( panj , “ five ” ) +‎ آب ( âb , “ river ” ) , a calque of Sanskrit पञ्चनद ( pañcanada , “ [Land of] the Five Rivers ” ) , from पञ्च ( pañca , “ five ” ) and नद ( nada , “ river ” ) .
