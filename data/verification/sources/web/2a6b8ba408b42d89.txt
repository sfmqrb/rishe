 Verb

 سَرِبَ • ( sariba ) I ( non-past يَسْرَبُ ( yasrabu ) , verbal noun سَرَب ( sarab ) ) 

 ( intransitive ) to leak , to seep , to ooze [ with مِن ( min ) ‘from something’ ]

 burrow , den , lair 

 offlet , offtake , air return , flue
