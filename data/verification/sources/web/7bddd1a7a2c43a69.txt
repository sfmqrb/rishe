 Persian گمرک 

 Borrowed from Ottoman Turkish كمرك ( gümrük ) , borrowed from Byzantine Greek κομμέρκιον ( kommérkion ) , borrowed from Latin commercium , from con- ( “ together, with ” ) + merx ( “ merchandise ” ) + -ium .
