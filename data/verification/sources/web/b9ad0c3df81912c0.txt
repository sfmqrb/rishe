 Etymology 2
 [ edit ] 

 From شیو ( šiv ) +‎ ـا ( -ā ) . The adjective form of the verb شیفتن ( šiftan , “ to disturb, to disorder, to confuse, to fall in love ” ) .

 Etymology 3 

 From indian shiva . An indian god.
