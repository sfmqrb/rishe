 Persian

 Persian Wikipedia has an article on: راه 

 Wikipedia fa 

 Alternative forms
 [ edit ] 

 ره ( rah ) 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian lʾs ( /⁠rāh⁠/ , “ road, way ” ) from Proto-Iranian *Hrāθáh , from Proto-Indo-Iranian *Hrātʰás , from Proto-Indo-European *Hret- . Compare Old Armenian ռահ ( ṙah ) , borrowed from Middle Iranian.
