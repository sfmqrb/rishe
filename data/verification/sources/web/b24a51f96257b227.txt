 Etymology 1
 [ edit ] 

 A contraction of لاوک ( lâvak , “ kneading trough ” ) , derived with a l < n sound change from Old Iranian *nāwaka- , a diminutive of Proto-Iranian *náHuš ( “ boat ” ) ; doublet of ناوک ( nâvak , “ hopper or feeder of a mill; a small boat ” ) and ناوه ( nâve , “ kneading trough; a small boat ” ) . [ 1 ] [ 2 ] Cognate with Mazanderani [script needed] ( lɔk , “ kneading trough ” ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 لاک • ( lâk ) 

 trough , wooden vessel 

 shell of a tortoise ; also metaphorical. 
 در لاک خودم فرو رفتم. dar lâk -e xodam foru raftam. I went into my shell .

 Etymology 2
 [ edit ] 

 Borrowed from Prakrit 𑀮𑀓𑁆𑀔𑀸 ( lakkhā , “ lac ” ) , from Sanskrit लाक्षा ( lākṣā ) . Compare Hindi लाख ( lākh , “ lac ” ) , English lac .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 لاک • ( lâk ) 

 nail polish ; short for لاک ناخن ( lâk-e nâxon , “ nail polish ” ) 

 lacquer
