 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Ottoman Turkish یلك ( yelek ) , from Old Anatolian Turkish يَلَك ( yélek ) , from Old Anatolian Turkish يَل ( yél , “ wind ” ) -ek ( “ noun forming suffix ” ) , from Proto-Turkic *yẹl ( “ wind ” ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 yelek ( definite accusative yeleği , plural yelekler ) 

 waistcoat , vest
