 Wikipedia 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Late Latin chalazion , from Ancient Greek χαλάζιον ( khalázion ) , diminutive of χάλαζα ( khálaza , “ hail ” ) .

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 IPA ( key ) : /kəˈleɪzi.ən/ 

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 chalazion ( plural chalazions or chalazia ) 

 ( ophthalmology , optometry ) A cyst in the eyelid , caused by inflammation of a blocked meibomian gland .

 Related terms
 [ edit ] 

 chalaza
