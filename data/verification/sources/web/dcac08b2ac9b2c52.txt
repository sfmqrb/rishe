 Old Persian
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Proto-Iranian *jráyah , from Proto-Indo-Iranian *ȷ́ráyas . Cognate with Avestan 𐬰𐬭𐬀𐬌𐬌𐬀𐬢𐬵 ( zraiiaŋh , “ sea ” ) , Sanskrit ज्रयस् ( jráyas , “ expanse ” ) . [ 1 ] 

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 𐎭𐎼𐎹 ( d-r-y / drayaʰ /) 

 sea
