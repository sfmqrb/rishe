 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Inherited from Middle Persian [script needed] ( ʾšwp /⁠āšōb⁠/ , “ turmoil, disturbance ” ) . Cognate with Parthian 𐫀𐫀𐫢𐫇𐫁 ( ʾʾšwb /⁠āšōβ⁠/ , “ turmoil, disturbance ” ) .
