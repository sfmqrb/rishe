 Persian مالیدن 

 Inherited from Middle Persian [script needed] ( mālīdan /⁠mʾlytn'⁠/ ) , from Proto-Iranian *Hmarz- ( “ to wipe; to rub ” ) , from Proto-Indo-Iranian [Term?] , from Proto-Indo-European *h₃merǵ- ( “ to wipe ” ) . Compare Sanskrit मृज् ( mṛj , “ to wipe, to rub ” ) , Ancient Greek ὀμόργνῡμῐ ( omórgnūmĭ , “ to rub ” ) . [ 1 ]

 Verb
 [ edit ] 

 مالیدن • ( mâlidan ) ( present stem مال ( mâl ) ) 

 to rub 
 Synonym: ساییدن ( sâyidan ) 

 to massage 
 Synonym: ماساژ دادن ( mâsâž dâdan ) 

 to smear
