 Alternative forms
 [ edit ] 

 Saca ( dated ) 

 Etymology 1
 [ edit ] 

 First attested in the late 19th century as a learned borrowing from Sanskrit शक ( śaka ) , from Old Persian 𐎿𐎣𐎠 ( s-k-a /⁠Sakā⁠/ , “ Scythian ” ) , from 𐎿𐎣 ( s-k /⁠Saka⁠/ , “ Scythia ” ) , from Scythian . Mostly displaced earlier Saca , ultimately from the same source via Latin. Doublet of Saca , Shaka , and Scyth .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 Saka ( plural Saka or Sakas ) 

 A member of any of various peoples formerly inhabiting steppes north of the Iranian plateau .

 Proper noun
 [ edit ] 

 Saka 

 The Iranian language of the Saka people.

 Statistics
 [ edit ] 

 According to the 2010 United States Census, Saka is the 40976 th most common surname in the United States, belonging to 532 individuals. Saka is most common among White (46.62%), Black/African American (31.02%) and Asian/Pacific Islander (17.67%) individuals.

 Etymology 1
 [ edit ] 

 Borrowed from Javanese ꦱꦏ ( saka ) , from Old Javanese śāka , śaka ( “ Shaka year ” ) , from Sanskrit शक ( śaka ) , शाक ( śāka ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ]

 Etymology 2
 [ edit ] 

 Borrowed from Russian саха́ ( saxá ) , from Yakut саха ( saxa ) , from Proto-Turkic *yaka ( “ edge; collar ” ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ]
