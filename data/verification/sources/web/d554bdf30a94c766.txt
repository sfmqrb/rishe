 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 אָבּ ( ob ) ( Latin spelling ob , Cyrillic spelling об ) 

 water 
 1904 , Shimon ben Eliyahu Hakham, “Yake Buzghola [ One Little Goat ]”‎ [2] : וְבִּיאָמַד אָבּ וְכָאמוֹשׁ כַרְד בְּאָתַשׁ, אָנְגִ׳י בִּיסוֹזָנד בְּעַצָא, אָנְגִ׳י בִּיזַר בְּאָן סַגּ, אָנְגִ׳י בִּיגַּזִיד בְּפִּישַׁךּ, אָנְגִ׳י בִּכוֹרְד בְּבּוּזְגָאלַה, אָנְגִ׳י בִּיכַרִיד פַדַר בְּדוּ תַנְגַּה. גָׄאן בּוּזְגָאלַה גָׄאן בּוּזְגָאלַה. Ve biyomad ob ve xomūsh xard be otash, onchi bisūzond be aso, onchi bizad be on sag, onchi bigozid be pishok, onchi bixūrd be buzghola, onchi bixarid padar be du tanga. Jon buzghola, jon buzghola. Water came and put out the fire that burned the stick that hit the dog that bit the cat that ate the goat that my father bought for two zuzim. Dear little goat, dear little goat.

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 אַב • ( av ) 

 singular construct state form of אָב ( av ) ‎ .

 Etymology 2
 [ edit ] 

 From Akkadian 𒌚𒉈 ( Abum ) .

 Proper noun
 [ edit ] 

 אָב • ( áv )  m 

 ( Judaism ) Av ( the eleventh month of the civil year and the fifth month of the ecclesiastical year in the Jewish calendar , after Tammuz and before Elul ) 
 a. 217 C.E. , Mishnah . Ta'anit , section 4.6 : חֲמִשָּׁה דְבָרִים אֵרְעוּ אֶת אֲבוֹתֵינוּ בְּשִׁבְעָה עָשָׂר בְּתַמּוּז וַחֲמִשָּׁה בְּתִשְׁעָה בְ אָב . Ḥamishá d'varím er'ú et avoténu b-shiv'a asár b-Tammúz, va-ḥamishá b-tish'á b- Av . Five things befell our ancestors on the seventeenth of Tammuz, and five things on the ninth of Av . 

 a. 500 C.E. , Babylonian Talmud . Ta'anit , 29a : אָמַר רַב יְהוּדָה בְּרֵיהּ דְּרַב שְׁמוּאֵל בַּר שִׁילַת מִשְּׁמֵיהּ דְּרַב: כְּשֵׁם שֶׁמִּשֶּׁנִּכְנַס אָב מְמַעֲטִין בְּשִׂמְחָה — כָּךְ מִשֶּׁנִּכְנַס אֲדָר מַרְבִּין בְּשִׂמְחָה. amár rav y'hudá b'reh d'rav shmu'él bar shilát mi-shmeh d'rav: k-shem she-mi-she-nikhnás av m'ma'tín b-simkhá — kakh mi-she-nikhnás adár marbín b-simkhá. Said R. Judah son of Rav Shmuel son of Sheilath, in the name of Rav: just as when Av comes one decreases rejoicing, so too when Adar comes one increases rejoicing.
