 Persian زمستان 

 Inherited from Middle Persian [script needed] ( ẕmstʾn' /⁠zamestān⁠/ ) , borrowed from a Northwestern Iranian language, from Proto-Iranian *jimáh + *stā́nam .

 Cognate with Northern Kurdish zivistan , Ossetian зымӕг ( zymæg ) , Avestan 𐬰𐬌𐬌𐬃 ( ziiā̊ ) , Latin hiems , Sanskrit हिम ( himá ) , Pashto ژمی ( žaməy ) / Pashto زمی ( zaməy ) .
