 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Proto-Indo-Aryan *udáram , from Proto-Indo-Iranian *udáras , from Proto-Indo-European *úderos ( “ belly ” ) . Cognate with Avestan 𐬎𐬛𐬀𐬭𐬋𐬚𐬭𐬄𐬯𐬀 ( udarōθrąsa , “ snake creeping on the belly ” ) , Latin uterus .

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 ( Vedic ) IPA ( key ) : /u.dɐ́.ɾɐ/ 

 ( Classical Sanskrit ) IPA ( key ) : /u.d̪ɐ.ɾɐ/ 

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 उ॒दर॑ • ( udára ) stem ,  n 

 the belly , abdomen , stomach 
 c. 1500 BCE – 1000 BCE , Ṛgveda 8.78.7 : क्रत्व॒ इत्पू॒र्णम् उ॒दरं॑ तु॒रस्या॑स्ति विध॒तः।
 वृ॒त्र॒घ्नः सो॑म॒पाव्नः॑॥ krátva ítpūrṇám udáraṃ turásyāsti vidhatáḥ.
 vṛtraghnáḥ somapā́vnaḥ. The belly of the Soma-drinker, the eager slayer of Vṛtra, is filled by the sacrificer's offering. 

 c. 1200 BCE – 1000 BCE , Atharvaveda
