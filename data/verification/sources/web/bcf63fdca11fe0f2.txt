 This Proto-Germanic entry contains reconstructed terms and roots . As such, the term(s) in this entry are not directly attested , but are hypothesized to have existed based on comparative evidence.

 Proto-Germanic
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Possibly from Proto-Indo-European *dʰrenǵ- ( “ to draw into one's mouth, sip, gulp ” ) , nasalised variant of *dʰreǵ- ( “ to draw, glide ” ) . Cognate with Lithuanian drė́gti ( “ to become moist ” ) , [ 1 ] Sanskrit ध्रजति ( dhrájati , “ to glide, swoop ” ) . [ 2 ]
