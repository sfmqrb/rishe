 Arabic
 [ edit ] 

 دَلْو 

 Arabic Wikipedia has an article on: دلو 

 Wikipedia ar 

 Etymology 1
 [ edit ] 

 Root 

 د ل و ( d l w ) 

 5 terms 

 From Proto-Semitic . Compare Akkadian 𒁕𒇻 ( /⁠dalū, dūlu⁠/ , “ bucket ” ) , Aramaic דול ( daul , “ bucket ” ) , Classical Syriac ܕܰܘܠܳܐ ( dawlā , “ pail, bucket ” ) , Hebrew דְּלִי ( dəlī , “ pail, bucket ” ) , as well as the loanwords from Semitic Old Armenian դոյլ ( doyl ) and Persian دول ( dul , “ bucket ” ) .

 Old Anatolian Turkish
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology 1
 [ edit ] 

 Inherited from Proto-Turkic *tēlbe .

 cognates

 Cognate with Bashkir тиле ( tile ) , Kazakh дәлду ( däldüw ) , Kyrgyz тели ( teli ) , Turkmen däli , Uyghur تەلۋە ( telwe ) and Uzbek telba .

 Alternative forms
 [ edit ] 

 دلی ( deli ) 

 Adjective
 [ edit ] 

 دلو • ( delü ) 

 mad , crazy , insane , lunatic , bonkers , not sane , exhibiting unsoundness or disorder of mind, mentally deranged 
 Synonyms: احمق ( aḥmaq ) , دیوانه ( dīvāne ) , مجنون ( mecnūn ) 

 brave , courageous , especially brave in an extreme manner

 rash , foolhardy , reckless , impulsive , precipitate , acting too quickly without considering the consequences and risks 

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 دلو • ( delü ) ( definite accusative دلویی ( delüyi ) , plural دلولر ( delüler ) ) 

 madman , madwoman , loony , nut , psycho , schizo , nutcase , a person who is insane or mentally disturbed

 braveheart , someone who is extremely brave 

 Derived terms
 [ edit ] 

 دلو دیوانه ( delü dīvāne , “ crazy ” ) 
 دلوجه ( delüce , “ darnel ” ) 
 دلورمك ( delürmek , “ to go crazy ” ) 
 دلولك ( delülik, delülük , “ madness ” )

 Ottoman Turkish
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Borrowed from Arabic دَلْو ( dalw , “ pail, bucket ” ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 دلو • ( delv ) ( plural دلا or ادلا ) 

 pail , bucket , a container used to carry liquids 
 Synonyms: آبریز ( abriz ) , قوغه ( koğa ) 

 ( astronomy ) Aquarius , a constellation of the zodiac 

 ( astrology ) Aquarius , the zodiac sign for the water carrier
