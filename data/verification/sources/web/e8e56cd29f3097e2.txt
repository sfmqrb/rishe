 Ancient Greek
 [ edit ] 

 Alternative forms
 [ edit ] 

 ἄρκος ( árkos ) 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Inherited from Proto-Hellenic *árktos , from Proto-Indo-European *h₂ŕ̥tḱos ( “ bear ” ) . [ 1 ] Cognates include Latin ursus , Welsh arth , Sanskrit ऋक्ष ( ṛ́kṣa ) , Old Armenian արջ ( arǰ ) , and Albanian ari . The meaning of “north” derives from Ἄρκτος ( Árktos , “ Ursa Major ” ) , because the constellation is in the northern part of the sky.
