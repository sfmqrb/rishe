Patila in Marathi glossary 

Marathi >

 pāṭīla (पाटील).—m ( H) The head managing officer of a village, the Paṭil. 2 Applied as a title of courtesy to a Maraṭha or Shudra in general. Pr. kāṃhīṃ nasō paṇa pāṭīla dādavyā asō Even if we have nothing in the house, yet let my husband be regarded with honor.
