 Alternative forms

 [ edit ] 

 From the imperfect of עָקַב , related to עָקֵב (heel).

 Proper noun
 [ edit ] 

 יַעֲקֹב • ( ya'akóv )  m 

 ( biblical ) Jacob 

 a male given name , equivalent to English Jacob
