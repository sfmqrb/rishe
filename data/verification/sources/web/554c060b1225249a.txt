 Persian
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Proto-Iranian *Háyxam , ultimately from Proto-Indo-European *h₁eyH- .

 Compare Ossetian их ( ix ) , Pashto يخ ( yax ) , Lithuanian ýnis ( “ glazed frost ” ) , Russian и́ней ( ínej , “ hoarfrost ” ) , Ossetian их ( ix , “ ice ” ) , German Eis , English ice .
