 Ancient Greek
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 According to Beekes, related to μασάομαι ( masáomai , “ to chew ” ) and μαστάζω ( mastázō , “ to chew ” ) , both terms he suspects to be of Pre-Greek origin. The formation is similar to that of πόρταξ ( pórtax ) and μύλαξ ( múlax ) . [ 1 ]

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 μάσταξ • ( mástax )  f ( genitive μάστακος ) ; third declension 

 mouth , jaws , that with which one chews 

 mouthful , morsel 

 locust (because of its voracity )

 Declension
 [ edit ]
