 This Proto-Indo-European entry contains reconstructed terms and roots . As such, the term(s) in this entry are not directly attested , but are hypothesized to have existed based on comparative evidence.

 Proto-Indo-European
 [ edit ] 

 Root
 [ edit ] 

 *h₂lek- 

 to protect 

 Reconstruction notes
 [ edit ] 

 The LIV reconstructs two separate roots, one of which is *h₂elk- [ 1 ] and the other is *h₂leks- . [ 2 ] The latter of these roots is considered by LIV to be the source *h₂lékseti , [ 2 ] though this same verb is otherwise classified as a derivative of the root *h₂elk- . [ 3 ]
