 [ edit ]

 Conjunction

 وَ • ( wa ) 

 and , a divider of words or clauses , particle of connection or continuation often unnecessary to render in translation , or able to be rendered as a comma or semi-colon 
 yet , but ; a versatile conjunction determined by context 

 while , at the same time , and also do , simultaneously , to do this and to do that concurrently

(Proverb about illnesses coming from bad combinations; originally the supposed increased risk of contracting leprosy, a myth stemming from fish scales and the white color of milk.)

 Usage notes
 [ edit ] 

 Arabic conjunctions and prepositions that have only one letter, such as وَ ( wa ) , are written as a prefix.
 The connection to the following word may however exceptionally be cut off by quotation marks enclosing foreign terms, emoji and similar intrusions of meta elements in the language of the internet.

 Sometimes editions of Arabic texts procured in Iran do not adhere to the rules, instead following the non-joining practice of Persian و ( va, o , “ and ” ) .

 Arabic tends to use the conjunctions وَ ( wa ) and أَو ( ʔaw , “ or ” ) between every conjunct in a list, contrasting with the English tendency to use and and or only between the final two.
 كُتُبٌ وَ طُرُودٌ وَ أَوْرَاقٌ kutubun wa- ṭurūdun wa- ʔawrāqun books, parcels , and papers (literally, “ books and parcels and papers ”)

 [ edit ] 

 ـَوـ • ( -aw- )

 Letter

 The twenty-fourth letter of the Kazakh alphabet , written in the Arabic script ; preceded by ھ ( h ) and followed by ۇ ( u ) . 

 Usage notes
 [ edit ] 

 Represents the back vowel phoneme /o/, unless a small ء (hamza) was placed in the beginning of the word. In Kazakh the Hamza marks that all vowels in the following word are fronted. If و is the first letter and is representing a front vowel, it is combined with the hamza and the varient ٶ is used.

 Etymology 1
 [ edit ] 

 Inherited from Arabic وَ ( wa , “ and ” ) . Some derived terms additionally reflect its Arabic function of introducing an oath.

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 IPA ( key ) : /w/ ; [u, o] ( before a single consonant ) ; [wə] ( before multiple consecutive consonants )

 Etymology 2
 [ edit ] 

 Sense 2 , فَوْعَل ( fa w ʕal; Ca w CaC ) , may be generalized from denominal verbs whose و ( w ) is part of the morphology: consider كَوْبَس ( kawbas , “ to have a nightmare ” ) , denominal from كَابُوس ( kābūs , “ nightmare ” ) , where the و ( w ) regularly represents the ا ( ā ) of the noun.

 Sense 1 , فَعْوَل ( faʕ w al; CaC w aC ) , may be metathesized from this or it may be from another source.

 Infix
 [ edit ] 

 و • ( -w- ) 

 Extension for triliteral roots, inserted between the first and second radicals, that imparts a diminutive, iterative, or frequentative meaning: فَعْوَل ( faʕ w al; CaC w aC ) 

 ( less productive ) Extension for triliteral roots, inserted between the second and third radicals, that imparts an intensive meaning [ 1 ] : فَوْعَل ( fa w ʕal; Ca w CaC )

 Etymology 1
 [ edit ] 

 Both inherited from Middle Persian and borrowed from Arabic , hence the multiple pronunciations:

 Pronunciation 1: From Middle Persian 𐭠𐭥𐭣 ( ʾʿd /⁠ud⁠/ ) , 𐭠𐭥 ( ʾʿ /⁠u⁠/ ) ; from Old Persian 𐎢𐎫𐎠 ( u-t-a /⁠utā⁠/ , “ and ” ) , from Proto-Indo-Iranian *(H)utá , from *(H)u , from Proto-Indo-European *h₂u .

 Pronunciation 2: From Arabic وَ ( wa ) , from Proto-Semitic *wa . Also the etymology of the written form. Cognate with Biblical Hebrew וְ־ ( wa- ) , Ge'ez ወ- ( wä- ) , Classical Syriac ܘ- ( wə- ) , and Turkish ve .

 Conjunction
 [ edit ] 

 و • ( wa, u / va, o ) ( Tajik spelling ва , or у ) 

 expressing two elements to be taken together or in addition to each other: 
 connecting words to each other: and 
 21st century , Mohammad Farrokhi Yazdi 
 گر چه جیب و جام من از مال و مِی تُهیست. Gar če jib o jām-e man az māl o mey tohist. Although my pocket and my cup are empty of money and wine. 

 connecting two clauses: and 

 connecting certain numbers: and 
 پنجاه و شش panjāh u šaš
panjâh o šeš fifty-six (literally, “ fifty and six ”) 

 combining numbers in addition: plus 
 پنج و شش چند می‌شود panj wa šaš čand mē-šawad
panj va šeš čand mi-šavad What does five plus six become? 

 ( not productive ) particle denoting an oath: by 
 والله wal-lāh
val-lâh By God 

 Usage notes
 [ edit ] 

 In the contemporary spelling of Persian, the و ( va ) conjunction is not joined to the following word. However, the practice of joining the conjunction to the following word, as it is in Arabic, was not uncommon in early classical texts.
