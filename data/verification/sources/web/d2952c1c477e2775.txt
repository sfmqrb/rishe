 Etymology 2
 [ edit ] 

 From West Circassian Къэфкъас ( Qɛfqas ) , from Ancient Greek Καύκασος ( Kaúkasos ) . This is a modern neo-Circassian name adopted by the diaspora in Turkey to honor their homeland. Its specific usage as a personal given name is a modern phenomenon unique to the Caucasian diaspora, rather than a historical or traditional Circassian name.

 Proper noun
 [ edit ] 

 Kafkas 

 a male given name from West Circassian , popular among Circassians in Turkey. [ 1 ] Circassian names are generally losing popularity due to assimilation and Turkification.
