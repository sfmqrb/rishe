 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian hwšng ( Hōšang ) , from Old Iranian . Akin to Avestan 𐬵𐬀𐬊𐬱𐬌𐬌𐬀𐬢𐬵𐬀 ( haošiiaŋha , “ Haoshyangha ” ) .

 Pronunciation

 ( Kabuli ) IPA ( key ) : [ʔoː.ʃäŋɡ]

 هوشنگ • ( hušang ) 

 ( Iranian mythology ) Hushang 

 a male given name , Hushang , Houshang , Hooshang , and Hoshang , from Middle Persian
