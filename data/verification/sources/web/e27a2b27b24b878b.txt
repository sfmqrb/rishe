 Varhadi
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Borrowed from Classical Persian رد ( radd ) , from Arabic رَدّ ( radd ) .

 Adjective
 [ edit ] 

 रद ( rad ) ( indeclinable ) 

 cancelled , null and void 
 बुढी बिमार असल्यानं म्या गावाले जानं रद केलं. buḍhī bimār aslyāna myā gāvāle jāna rad kela. I cancelled my plans to go out of town because of mother's illness. 

 deficient , inferior 
 आपल्या पोरीमोहरं साऱ्या पोरी रद हायत. āplyā porīmohra sāryā porī rad hāyt.
