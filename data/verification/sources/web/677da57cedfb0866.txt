 Etymology 1
 [ edit ] 

 Persian Wikipedia has an article on: شهر ری 

 Wikipedia fa 

 From Middle Persian [Inscriptional Pahlavi needed] ( ldy /⁠Ray⁠/ ) , from Old Persian 𐎼𐎥𐎠 ( Ragā ) , a Median district. Compare Akkadian 𒊏𒂵𒀪 ( ra-ga-' ) , Elamite 𒊩𒋡𒀭 ( rák-ka 4 -an ) (loc.), Ancient Greek Ῥάγαι ( Rhágai ) , Latin Rhagae , Rhaganae , Iranian borrowings.
