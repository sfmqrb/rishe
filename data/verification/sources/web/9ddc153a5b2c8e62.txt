 Wikipedia 

 Alternative forms
 [ edit ] 

 ( surname ) : Mervin , Mervine , Mirfin 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Welsh Merfyn ( “ Mervyn ” ) , probably from mêr ( “ marrow ” ) + myn ( “ eminent ” ) .

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 ( Received Pronunciation ) IPA ( key ) : /ˈmɑː.vɪn/ 

 ( General American ) IPA ( key ) : /ˈmɑɹ.vɪn/ 

 Rhymes: -ɑː(ɹ)vɪn 

 Proper noun
 [ edit ] 

 Marvin 

 A male given name from Welsh .
 1979 , Douglas Adams, The Hitch Hiker's Guide to the Galaxy: Chapter 11 : "Thank you the marketing division of the Sirius Cybernetic Corporation," said Marvin and trudged desolately up the gleaming curved corridor that stretched out before them. " Let's build robots with Genuine People personalities , they said. So they tried out with me. I'm a personality prototype. You can tell can't you?" 

 A surname from Welsh .

 A place in the United States :
 An unincorporated community in Morgan County , Missouri .

 A village in Union County , North Carolina .

 A small town in Grant County , South Dakota .
