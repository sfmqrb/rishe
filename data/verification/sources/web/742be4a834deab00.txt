 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Hebrew אבנר ( Abner , “ father of light ” ) . From אָב ( av , “ father ” ) + נור ( nur , “ light ” ) .

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 ( US ) IPA ( key ) : /ˈæb.nɚ/ 

 Proper noun
 [ edit ] 

 Abner ( plural Abners ) 

 ( biblical ) A cousin of Saul mentioned in the Old Testament .
 1611 , The Holy Bible,   [ … ] ( King James Version ), London: [ … ] Robert Barker ,   [ … ] , →OCLC , 1 Samuel 17:55 : And when Saul saw David go forth against the Philistine, he said unto Abner , the captain of the host, Abner , whose son is this youth? And Abner said, As thy soul liveth, O king, I cannot tell. 

 A male given name from Hebrew .
