 Alternative forms

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Medieval Latin Avicenna , from Arabic اِبْن سِينَا ( ibn sīnā ) .

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 ( UK , US ) IPA ( key ) : /ˌævɪˈsɛnə/ 

 Rhymes: -ɛnə 

 Proper noun
 [ edit ] 

 Avicenna 

 Persian polymath (c. 980–1037).
 1528 , Joannes de Mediolano, translated by Thomas Paynell, Regimen Sanitatis Salerni , page 33 : Auicen saythe ; There be certeyne medicins ... which wyl not suffre poyson to approche nere the harte , as triacle and Metridate .
