 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 ( Standard Hindi ) IPA ( key ) : /t̪ɑːl/ , [t̪äːl] 

 Etymology 1
 [ edit ] 

 Borrowed from Sanskrit ताल ( tāla ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 ताल • ( tāl )  m ( Urdu spelling تال ) 

 ( music ) rhythm , beat , tune 

 palm tree 
 Synonym: ताड़ ( tāṛ ) 

 the palm of the hand 
 Synonym: हथेली ( hathelī ) 

 the clapping of palms 
 Synonym: ताली ( tālī ) 

 ( wrestling ) slapping the hands on the arms in defiance

 Etymology 1
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology tree

 Proto-Dravidian *tāẓ der. 
 Dravidian bor. 
 Sanskrit ताल 

 Borrowed from Dravidian ; ultimately from Proto-Dravidian *tāẓ . Compare Kannada ತಾಳೆ ( tāḷe , “ palmyra or toddy palm ” ) , Telugu తాటి ( tāṭi , “ of or pertaining to the palmyra tree ” ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 ताल • ( tāla ) stem ,  m 

 the palm tree , palmyra tree, or fan palm (Borassus flabelliformis, producing a sort of spirituous liquor)

 the nut of fan palm

 toddy 

 a fragrant earth

 Etymology 6
 [ edit ] 

 From ताली ( tālī , “ a type of ornament ” ) , of Dravidian origin; compare Tamil தாலி ( tāli , “ neck ornament ” ) , Telugu తాళి ( tāḷi , “ neck gold ” ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 ताल • ( tāla ) stem ,  m or n 

 an orpiment
