 Latin
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Proto-Italic *westis , from Proto-Indo-European *wéstis , from *wes- ( “ to be dressed ” ) . [ 1 ] Cognate with Sanskrit वसन ( vasana , “ garment ” ) , Old Armenian զգեստ ( zgest ) , Gothic 𐍅𐌰𐍃𐍄𐌹 ( wasti ) , Tocharian B wastsi , and Ancient Greek εἷμα ( heîma , “ garment ” ) . The root was also the source of English wear .

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 ( Classical Latin ) IPA ( key ) : [ˈwɛs.tɪs] 

 ( modern Italianate Ecclesiastical ) IPA ( key ) : [ˈvɛs.tis] 

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 vestis   f ( genitive vestis ) ; third declension 

 garment , gown , robe , vestment , clothing , vesture 
 Synonyms: amictus , vēlāmen
