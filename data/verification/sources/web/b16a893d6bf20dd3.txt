 Old Armenian
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Perhaps from Proto-Indo-European *gol- and cognate with Tocharian A kälyme , Tocharian B kälymiye ( “ direction ” ) . Alternatively, of the same origin as կողր ( kołr ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 կող • ( koł ) 

 rib 
 ի կող լինել ― i koł linel ― to stretch or lay oneself down, to lie down, to go to bed 

 side ( of a mountain, etc. ) 
 ընդ կողաց ― ənd kołacʻ ― aside, awry, sidelong 
 զառ ի կող, առ ի կող ― zaṙ i koł, aṙ i koł ― inclining, that bends, that goes stooping 
 կողք գրեանց ― kołkʻ greancʻ ― cover or binding of a book 
 անդէն ի կողի ― andēn i kołi ― directly, at once, forthwith 

 ( figuratively ) spouse 

 Usage notes
 [ edit ] 

 In some compounds the word has the combining form կողին- ( kołin- ) , which is the genitive of *կողն ( *kołn ) , an alternative form of կող ( koł ) .
