 Alternative forms
 [ edit ] 

 ژیره ( žire ) ( archaic ) 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 (This etymology is missing or incomplete. Please add to it, or discuss it at the Etymology scriptorium .) 

 Cognate with Baluchi زیرگ ( zirrag ) , Northern Kurdish zîre , Central Kurdish زیرە ( zîre ) , Southern Kurdish زیرە ( zîre ) , Sogdian [script needed] ( zyr'kk /⁠zîrê⁠/ ) , and distantly with Sanskrit जीरक ( jīraka ) . Further origins outside of Iranian unclear.
