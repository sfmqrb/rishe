 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 kamees ( plural kamees or kameeses ) 

 A long, loose shirt or blouse worn in some South Asian and Islamic countries.
 2023 , Radhika Iyengar, Fire on the Ganges , Fourth Estate, page 125 : Komal caresses the edge of her kameez with her fingers.
