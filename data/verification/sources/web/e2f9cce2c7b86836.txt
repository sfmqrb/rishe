 This Proto-Scythian entry contains reconstructed terms and roots . As such, the term(s) in this entry are not directly attested , but are hypothesized to have existed based on comparative evidence.

 Proto-Scythian
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Uncertain; possibly from *šali ( “ leg, thigh ” ) (cognate with Persian شل ( šal , “ thigh ” ) ) + *wāri ( “ covering, enclosure ” ) , Proto-Iranian *wā́rah . Compare Younger Avestan 𐬯𐬁𐬭𐬀𐬬𐬁𐬭𐬋 ( sāravārō , “ helmet ” , literally “ head covering ” ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 *šalawāri   m 

 pants , trousers 

 Alternative reconstructions
 [ edit ] 

 *šaraβāri [ 1 ]
