 This Proto-Indo-European entry contains reconstructed terms and roots . As such, the term(s) in this entry are not directly attested , but are hypothesized to have existed based on comparative evidence.

 Proto-Indo-European
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Perhaps some variation or gradation of *gʷeh₂- , hypothetically seen also on the pairs *men-dʰ(H)-  : *meh₂-dʰ(H)- and *drem-  : *dreh₂- ( “ to run ” ) . This etymology is incomplete. You can help Wiktionary by elaborating on the origins of this term. 

 Root
 [ edit ] 

 *gʷem- ( perfective ) [ 1 ] [ 2 ] 

 to come , [ 3 ] to step [ 4 ] 
 Synonyms: *gʷeh₂- , *melh₃-
