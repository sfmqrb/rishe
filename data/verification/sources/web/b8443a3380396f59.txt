 Old Armenian
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Parthian 𐫤𐫓𐫇𐫀𐫡 ( tlwʾr /⁠talawār⁠/ , “ hall, tabernacle ” ) . Compare Persian تالار ( tâlâr , “ hall, saloon ” ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 տաղաւար • ( taławar ) 

 tent , tabernacle , booth , cottage
