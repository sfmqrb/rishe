 Persian
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian stpl ( stabr , “ strong, firm ” ) . Akin to Sanskrit स्तम्भ ( stambha , “ pillar ” ) (and स्तभ्नाति ( stabhnāti , “ he supports ” ) ), Old Norse stafr ( “ staff ” ) , Old Church Slavonic стоборъ ( stoborŭ , “ column ” ) and Ancient Greek στιβαρός ( stibarós , “ firm ” ) .
