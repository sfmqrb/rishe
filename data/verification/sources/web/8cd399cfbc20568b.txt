 Tagalog
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Western Proto-Malayo-Polynesian *ulak .

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 ( Standard Tagalog ) IPA ( key ) : /ʔuˈlak/ [ʔʊˈlak̠̚] , /ˈʔulak/ [ˈʔuː.lɐk̠̚] 

 Rhymes: -ak , -ulak 

 Syllabification: u‧lak 

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 ulák or ulak ( Baybayin spelling ᜂᜎᜃ᜔ ) 

 spool ; reel 
 Synonyms: ulakan , ikiran , kidkiran , karete , puluputan , bilibiran , pulunan 

 act of making thread or yarn ( from fibers or filaments )

 Turkish
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Ottoman Turkish اولاق ( ulak ) , analyzable as ula- +‎ -k . Ultimately from Proto-Turkic *ula- ( “ to attach, join (ends) ” ) .
