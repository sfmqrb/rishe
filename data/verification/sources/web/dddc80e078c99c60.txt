 Etymology 1.1
 [ edit ] 

 Root 

 ن س خ ( n s ḵ ) 

 9 terms 

 Borrowed from Aramaic נְסַח  / 𐡍𐡎𐡇 ( nsaḥ , “ to take away, to draw off, to eliminate ” ) related to the Arabic root ن ز ع ( n z ʕ ) .

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 IPA ( key ) : /na.sa.xa/ 

 Verb
 [ edit ] 

 نَسَخَ • ( nasaḵa ) I ( non-past يَنْسَخُ ( yansaḵu ) , verbal noun نَسْخ ( nasḵ ) ) 

 to transcribe , to copy 

 ( computing ) to copy 

 to abolish 

 to delete 

 to abrogate , to invalidate 

 to repeal , to revoke , to withdraw 

 to cancel 

 to substitute , to replace

 Etymology 1.2
 [ edit ] 

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 IPA ( key ) : /na.sa.xa/ 

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 نَسْخ • ( nasḵ )  m 

 verbal noun of نَسَخَ ( nasaḵa )   ( form I ) 

 abolition , abolishment , abrogation , cancellation , invalidation 

 copying , transcription 

 Naskh , a cursive style of Arabic calligraphy or font , the one most popular for and characteristic of the Arabic language itself.
