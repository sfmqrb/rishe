 English
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Persian میرزا ( mirzâ ) .

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 ( UK ) IPA ( key ) : /ˈmɪəzə/ , /ˈməːzə/ 

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 mirza ( plural mirzas ) 

 ( historical ) An educated man in India or Iran (Persia); an official , a clerk . [from 16th c.] 
 1788 , Edward Gibbon , Decline and Fall of the Roman Empire , VI.65 : The same success attended the other mirzas and emirs in their excursions. 

 1990 , Peter Hopkirk , The Great Game , Folio Society, published 2010 , page 46 : This he presented to the Khan of Puhra, who called upon his mirza , or clerk, to read it aloud.
