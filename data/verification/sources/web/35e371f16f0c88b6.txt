 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Inherited from Middle Persian 𐭦𐭱𐭲 ( zyšt' /⁠zišt⁠/ , “ hateful, ugly ” ) , a loanword from Avestan 𐬰𐬀𐬉𐬱𐬀 ( zaēša ) from Proto-Indo-Iranian *ȷ́ʰáyždas , from Proto-Indo-European *ǵʰéysd-os , from *ǵʰeysd- ( “ anger, agitation ” ) . Cognate with Proto-Germanic *gaistaz ( “ ghost, mind ” ) .
