 Old Persian
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Proto-Iranian *hijwáH .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 𐏃𐏀𐎠𐎴𐎶 ( h-z-a-n-m / hⁱzānam /) ( accusative singular ) 

 tongue 
 DB 2.74: [𐏐] 𐎢𐎫𐎠 𐏐 𐎴𐎠𐏃𐎶 𐏐 𐎢𐎫𐎠 𐏐 𐎥𐎢𐏁𐎠 𐏐 𐎢𐎫𐎠 𐏐 𐏃𐏀𐎠𐎴𐎶 𐏐 𐎳𐎼𐎠𐎩𐎴𐎶 𐏐 𐎢𐎫𐎠𐏁/𐎡𐎹 [ : ] u-t-a : n-a-h-m : u-t-a : g-u-š-a : u-t-a : h-z-a-n-m : f-r-a-j-n-m : u-t-a-š/i-y I cut off his nose, ears, and tongue , and I put out one of his eyes.
