 Pronunciation
 [ edit ]

 room , apartment , flat

 settlement , decision , scheme 

 garden bed

 بَيَّتَ • ( bayyata ) II ( non-past يُبَيِّتُ ( yubayyitu ) , verbal noun تَبْيِيت ( tabyīt ) ) 

 to brood 

 to contrive , to hatch (a plan, plot)

 to put (someone) up for the night

 to flunk , to fail (in school)

 Noun

 بيت • ( bīt )  m ( plural بيوتة ( byūta ) ) 

 ( sports ) goal 
 وخا يمركيوا بيت ما غاديش يربحوا. waḵḵa ymarkīw bīt ma ḡādīš yrabḥu Even if they score a goal they won't win.
