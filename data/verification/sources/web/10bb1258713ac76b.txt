 Talysh
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology 1
 [ edit ] 

 Probably related to Northern Kurdish gir ( “ big, large ” ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 qir 

 power 
 Synonym: zü 

 difficulty 

 Etymology 2
 [ edit ] 

 Cognate to Persian گیر ( “ catch ” ) , probably a loanword from Persian.

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 qir 

 grasp , catch
