 Persian
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Inherited from Middle Persian [Book Pahlavi needed] ( pyš /⁠pēš⁠/ , “ before, pre-, past, beside ” ) , [ 1 ] of unclear further origin. Multiple theories have been proposed: [ 2 ] 

 proposed etymologies

 Horn takes the word as a derivative of Old Persian [script needed] ( patiš , “ against ” ) , from Proto-Iranian *pati- ( “ against ” ) , itself from Proto-Indo-European *per- ( “ in front; first ” ) ; this is rejected by Hubschmann.

 Nyberg considers the word as a southwestern dialectal form of Old Persian [script needed] ( paišiyā , “ in front, before ” ) ; however, he does not elaborate further. [ 3 ] 

 Gershevitch derives the word from Proto-Iranian *pasya- , from Proto-Iranian *pas- ( “ to observe, look ” ) (with phonetic assimilation of *sy- to š- , à la the shift from Proto-Iranian *kacyápah ( “ tortoise, turtle ” ) to Persian کشف ( kašaf , “ tortoise, turtle ” ) ), from Proto-Indo-European *speḱ- ( “ to see, look, observe ” ) .

 Most likely, according to Edelman, is a derivation from an earlier Proto-Iranian *pati-aši- ( “ before or against one's eyes ” ) , from Proto-Iranian *pati- ( “ against ” ) + *Háši ( “ eye ” ) , the latter from Proto-Indo-Iranian *Hákši ( “ eye ” ) . This would make the word an exact cognate with Sanskrit प्रत्यक्ष ( pratyakṣa , “ present before the eyes, obvious, clear ” ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 پیش • ( pēš / piš ) ( Tajik spelling пеш ) 

 front ( side of something ) 
 Antonyms: پس ( pas ) , پشت ( pušt  / pošt ) 

 ( chiefly Dari , linguistics ) damma ( vowel diacritic ) 
 Synonym: ( more common in Iran ) ضمه ( zamma  / zamme ) 

 Adjective
 [ edit ] 

 پیش • ( pēš / piš ) ( Tajik spelling пеш ) 

 last , prior , before 
 پنج ساعت پیش panj sā'at-i pēš 
panj sâ'at-e piš the last five hours 

 هفتهٔ پیش hafta-yi pēš 
hafte-ye piš the prior week 

 of the past , of old 

 Adverb
 [ edit ] 

 پیش • ( pēš  / piš ) ( Tajik spelling пеш ) 

 ( of time ) ago , before , previously 
 پنج ساعت پیش panj sā'at pēš 
panj sâ'at piš five hours ago 

 از مدت‌ها پیش az muddat-hā pēš 
az moddat-hâ piš from a long time ago 

 in advance 

 ( of movement ) forward , ahead 

 Preposition
 [ edit ] 

 پیش • ( pēš  / piš ) ( Tajik spelling пеш ) ( with pronoun or ezâfe ) 

 in front of , before , in the presence of
 پیش شاهنشاه pēš-i šāhanšāh
 piš-e šâhanšâh before the king; in front of the king 

 indicating possession: on , with 
 کتاب خود را پیش من بگذار kitāb-i xwad rā pēš-i man buguzār
ketâb-e xod râ piš-e man bogozâr leave your book with me 

 کیفتو بذار پیشش ( colloquial Tehrani ) kifeto bezâr pišeš leave your bag with him/her 

 پیسه پیشم نیس ( colloquial Kabuli ) paysa pēšum nēs I don't have money on me 

 beside , near 
 پیش من بنشین pēš-i man binišīn
 piš-e man benešin sit beside me 

 Usage notes
 [ edit ] 

 adverb sense 1 : When used to mean "ago", the noun must be in the plural or given a quantity, thus: 

 یک ماه پیش yak māh pēš
yek mâh piš a month ago 

 or 

 ماه‌ها پیش māh-hā pēš
mâh-hâ piš months ago 

 not 

 ماه پیش* māh pēš
mâh piš *month ago
