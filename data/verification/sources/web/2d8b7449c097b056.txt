 Etymology 2
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology tree

 Arabic أُمَّة ( ʔumma ) 

 Proto-Afroasiatic 
 Proto-Semitic 
 Arabic ـِيّ ( -iyy ) 

 Arabic أمي 

 From أُمَّة ( ʔumma , “ nation ” ) + ـِيّ ( -iyy ) .

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 IPA ( key ) : /ʔum.mijj/ 

 Adjective
 [ edit ] 

 أُمِّيّ • ( ʔummiyy ) ( feminine أُمِّيَّة ( ʔummiyya ) , masculine plural أُمِّيُّون ( ʔummiyyūn ) , feminine plural أُمِّيَّات ( ʔummiyyāt ) ) 

 gentile , unlettered 
 609–632 CE , Qur'an , 7:157 : الَّذِينَ يَتَّبِعُونَ الرَّسُولَ النَّبِيَّ ال أُمِّيَّ الَّذِي يَجِدُونَهٗ مَكْتُوبًا عِنْدَهُمْ فِي التَّوْرٰةِ وَالاِنْجِيلِ And they that follow the Messenger, the gentile/unlettered prophet, whom they find recorded in their possession within the Law (Torah) and the Gospel 

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 أُمِّيّ • ( ʔummiyy )  m ( plural أُمِّيُّون ( ʔummiyyūn ) ) ( countable ) 

 gentile , illiterate , heathen 
 609–632 CE , Qur'an , 2:78 : وَمِنْهُمْ أُمِّيُّونَ لَا يَعْلَمُونَ الْكِتَابَ إِلَّا أَمَانِيَّ وَإِنْ هُمْ إِلَّا يَظُنُّونَ wa-minhum ʔummiyyūna lā yaʕlamūna l-kitāba ʔillā ʔamāniyya wa-ʔin hum ʔillā yaẓunnūna And among them are gentiles/illiterates who know not the Scripture but wishful thinking, and they but conjecture.
