 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology tree

 Proto-Indo-European *skend- der. ?

 Latin scandalum 

 From Ancient Greek σκάνδαλον ( skándalon , “ a trap laid for an enemy, a cause of moral stumbling ” ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 scandalum   n ( genitive scandalī ) ; second declension 

 scandal (in the moral sense)

 temptation (to sin)

 stumbling block 

 trap
