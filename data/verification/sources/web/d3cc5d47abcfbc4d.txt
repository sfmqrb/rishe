 Etymology 1
 [ edit ] 

 Inherited from Proto-Turkic *yǖŕ ( “ hundred ” ) .

 cognates

 Cognate with Old Turkic 𐰘𐰇𐰕 ( yüz ) , Bashkir йөҙ ( yüð ) , Chuvash ҫӗр ( śĕr ) , Kazakh жүз ( jüz ) , Kyrgyz жүз ( jüz ) , Turkmen ýüz , Azerbaijani yüz , Turkish yüz and Yakut сүүс ( süüs ) .

 Numeral
 [ edit ] 

 یوز • ( yüz ) 

 hundred , a numerical value equal to 100 (10 2 ), occurring after ninety-nine 

 Determiner
 [ edit ] 

 یوز • ( yüz ) 

 much , a lot 
 Synonym: یوکوش ( yüküš )

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 یوز • ( yüz ) ( plural یوزلار ( yüz-lär ) ) 

 face , visage , countenance , the front part of the head of a human or other animal , featuring the eyes , nose , and mouth , and the surrounding area
 Synonyms: چهره ( čährä ) , پیکر ( päykär ) , جبین ( jäbīn ) , روی ( rūy ) 
 1398 , Sibicabi, کلستان [ land of roses ], page 10b : اما ان‌لار ارا بیر ییکیت بار ایردی کیم ییکیت‌لیک عنفوانی نو ییتلیب حسن آیتی یوزی‌نینگ رایتیدا یانک‌لا ییلتب تورور ammā an-lar ara bir yigit bar erdi kim yigit-lik ʼunfuwāni näw yeltip ḥüsn āyeti yüzi-niŋ rāyätidä yaŋ-la yeltip turur but among them was a young man who had just reached the beginning of youth and the mark of beauty had just ripened on his face 

 1484 , Ali-Shir Nava'i , فرهاد و شیرین [ Farhad and Shirin ], page 257a , line 13 : یوزیدین روشن ایلب یوز‌نی کونین
انگا کوز‌دین اوجوب بیر قره العین yüzidin rūšän äyläp yüz-ni käwnäyn
aŋa köz-din učub bir qurrätü-lʼäyn (please add an English translation of this quotation) 

 face , surface , the outermost or uppermost layer of a physical object and usually the first region that can be perceived by an observer

 Etymology 2

 یوزلر 
 Inherited from Old Anatolian Turkish یوز ( yüz ) , from Proto-Turkic *yǖŕ ( “ face ” ) .

 Cognates

 Cognate with Old Turkic 𐰘𐰇𐰕 ( yüz ) , Azerbaijani yüz , Bashkir йөҙ ( yüð ) , Chuvash ҫӗр ( śĕr ) , Kazakh жүз ( jüz ) , Kyrgyz жүз ( jüz ) , Turkmen ýüz , Uyghur يۈز ( yüz ) , Uzbek yuz and Yakut сүүс ( süüs ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 یوز • ( yüz ) ( definite accusative یوزی ( yüzü ) , plural یوزلر ( yüzler ) ) 

 face , visage , countenance , the front part of the head of a human or other animal , featuring the eyes , nose , and mouth , and the surrounding area
 Synonyms: چهره ( çehre ) , رو ( rû ) , وجه ( vech, vecih ) 

 ( by extension ) face , surface , the outermost or uppermost layer of a physical object and usually the first region that can be perceived by an observer 
 Synonym: اوست ( üst ) 

 ( figuratively ) impudence , insolence , cheek , brazenness , effrontery , the quality of being impudent or cheeky , lack of respect 
 Synonym: پیشانی ( pişanî )

 Etymology 3
 [ edit ] 

 یوز 
 Borrowed from Persian یوز ( yuz , “ cheetah ” ) , from Middle Persian ywc ( yōz ) 

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 یوز • ( yuz ) ( definite accusative یوزی ( yuzu ) , plural یوزلر ( yuzlar ) ) 

 panther , any mammal of the genus Panthera , which includes big cats such as the leopard , jaguar , tiger , etc.
 Synonyms: بارس ( “ bars ” ) , پلنك ( “ peleng ” ) 

 cheetah , a distinctive member ( Acinonyx jubatus ) of the cat family, slightly smaller than the leopard
