 Arabic
 [ edit ] 

 مرجان 

 Arabic Wikipedia has an article on: مرجان (حيوان) 

 Wikipedia ar 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Classical Syriac ܡܪܰܓܳܢ ( margān , “ pearl-like ” ) , from ܡܰܪܓܳܢܺܝܬܳܐ ( margānīṯā , “ pearl ” ) , from Ancient Greek μαργαρίτης ( margarítēs , “ pearl ” ) , an Iranian borrowing. For the meaning development compare Arabic مَرْو ( marw , “ odoriferous plants; Maerua crassifolia ; pebble; flint; quartz ” ) .

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 IPA ( key ) : /mar.d͡ʒaːn/ , /mur.d͡ʒaːn/ 

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 مَرْجَان or مُرْجَان • ( marjān or murjān )  m ( collective , singulative مَرْجَانَة   f ( marjāna ) or مُرْجَانَة   f ( murjāna ) ) 

 small pearls 

 corals
