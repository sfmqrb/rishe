 Noun

 نسخه ( plural نسخه - نسخه or نسخه ٢ ) 

 Jawi spelling of naskhah .‎

 Ottoman Turkish
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Borrowed from Arabic نُسْخَة ( nusḵa ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 نسخه • ( nüsha ) 

 copy 

 number , issue 

 recipe , prescription
