 Etymology

 Etymology tree

 Proto-Indo-European *wes- der.

 Middle Persian wʾčʾl 
 Classical Persian بَازَار ( bāzār ) bor. 
 Ottoman Turkish بازار 

 Borrowed from Classical Persian بَازَار ( bāzār , “ market, marketplace ” ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 بازار • ( bazar ) ( definite accusative بازاری ( bazarı ) , plural بازارلر ( bazarlar ) ) 

 market , mart , fair 
 Synonym: سوق ( suk ) 

 market , marketplace , bazaar 
 Synonyms: چارشو ( çarşu ) , سوق ( suk ) 

 ellipsis of بازار كونی ( bâzâr günü , “ Sunday ” )
