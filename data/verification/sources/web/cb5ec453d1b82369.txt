 Persian
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Inherited from Middle Persian plwl-tn' ( parwardan , “ to foster, nourish, cherish, educate ” ) . Compare Northern Kurdish bergerr ( “ nourishment, nutrient; food for guests ” ) , Armenian պարարտ ( parart , “ fat, plump, portly ” ) . Exact cognate to pervert .

 Verb
 [ edit ] 

 پروردن • ( parvardan ) ( present stem پرور ( parvar ) ) 

 to foster , to nurture 

 to nourish 

 to cherish 

 to breed 

 to propagate 

 to raise 

 to rear 

 to mother 

 to train 

 to preserve ( to prepare food for storage )
