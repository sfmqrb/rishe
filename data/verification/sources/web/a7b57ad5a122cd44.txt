 Arabic تلفن 

 Derived from تِلِفُون ( tilifūn ) , borrowed from French téléphone , from télé- + -phone . First attested in 1932.

 تلفن • ( telefon ) ( plural تلفن‌ها ( telefon-hâ ) ) 

 telephone 
 شُمارِهٔ تِلِفُن شُما چیسْت؟ ( Iranian Persian, formal ) šomâre-ye telefon šomâ čist? What's your telephone number?
