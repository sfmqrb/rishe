 Persian
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian [Book Pahlavi needed] ( wcʾltn' /⁠wizārdan, wizār-⁠/ , “ to separate; to explain, interpret; to perform, fulfil, redeem ” ) , from Proto-Iranian *wí ( “ apart ” ) + a causative formation from Proto-Iranian *čarH ( “ to come and go, wander ” ) , from Proto-Indo-Iranian *wí + Proto-Indo-Iranian *čálHati ( “ to move, wander ” ) , the prefix ultimately from Proto-Indo-European *wí ( “ apart ” ) . Compare Old Armenian վճար ( včar ) , an Iranian borrowing.

 Verb
 [ edit ] 

 گزاردن • ( gozârdan ) ( present stem گزار ( gozâr ) ) 

 to express , to announce , to explain 

 to interpret 

 to perform 

 to pay , reimburse
