 Persian
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Inherited from Middle Persian [Book Pahlavi needed] ( hwnydʾk' /⁠huniyāg⁠/ , “ joyful ” ) , from Proto-Iranian *xwan-iyā-ka- , from the same root as خواندن ( xândan , “ to sing ” ) , ultimately from Proto-Indo-European *swenh₂- ( “ to sound ” ) .
