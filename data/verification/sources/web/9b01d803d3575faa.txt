 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology tree

 Arabic طَيِّء ( ṭayyiʔ ) der. 
 Middle Persian tʾcyk' 
 Persian تازیک bor. 
 Persian تاجیک 

 Possibly related to تازیک ( tâzik ) , a re-borrowing from Old Turkic . According to W. Henning, from *Tāt-čīk , originally *Tād-čīk . Popular folk etymology traces the word to Tajik тоҷ ( toj , “ crown ” ) (compare Persian تاج ( tâj , “ crown ” ) ).

 The Tajiks are an Aryan people.
