 Persian
 [ edit ] 

 Alternative forms
 [ edit ] 

 ارجیج ( arjīj  / arjij ) ( rare , obsolete ) 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian ʾlcyc ( arzīz , “ tin, lead ” ) ( Manichaean Middle Persian ʾrzyz ). Akin to Middle Armenian արճիճ ( arčič , “ lead ” ) , an Iranian borrowing. Perhaps ultimately from Proto-Iranian *arjata- ( “ silver ” ) , from Proto-Indo-European *h₂erǵ- . [ 1 ] Cognate with Avestan 𐬆𐬭𐬆𐬰𐬀𐬙𐬀 ( ər ə zata ) and Sanskrit रजत ( rajatá , “ silver ” ) .
