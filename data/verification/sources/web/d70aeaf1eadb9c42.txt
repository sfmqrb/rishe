 Alternative forms
 [ edit ] 

 آرنگ ( ârang ) 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian [Book Pahlavi needed] ( ʾplnc /⁠āranǰ⁠⁠/ ) , borrowed from Sogdian 𐫀𐫀𐫡𐫀𐫏𐫗𐫝 ( ʾʾrʾync /⁠ārinǰ⁠⁠/ ) , ultimately from Proto-Indo-Iranian *Haratníš . Compare Sanskrit अरत्नि ( aratni ) .

 Pronunciation
