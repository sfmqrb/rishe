 Arabic ترجم 

 Possibly from Classical Syriac ܬܰܪܓܶܡ , or possibly a back-formation from تَرْجُمَان ( tarjumān ) .

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 IPA ( key ) : /tar.d͡ʒa.ma/ 

 Verb
 [ edit ] 

 تَرْجَمَ • ( tarjama ) Iq ( non-past يُتَرْجِمُ ( yutarjimu ) , verbal noun تَرْجَمَة ( tarjama ) ) 

 to translate 
 تَرْجَمَتِ ٱلْكِتَابَ مِنَ ٱلْفَرَنْسِيَّةِ إِلَى ٱلْعَرَبِيَّةِ. tarjamati l-kitāba mina l-faransiyyati ʔilā l-ʕarabiyyati. She translated the book from French into Arabic. 

 to interpret 

 to expound , to treat by way of explanation

 to give biographical information on [ with لِ ( li ) ]
 هٰذَا ٱلْكِتَابُ يُتَرْجِمُ لِأَهَمِّ ٱلشُّعَرَاءِ ٱلْعَرَبِ. hāḏā l-kitābu yutarjimu liʔahammi š-šuʕarāʔi l-ʕarabi. This book outlines the biographies of the most important Arab poets.
