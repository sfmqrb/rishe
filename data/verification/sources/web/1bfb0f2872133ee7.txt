 Root 

 ه و ي ( h w y ) 

 17 terms 

 Etymology 1

 From the root ه و ي ( h w y ) , meaning a hole or hollow, a depression or declivity, a valley or chasm, hence the empty space or air that fills them.

 Pronunciation

 IPA ( key ) : /ha.waːʔ/ 

 Noun

 هَوَاء • ( hawāʔ )  m ( plural أَهْوِيَة ( ʔahwiya ) ) 

 ( uncountable ) air 

 ( uncountable ) atmosphere 

 synonym of جَوّ ( jaww , “ the apparently open space above the ground ” ) 

 empty space , vacancy , void 
 609–632 CE , Qur'an , 14:43 : وَأَفْئِدَتُهُمْ هَوَاءٌ waʔafʔidatuhum hawāʔun Their hearts are vain.
