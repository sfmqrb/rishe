 Luarsab Gender Male 
 Origin 
 Word/name Persian 
 Meaning Red Horse 
 Region of origin Georgia 

 Luarsab ( Georgian : ლუარსაბ ) is a Georgian male name derived from the Persian Lohrāsp , a name of the legendary Kayanid king from Ferdowsi ’s Shahnameh who reigned for 120 years.
