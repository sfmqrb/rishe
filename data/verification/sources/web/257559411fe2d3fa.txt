 Alternative forms
 [ edit ] 

 paraffine ( dated ) 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Latin paraffinum , from parum (too little) + affinis (related, affinity). Therefore low affinity or being chemically neutral .

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 ( Received Pronunciation ) IPA ( key ) : /ˈpæɹ.ə.fɪn/ 

 Audio ( Southern England ) : ( file ) 

 ( General American ) IPA ( key ) : /ˈpɛɹ.ə.fɪn/ 

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 English Wikipedia has an article on: paraffin 

 Wikipedia 

 paraffin ( countable and uncountable , plural paraffins ) 

 ( UK , Ireland ) A petroleum -based thin and colourless fuel oil .
 Synonym: kerosene 
 1919 , W[illiam] Somerset Maugham , “ chapter 57 ”, in The Moon and Sixpence , [ New York, N.Y. ] : Grosset & Dunlap Publishers   [ … ] , →OCLC : She poured paraffin on the dry floors and on the pandanus-mats, and then she set fire. 

 2023 October 18, Tom Perkins, “Flame retardant pollution threatens wildlife on all continents, research finds”, in The Guardian ‎ [1] : The paper found high levels of phased-out flame retardants like PCBs and PBDEs, as well as allegedly safe, newer replacements chemicals, such as chlorinated paraffins and organophosphates, across the globe. 

 ( petrochemistry , organic chemistry ) Any member of the alkane hydrocarbons .

 Paraffin wax .
