 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Persian زهر ( zahr , “ poison, venom ” ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 زهر • ( zehr, zehir ) 

 poison , venom 
 Synonyms: اغو ( ağu ) , اوت ( ot ) , سم ( sem ) 

 anything very disagreeable 

 grief , anxiety , care 

 anger , rage , chafe 

 Alternative forms
 [ edit ] 

 զէհր ( zehr ) , զէհիր ( zehir ) — Armeno-Turkish
