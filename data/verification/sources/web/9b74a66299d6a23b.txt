 This Proto-Indo-European entry contains reconstructed terms and roots . As such, the term(s) in this entry are not directly attested , but are hypothesized to have existed based on comparative evidence.

 Proto-Indo-European
 [ edit ] 

 Root
 [ edit ] 

 *mer- ( perfective ) [ 1 ] [ 2 ] [ 3 ] [ 4 ] 

 to die 
 Synonyms: *dʰew- , *dʰgʷʰey- , *neḱ- 
 Antonym: *gʷeyh₃- 

 to disappear 

 Reconstruction notes
 [ edit ] 

 According to Kloekhorst, the original meaning of the word was likely "to disappear," which is preserved in Hittite. The meaning "to die" perhaps emerged originally as a euphemism, but then later replaced the original meaning. For a comparison of a similar semantic development, Kloekhorst cites English pass away . If this theory is accepted, then—according to Kloekhorst—the more 'original' PIE word for "to die" may still be preserved in Hittite 𒀀𒆠 ( a-ki , “ to die ” ) . [ 5 ] [ 6 ]
