 Etymology 1
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian [Term?] ( /⁠āhūg⁠/ ) , ultimately from Proto-Indo-European *h₂eḱus ( “ sharp, swift ” ) . Cognates with Northern Kurdish ask and Baluchi آسک ( ásk ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 آهو • ( âhu ) 

 ( archaic ) defect, imperfection
 10th Century CE , Ferdowsi , Shahnameh 
 دو گوش و دو پای من آهو گرفت / تهی‌دستی و سال نیرو گرفت du gōš u du pāyi man āhō girift / tuhīdastī u sāl nērō girift My both ears and legs (my body) have grown faulty / and destitution and the old age emerged instead!
