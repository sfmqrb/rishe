 English
 [ edit ] 

 English Wikipedia has an article on: harmattan 

 Wikipedia 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Twi Akan haramata or a related word (whence also Spanish harmatán ). The ultimate origin is supposed by some to be an Arabic word, but which one is uncertain; حَرَام ( ḥarām , “ accursed thing ” ) has been suggested. [ 1 ] 

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 ( UK ) IPA ( key ) : /ˌhɑːməˈtæn/ , /ˌhɑːˈmætən/ 

 ( General American ) IPA ( key ) : /ˌhɑɹməˈtæn/ , /ˌhɑɹməˈtɑn/ , /ˌhɑɹˈmætən/ 

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 harmattan ( plural harmattans ) 

 A dry and dusty wind which blows from the Sahara over the Atlantic coast of West Africa in December, January and February, being a hot wind in some areas and a cold wind in others.
 1900 , Joshua Slocum , Sailing Alone Around the World : Storm-clouds stretched their arms across the sky; from the east, to all appearances, might come a fierce harmattan , or from the south might come the fierce hurricane. 

 1958 June 17, Chinua Achebe , Things Fall Apart , New York, N.Y.: Astor-Honor , published 1959 , →OCLC , part 1, page 1 : That was many years ago, twenty years or more, and during this time Okonkwo's fame had grown like a bush-fire in the harmattan . 

 A season which spans the period in which the harmattan wind blows.
