 Proper noun
 [ edit ] 

 Anahita   f 

 A taxonomic genus within the family Ctenidae  – certain wandering spiders .

 [ edit ]

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Old Persian 𐎠𐎴𐏃𐎡𐎫 ( a-n-h-i-t /⁠anahita⁠/ ) .

 Proper noun
 [ edit ] 

 Anahita 

 ( Iranian mythology ) An Iranian goddess associated with fertility , healing and wisdom .
