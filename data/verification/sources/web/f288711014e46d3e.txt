 This Old Median entry contains reconstructed terms and roots . As such, the term(s) in this entry are not directly attested , but are hypothesized to have existed based on comparative evidence.

 Old Median
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Proto-Iranian *cwitah , from Proto-Indo-Iranian *ćwitas , from Proto-Indo-Iranian *Háćwaytˢt ~ *Háćwitan + *-tas , from Proto-Indo-European *ḱwéyt-t ~ *ḱwit-ént , from *ḱweyt- ( “ to shine ” ) . Cognate with Avestan 𐬯𐬞𐬌𐬙𐬀 ( spita ) . [ 1 ] 

 Adjective
 [ edit ] 

 *spitah 

 white 

 pure 

 References
 [ edit ]
