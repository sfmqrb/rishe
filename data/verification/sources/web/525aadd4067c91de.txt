 Middle Irish
 [ edit ] 

 Alternative forms
 [ edit ] 

 Hériu 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Old Irish Ériu .

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 IPA ( key ) : ( earlier ) /ˈeːɾʲu/ , ( later ) /ˈeːɾʲə/ 

 Proper noun
 [ edit ] 

 Ériu   f 

 Ireland 
 c. 1000 , “ The Tale of Mac Da Thó's Pig ”, in Ernst Windisch , editor, Irische Texte , volume 1, published 1800 , section 1 : 

 Ailbe ainm in chon, ocus lan Heriu dia aurdarcus. Ailbe was the dog’s name, and Ireland was full of his fame. 

 Is í sein int ṡeised bruiden ro·boi i n‑Herind in tan sin [ … ] That is one of the six halls that were in Ireland at that time [ … ] (literally, “ That is the sixth hall that was [ … ] ”) 

 Declension
 [ edit ] 

 Accusative/dative singular: Érind , Érinn

 Etymology
 [ edit ]

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 IPA ( key ) : /ˈeː.ɾʲu/ 

 Proper noun
 [ edit ] 

 Ériu   f 

 Ireland (a country and island of Europe )
