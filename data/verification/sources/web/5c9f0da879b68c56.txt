 Alternative forms
 [ edit ] 

 گیسو ( gêsu ) 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian gys ( gēs , “ curl, locks ” ) , from Proto-Indo-Iranian *káyćas ~ *gáyćas , of unknown source, likely a borrowing from the the BMAC substrate . Compare Manichaean Middle Persian gyswg ( gēsūg ) . Akin to Old Armenian գէս ( gēs ) , an Iranian borrowing. Cognate with Avestan 𐬔𐬀𐬉𐬯𐬎 ( gaēsu , “ curls ” ) , Sanskrit केश ( keśa ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 گیس • ( gēs / gis ) ( plural گیس‌ها ( gēs-hā / gis-hâ ) ) 

 long hair 

 ringlet of hair, a forelock , a sidelock , curl 

 plait , braid
