 Persian
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology tree

 Proto-Indo-European *tews- ? 
 Middle Persian [script needed] ( th /⁠tah⁠/ ) 
 Persian ته 

 Inherited from Middle Persian [script needed] ( th /⁠tah⁠/ ) , possibly from Proto-Indo-European *tews- ( “ to be empty, drain ” ) , and thus cognate with تهی ( tohi , “ empty, hollow ” ) , Sanskrit तुच्छ्य ( tucchya , “ empty, vain ” ) . [ 1 ] [ 2 ] Compare also Persian تک ( tak , “ bottom ” ) .

 bottom

 final 
 ته خط ― tah -e xat ― end of the road
