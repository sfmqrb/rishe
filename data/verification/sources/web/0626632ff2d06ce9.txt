 Etymology 1
 [ edit ] 

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 IPA ( key ) : /nam.ma/ 

 Verb
 [ edit ] 

 نَمَّ • ( namma ) I ( non-past يَنُمُّ ( yanummu ) , verbal noun نَمّ ( namm ) ) 

 to gossip , to chatter , to palaver , to speak ill of 
 من نمّ لك نمّ عليك ― Whoever speaks ill to you of others, will speak ill of you ( Imam al-Shafi'i ) 

 to slander , to defame , to spread calumnies 

 to betray someone, to denounce someone, to inform against someone, to report someone (to another in authority )

 to be indicative of, to bespeak , to reveal , to show , to suggest [ with عَن ( ʕan ) or عَلَى ( ʕalā ) ]

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 نم • ( nam ) ( Tajik spelling нам ) 

 moisture , humidity 
 Synonyms: رطوبت ( rotubat ) , نمناکی ( namnâki ) 

 ( obsolete ) dew 
 Synonyms: شبنم ( šabnam ) , ژاله ( žâle ) 

 ( obsolete ) teardrop 
 Synonym: اشک ( ašk ) 
 c. 1390 , Shams-ud-Dīn Muḥammad Ḥāfiẓ , “Ghazal 417”, in دیوان حافظ [ The Divān of Ḥāfiẓ ]‎ [2] : جانا چه گویم شرح فراقت
چشمی و صد نم جانی و صد آه jānā či gūyam šarh-i firāqat
čašmē u sad nam jānē u sad āh My love, how shall I explain my separation from you?
One eye, and a hundred tears ! One life, and a hundred sighs! (Classical Persian transliteration)
