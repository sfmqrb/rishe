 صَرَّاف 
 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Occupational noun from the verb صَرَفَ ( ṣarafa , “ to pay out , spend , change money ” ) .

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 IPA ( key ) : /sˤar.raːf/ 

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 صَرَّاف • ( ṣarrāf )  m ( plural صَرَّافُون ( ṣarrāfūn ) , feminine صَرَّافَة ( ṣarrāfa ) ) 

 moneychanger 

 cashier 
 صَرَّاف آلِيّ ― ṣarrāf ʔāliyy ― a cash machine; an ATM 

 banker 

 grammarian

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 صراف • ( sarrāf / sarrâf ) ( plural صَرَّافَان ( sarrāfān / sarrâfân ) , Tajik spelling сарроф ) 

 money changer 

 ( historical ) banker , money dealer 
 c. 1520 , Selim I of the Ottoman Empire , edited by Benedek Péri, The Persian Dīvān of Yavuz Sulṭān Selīm , Budapest, Hungary: Research Centre for the Humanities, Eötvös Loránd Research Network, →ISBN , page 239 : صراف بسی وصف در و گهر خود گفت
خندید که بهتر نبود از گهر من sarrāf basē wasf-i durr u guhar-i xwad guft
xandīd ki bihtar nabūd az guhar-i man The banker made a long description of his pearls and jewels,
But he laughed that there was nothing better than my own jewel. (Classical Persian transliteration)
