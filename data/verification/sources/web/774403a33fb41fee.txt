 Persian

 Etymology 1
 [ edit ] 

 Inherited from Middle Persian [script needed] ( sh /⁠sē̆⁠/ ) , from Old Persian *çayaʰ , from Proto-Iranian *θráyah , from Proto-Indo-Iranian *tráyas , from Proto-Indo-European *tréyes .

 Indo European cognates include Avestan 𐬚𐬭𐬌- ( θri- ) , Northern Kurdish sê , Pashto درې ( dre ) , Sanskrit त्रि ( tri ) , Greek τρεις ( treis ) , Italian tre , English three , Latin trēs , French trois , Spanish tres , etc. False cognate of Korean 세 ( se ) .
