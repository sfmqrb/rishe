 Persian
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian [Book Pahlavi needed] ( ktk' /⁠kadag⁠/ , “ house ” ) , from Proto-Iranian *katákah ( “ house ” ) , diminutive of *kátah ( “ house; roof ” ) . Compare Armenian քաղաք ( kʻaġakʻ , “ city, town ” ) , Gurani کەَ ( ka , “ house ” ) , Pashto کلی ( kəlay , “ village ” ) , Northern Kurdish kedî ( “ domestic ” ) , Yaghnobi кат ( kat , “ house ” ) .
