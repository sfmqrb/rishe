 Persian
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology tree

 Proto-Indo-European *kʷeḱ- der. 
 Middle Persian [script needed] ( ʾkʾs /⁠āgāh⁠/ ) 
 Persian آگاه 

 Inherited from Middle Persian [Book Pahlavi needed] ( ʾkʾs /⁠āgāh⁠/ , “ aware, knowing ” ) , ultimately a derivative of Proto-Indo-European *kʷeḱ- ( “ to see; to look ” ) . Related to نگاه ( negâh ) and dialectal گاس ( gâs ) . Related to Old Armenian ակահ ( akah ) , an Iranian borrowing.
