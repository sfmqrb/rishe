 Arabic
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Borrowed from Hebrew שִׁמְעוֹן ( šimʕōn , “ Simon ” ) , originally a patronymic . Doublet of سِمْعَان ( simʕān ) , an older borrowing, and سِيمُون ( sīmūn ) , a borrowing from French.

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 IPA ( key ) : /ʃam.ʕuːn/ 

 Rhymes: -uːn 

 Proper noun
 [ edit ] 

 شَمْعُون • ( šamʕūn )  m or f by sense 

 a surname from Hebrew, equivalent to English Simon
