 Etymology

 Etymology tree

 Persian دادن 

 Inherited from Old Persian 𐎭𐎭𐎠𐎬𐎢𐎺 ( d-d-a-tu-u-v /⁠dadātuv⁠/ ) , from Proto-Indo-Iranian *dádaHti , from Proto-Indo-European *dédeh₃ti , reduplication of *deh₃- , or from *-ti . Cognates include Avestan 𐬛𐬀𐬛𐬁𐬌𐬙𐬌 ( dadā i ti ) , Polish dać , Ancient Greek δίδωμι ( dídōmi ) , Sanskrit ददाति ( dádāti ) , Latin dare , English data and Latin datum . [ 1 ]

 Verb
 [ edit ] 

 دادن • ( dādan / dâdan ) ( present stem ده ( dah ) , Tajik spelling додан ) 

 to give 
 کتابی را که به من داد گم کردی؟ ( more literary ) ketâb-i râ ke be man dâd gom kardi? 

 کتابی رو که بهم داد گم کردی؟ ( more colloquial ) ketâb-i ro ke behem dâd gom kardi? 

 کتابیو که بم داد گم کردی؟ ( more colloquial ) ketâb-iyo ke bem dâd gom kardi? Did you lose the book he gave me? 

 to marry off (i.e. one's child)

 ( vulgar ) to bottom in sex 
 Coordinate term: کردن
