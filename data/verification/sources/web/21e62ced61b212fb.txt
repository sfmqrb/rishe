 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian myhn' ( mēhan , “ home ” ) , from Proto-Iranian [Term?] . Cognate with Avestan 𐬨𐬀𐬉𐬚𐬀𐬥𐬀 ( maēθana , “ station, dwelling, abode, residence ” ) , Baluchi میتگ ( mētag , “ village, home ” ) .

 Pronunciation

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 میهن • ( mēhan / mihan ) ( Tajik spelling меҳан ) 

 homeland , native country

 motherland , fatherland , mother country
