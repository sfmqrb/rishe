 Russian
 [ edit ] 

 FWOTD – 31 August 2024 

 Etymology 1
 [ edit ] 

 Inherited from Old East Slavic бързъ ( bŭrzŭ ) , from Proto-Slavic *bъrzъ .

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 IPA ( key ) : [bɐrˈzoj] 

 Adjective
 [ edit ] 

 борзо́й • ( borzój ) 

 ( dated ) swift , fleet 
 борза́я лошадь ― borzája lošadʹ ― a swift horse 

 ( relational ) borzoi , greyhound 
 борзы́ми щенка́ми брать ― borzými ščenkámi bratʹ ― to take bribes in kind (literally, “ to take borzoi puppies ”) 
 борзо́й кобе́ль ― borzój kobélʹ ― a male borzoi 

 ( low colloquial ) cheeky , insolent
