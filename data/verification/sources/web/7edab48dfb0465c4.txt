 Persian
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Inherited from Middle Persian [Book Pahlavi needed] ( nmt' /⁠namad⁠/ , “ felt ” ) . The word was borrowed from ancient Iranian into other languages, e.g. Sanskrit नमत ( namata , “ felt ” ) , Hungarian nemez ( “ felt ” ) .
