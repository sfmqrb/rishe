 Noun

 وُضُوء • ( wuḍūʔ )  m 

 verbal noun of وَضُؤَ ( waḍuʔa )   ( form I ) 

 verbal noun of تَوَضَّأَ ( tawaḍḍaʔa )   ( form V ) 

 cleanness 

 ( by extension , Islam ) wudu , ablution
