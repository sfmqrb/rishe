 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 نُقْرَة • ( nuqra )  f ( plural نُقَر ( nuqar ) or نِقَار ( niqār ) ) 

 cavity , cleft , socket 

 ( archaic ) concentration of silver , high - quality silver 

 hollow of the backneck , condyloid fossa
