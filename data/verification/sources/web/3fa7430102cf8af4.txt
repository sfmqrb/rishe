 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Borrowed from Sanskrit पक्ष ( pakṣa ) .

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 ( Standard Hindi ) IPA ( key ) : /pəkʂ/ , [pɐkʃ] 

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 पक्ष • ( pakṣ )  m 

 faction , side , party 
 Synonym: तरफ़ ( taraf ) 
 पक्ष साथ-साथ लड़ रहे थे। pakṣ sāth-sāth laṛ rahe the. The factions were fighting amongst themselves. 

 aspect , quality 

 support , approval 

 ( Hinduism ) paksha , lunar fortnight 
 Synonym: पखवाड़ा ( pakhvāṛā )

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Probably from Proto-Indo-Iranian *pakšás ( “ side, flank ” ) , from Proto-Indo-European *peg- ( “ side, flank ” ) .

 Cognate with Ossetian фахс ( faxs , “ side ” ) and maybe Latvian paksis ( “ corner of a house ” ) . [ 1 ] 

 Alternatively, from a Proto-Indo-Iranian *paćšás and cognate with Prasuni [script needed] ( obuć , “ side ” ) , Waigali pāći ( “ side ” ) .

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 ( Vedic ) IPA ( key ) : /pɐk.ʂɐ́/ 

 ( Classical Sanskrit ) IPA ( key ) : /pɐk.ʂɐ/ 

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 प॒क्ष • ( pakṣá ) stem ,  m 

 wing , pinion 

 shoulder 

 the flank or side or the half of anything

 the side or wing of a building

 the wing or flank of an army

 the half of a lunar month

 side , party , faction 

 multitude , number , troop , set , class of beings

 partisan , adherent , follower , friend 

 side i.e. position , place , stead 

 one of two cases or one side of an argument, an alternative

 a point or matter under discussion, a thesis, a particular theory, a position advanced or an argument to be maintained

 action , lawsuit 

 ( logic ) the proposition to be proved in a syllogism

 any supposition or view, motion, idea, opinion

 ( algebra ) a primary division or the side of an equation in a primary division
