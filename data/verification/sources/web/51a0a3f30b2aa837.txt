 Verb
 [ edit ] 

 طَرَحَ • ( ṭaraḥa ) I ( non-past يَطْرَحُ ( yaṭraḥu ) , verbal noun طَرْح ( ṭarḥ ) ) 

 to throw , to cast , to fling , to toss 

 to discard (an old garment), to throw away , to dump 

 to remove , to expel , to reject , to disown , to repudiate 

 to present , to put forward , to bring forward (a proposal, an idea, evidence, an argument, a suggestion)

 to introduce (a product, an idea)

 to submit (a proposal, an idea)

 to pose , to raise , to ask (a question)

 to bring up (a topic of discussion)

 to raise , to present (an issue, a problem)

 to teach 

 to cede , to surrender , to yield 

 to miscarry 

 to deduct , to discount , to subtract (five from ten)

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 طرح • ( tarh or tarah or tarā )  f ( Hindi spelling तरह ) 

 method , manner , mode 
 یہ کس طرح سے بنا ہے؟ ― ye kis tarah se banā hai? ― By what method is this made? 
 Synonyms: ڈھنگ ( ḍhaṅg ) , انداز ( andāz ) , طریقہ ( tarīqā ) 
 way ( the manner or identified or described ) 
 اِس طرح کرو۔ ― is tarah karo. ― Do it this way . 
 میں کمرا اپنی طرح سجاؤں گی۔ ma͠i kamrā apnī tarah sajāūṅgī. I will decorate the room my way . 

 اسے صحیح طرح سمجھانا ہے۔ use sahīh tarah samjhānā hai. You have / one has to persuade him/her the right way . 

 type , kind 
 یہ کس طرح کا سوال ہے؟ ye kis tarah kā savāl hai? What type of question is this? 

 Synonym: قسم ( qism ) 

 condition 
 Synonyms: حال ( hāl ) , حالت ( hālat )
