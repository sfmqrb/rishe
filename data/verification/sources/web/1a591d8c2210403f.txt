 See also: Reconstruction:Proto-Germanic/banō 

 This Proto-Germanic entry contains reconstructed terms and roots . As such, the term(s) in this entry are not directly attested , but are hypothesized to have existed based on comparative evidence.

 Proto-Germanic
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Probably from Proto-Indo-European *gʷʰon-ō , an individualizing n-stem derivative of *gʷʰonós ( “ one who strikes down ” ) , [ 1 ] from the root *gʷʰen- ( “ to strike, kill ” ) , though the sound change required from *gʷʰ > *b is controversial (but appears to be regular in word-initial position). [ 2 ] 

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 IPA ( key ) : /ˈbɑ.nɔːː/ 

 Rhymes: -ɑ.nɔːː
