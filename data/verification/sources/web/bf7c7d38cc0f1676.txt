 Etymology 1
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian ʾʾhng ( āhang , “ pull, draught; melody ” ) , derived from آهیختن ( âhixtan , “ to pull, draw out ” ) .

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ]

 ( Dari, formal ) IPA ( key ) : [ʔɑː.ɦäŋɡ]
