 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 شبستان • ( šabistān / šabestân ) ( Tajik spelling шабистон ) 

 harem 
 Synonym: حرمسرای ( haramsarây ) 

 bedroom (of kings and others who reside at their palace)

 an area of the mosque which is used at night, typically by Dervishes and Sufis, who also spend the night there

 Synonyms: خواب گاہ ( xwāb gāh ) , آرام گاہ ( ārām gāh )
