 Persian
 [ edit ] 

 Persian Wikipedia has an article on: شیدا 

 Wikipedia fa 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Borrowed from Aramaic שידא ( šēḏā ) or Classical Syriac ܫܐܕܐ ( šēḏā , “ demon ” ) , from Akkadian 𒀭𒆘 ( šēdum , “ a protective deity ” ) .

 For the semantic sense of "mad, crazy; enamored, infatuated", compare دیوانه ( dēwāna, dīwāna , “ mad, fan, patron ” ) . Both stemming from etymological meanings of "demonic" and "crazy", but being poetically used for "love" and "infatuation", especially in Sufi mysticism . 

 Also for the semantic shift, compare Arabic مجنون ( majnūn , “ mad, insane; enamored ” ) , which is from جن ( jinn , “ demon ” ) .

 شیدا • ( šaydā / šeydâ ) ( comparative شیداتَر , superlative شیداتَرین , Tajik spelling шайдо ) ( Islam , chiefly Sufism ) 

 ( archaic , poetic , literary ) mad , insane 

 ( poetic ) enamored , enamoured

 Urdu
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Borrowed from Classical Persian شَیدَا ( šaydā , “ mad, insane; enamored ” ) , from Aramaic שידא ( šēḏā ) or Classical Syriac ܫܐܕܐ ( šēḏā , “ demon ” ) , from Akkadian 𒀭𒆘 ( šēdum , “ a protective deity ” ) . 

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 ( Standard Urdu ) IPA ( key ) : /ʃɛː.d̪ɑː/ 

 Adjective
 [ edit ] 

 شَیدَا • ( śaidā ) ( Hindi spelling शैदा ) ( Islam , chiefly Sufism ) 

 enamored , infatuated ; deeply in love 

 mad , crazy 

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 شَیدَا • ( śaidā )  m or f ( Hindi spelling शैदा ) ( Islam , chiefly Sufism ) 

 lover 
 میری شیدا نے مجھے ہونٹوں پر بوسہ دیا. merī śaidā ne mujhe honṭõ par bosa diyā. My lover kissed me on the lips. 

 madman 

 Derived terms
 [ edit ] 

 شَیدَائِی ( śaidāī , “ insanity; infatuation ” ) 

 والَہ و شَیدَا ( vāla-o-śaidā , “ head over heels ” )
