 ( taxonomy ) Synonym of tribe .

Xeruleae and Myceneae.

 Latin

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 ( Classical Latin ) IPA ( key ) : [ˈtrɪ.bʊs] 

 ( modern Italianate Ecclesiastical ) IPA ( key ) : [ˈtriː.bus] 

 Etymology 1
 [ edit ] 

 An old compound from the stem of trēs ( “ three ” ) , cognate with Umbrian 𐌕𐌓𐌉𐌚𐌖 ( trifu ) . Either from: [ 1 ] [ 2 ] 

 Proto-Italic *triðus if from Proto-Indo-European *dʰeh₁- ( “ to put, place, set ” ) , thus Proto-Indo-European *tri-dʰh₁-o- ( “ rendered in three, tripartite ” ) but changed to a u -stem; cf. with Skt. multiplicatives in -dhā such as Sanskrit त्रिधा ( tridhā , “ threefold ” ) , and with other derivatives such as Lithuanian iñdas ( “ dishware ” ) < *h₁n̥-dʰh₁-o- . This would be evidence to the development of PIt. *þ before vocalic *u as after it;

 Proto-Italic *triβus if from Proto-Indo-European *bʰuH- ( “ to grow, become, come into being, appear ” ) ; in that case, the u -suffix was originally part of the root.

 Originally referring to three divisions of the (Roman) people. Cognate to Umbrian trifu ( “ tribe, people ” ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 tribus   f ( genitive tribūs ) ; fourth declension 

 One of the three original tribes of Rome: Ramnes , Tities , Luceres .

 A division of the Roman people.

 A tribe .
 Synonyms: gēns , prōgeniēs 

 The mob , the lower classes .
