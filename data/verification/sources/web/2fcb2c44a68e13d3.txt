 Etymology 1
 [ edit ] 

 Inherited from Middle Persian 𐭠𐭩𐭥𐭯 ( ʾywp /⁠ayāb⁠/ ) / 𐫀𐫏𐫀𐫁 ( ʾyʾb /⁠ayāb⁠/ ) . Cognate with Parthian 𐫀𐫃𐫀𐫖 ( ʾgʾm /⁠āgām⁠/ ) . Further etymology unclear; not found in Old Persian.

 c. 1390 , Hafez , “Ghazal 12”, in دیوان حافظ [ The Divān of Hafez ]‎ [2] : عزم دیدار تو دارد جانِ بر لب آمده
باز گردد یا برآید؟ چیست فرمان شما؟ azm-i dîdâr-i tu dârad jân-i bar lab âmada
bâz gardad yâ bar-âyad? čî-st farmân-i šumâ? My soul, resolved to see you, has come to the edge;
Shall it go back, or come forth? Which do you command? (romanization in Classical Persian)
