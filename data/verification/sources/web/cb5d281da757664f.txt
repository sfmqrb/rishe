 Persian لشکر 

 Inherited from Middle Persian lškl , from Proto-Indo-Iranian *Hrákšati + Proto-Iranian *karah . Compare Sanskrit रक्षति ( rakṣati ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 لشکر • ( laškar ) ( plural لشکرها ( laškar-hā / laškar-hâ ) , Tajik spelling лашкар ) 

 army 
 Synonym: سپاه ( sepâh ) 
 c. 1518 , Selīm I , Dīvān of Sultan Selīm I : لشکر از تخت استانبول سوی ایران تاختم
سرخسر را غرقهٔ خون ملامت ساختم laškar az taxt-i istânbûl sôy-i êrân tâxtam
surxsar râ ġarqa-yi xûn-i malâmat sâxtam From Istanbul’s throne towards Iran, I made an army gallop out;
I made the Qizilbash drown in the blood of reproach. (Classical Persian transcription) 

 ( military ) division

 Pronunciation

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 لَشْکَر • ( laśkar )  m ( Hindi spelling लश्कर ) 

 army , armed force , military 
 Synonyms: فوج ( foj ) , آرمی ( ārmī ) 

 encampment , cantonment 
 Synonym: چھاؤنی ( chāonī ) 

 crew , company ( of a ship )
