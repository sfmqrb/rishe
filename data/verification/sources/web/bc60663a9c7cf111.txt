 Alternative forms
 [ edit ] 

 χῑ́λι’ ( khī́li’ ) — contracted 

 χείλιοι ( kheílioi ) — Ionic 

 χήλιοι ( khḗlioi ) , χέλιοι ( khélioi ) — Laconian 

 χέλλιοι ( khéllioi ) — Lesbian , Thessalian 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Proto-Hellenic *kʰehliyoi , from Proto-Indo-European *ǵʰesliyoy , from *ǵʰes- , whence χείρ ( kheír ) ), as if meaning “full hand”. Cognates include Sanskrit सहस्र ( sahásra ) and Latin mīlle . [ 1 ]

 Numeral
 [ edit ] 

 χῑ́λῐοι • ( khī́lĭoi ) ( feminine χῑ́λῐαι , neuter χῑ́λῐα ) 

 thousand 
 c. 177 CE , Aulus Gellius , Noctes Atticae (in Latin) 1.16.8 : “Mille” enim non pro eo ponitur, quod Graece χίλιοι dicitur, sed quod χιλιάς et sicuti una χιλιὰς et duae χιλιάδες, ita “unum mille” et “duo milia” certa atque directa ratione dicitur. Because mille is not put for what is said χίλιοι in Greek but for χιλιάς and is like one χιλιὰς and two χιλιάδες , thus unum mille and duo milia are said with certain and straight mind. 

 c. 800 BCE – 600 BCE , Homer , Iliad 7.470–471 : χωρὶς δ’ Ἀτρεΐδῃς Ἀγαμέμνονι καὶ Μενελάῳ
δῶκεν Ἰησονίδης ἀγέμεν μέθυ χίλια μέτρα. khōrìs d’ Atreḯdēis Agamémnoni kaì Meneláōi
dôken Iēsonídēs agémen méthu khília métra. (please add an English translation of this quotation)
