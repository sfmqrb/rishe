 Avestan
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Inherited from Proto-Iranian *mazdáH , from Proto-Indo-Iranian *mazdʰáH , from Proto-Indo-European *mn̥s-dʰéh₂ , from *men(s)dʰh₁- ( “ to pay attention, wisdom ” ) . Cognate with Sanskrit मेधा ( medhā́ ) . [ 1 ] 

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 𐬨𐬀𐬰𐬛𐬁 • ( mazdā )  f 

 ( Zoroastrianism ) Mazda 

 wisdom
