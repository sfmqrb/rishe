 Avestan
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Proto-Iranian *Áryah , from Proto-Indo-Iranian *Áryas . Cognate to Old Persian 𐎠𐎼𐎡𐎹 ( a-r-i-y /⁠Aryaʰ⁠/ ) , Sanskrit आर्य ( ārya ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 𐬀𐬌𐬭𐬌𐬌𐬋 • ( a i riiō ) 

 ethno-linguistic autonym , i.e. Iranian .
