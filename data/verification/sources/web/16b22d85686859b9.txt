 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From earlier اَنَاهِید ( anāhīd ) , from Middle Persian ʾnʾhyt' ( anāhīd ) . Cognate with Northern Kurdish Nehîda , Nehî and Central Kurdish ناهیە ( nahye ) , ناهێ ( nahê ) .
