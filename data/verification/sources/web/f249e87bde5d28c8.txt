 This Proto-Indo-European entry contains reconstructed terms and roots . As such, the term(s) in this entry are not directly attested , but are hypothesized to have existed based on comparative evidence.

 Proto-Indo-European
 [ edit ] 

 Root
 [ edit ] 

 *men- ( perfective ) [ 1 ] [ 2 ] 

 to think , mind 
 Synonyms: *teng- , *h₂reh₁- 

 spiritual activity 

 Extensions
 [ edit ] 

 *mneh₂- 

 *men(s)dʰh₁- ? ( probably neo-root of einzelsprachlich origin, re-analyzed from *mn̥(s)- *dʰeh₁- )
