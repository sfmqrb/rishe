 Persian
 [ edit ] 

 Alternative forms
 [ edit ] 

 شیو ( šiv ) 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Proto-Iranian [Term?] .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 شیب • ( šêb, šib ) 

 slope , declivity , tilt , descend 

 bottom , base , lower part
 fundament , bottom of human anatomy

 base of a shield 

 wet ground made unequal and rugged by man tramping 

 Adjective
 [ edit ] 

 شیب • ( šêb, šib ) 

 not the full shilling , doolally , barmy 

 hasty , precipitate 
 Synonyms: تیب ( têb ) , شتابان ( šitābān ) , شتاب‌زده ( šitābzada ) , عجولانه ( ʼajulāna )
