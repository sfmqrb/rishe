 This Proto-Semitic entry contains reconstructed terms and roots . As such, the term(s) in this entry are not directly attested , but are hypothesized to have existed based on comparative evidence.

 Proto-Semitic
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Often compared to Egyptian bꜣk and to Cushitic forms such as Afar gulúb .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 *birk-   m or f 

 knee 

 Reconstruction notes
 [ edit ] 

 In Common Semitic, there was a metathetic root formed, which yielded *ruk(u)b-at- ( “ knee ” ) (compare Arabic رُكْبَة ( rukba ) ), both of which had reflexes preserved in Arabic, Aramaic , and Ge'ez.
