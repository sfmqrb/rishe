 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian ʾpkntn' ( abgandan , “ to throw ” ) 𐭫𐭬𐭩𐭲𐭭𐭲𐭭𐭩 ( lmytntny ) , is a compound verb, so the elements are *af- from Proto-Iranian *Hápa ( “ off, away, from ” ) or Proto-Indo-European *upó ( “ under, below, towards ” ) + *kan- from Proto-Iranian *kánHati ( “ to dig ” ) .
