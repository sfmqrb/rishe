 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology tree

 Proto-Indo-European *yeh₂- 

 Proto-Indo-European *-los 

 Proto-Indo-European *yéh₂los 
 Ancient Greek ζῆλος 

 From Proto-Indo-European *yeh₂- ( “ to go, travel, search, inquire ” ) . Related to Ζητήρ ( Zētḗr ) . [ 1 ]

 ( 10 th CE Medieval ) IPA ( key ) : /ˈzi.los/

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 ζῆλος • ( zêlos )  m ( genitive ζήλου ) ; second declension 

 eager rivalry , zealous imitation , emulation , a noble passion 
 Synonym: φθόνος ( phthónos )

 384 BCE – 322 BCE , Aristotle , Rhetoric 2.11.1
