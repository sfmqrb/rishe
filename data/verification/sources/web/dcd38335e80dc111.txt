 Etymology 2
 [ edit ] 

 Tool noun of عَصَرَ ( ʕaṣara , “ to press, squeeze, wring ” ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 عَصَّارَة • ( ʕaṣṣāra )  f ( plural عَصَّارَات ( ʕaṣṣārāt ) ) 

 machine that is devised to wring, particularly saps – press , wringer , juicer
