 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 گلاب • ( gulāb / golâb ) ( Tajik spelling гулоб ) 

 rosewater 
 c. 1390 , Shams-ud-Dīn Muḥammad Ḥāfiẓ , “Ghazal 421”, in دیوان حافظ [ The Divān of Ḥāfiẓ ]‎ [1] : عروس بخت در آن حجله با هزاران ناز
شکسته کسمه و بر برگ گل گلاب زده arūs-i baxt dar ān hajla bā hazārān nāz
šikasta kasma u bar barg-i gul gulāb zada With a thousandfold coquetry, the bride of fortune in that bridal chamber
Has scattered the curls over her face and spilled rosewater [metaphor for blushing] on the rose leaf [metaphor for the delicate face]. (Classical Persian transliteration) 

 ( Dari ) a kind of rose
