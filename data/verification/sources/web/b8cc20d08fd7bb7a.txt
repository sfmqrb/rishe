 Persian

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Akin to Proto-Georgian-Zan *bal- ( “ wild cherry ” ) . The nature of the relationship and the ultimate origin are unknown.

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 بالو • ( bâlu ) 

 cherry 
 Synonym: گیلاس ( gilâs )
