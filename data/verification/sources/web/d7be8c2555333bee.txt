 This Proto-Indo-European entry contains reconstructed terms and roots . As such, the term(s) in this entry are not directly attested , but are hypothesized to have existed based on comparative evidence.

 Proto-Indo-European
 [ edit ] 

 Root
 [ edit ] 

 *h₁wegʷʰ- [ 1 ] [ 2 ] 

 to promise , to vow 

 to praise 

 Reconstruction notes
 [ edit ] 

 Although these terms are usually unified under a singular root, the Encyclopedia of Indo-European Culture suggests that they may be split into two separate roots *h₁ewgʷʰ- and *(H)wegʷʰ- . If this division is accepted, then theoretically Hittite [script needed] ( ḫuek- ) could be connected to the latter root if it was reconstructed as *h₂wegʷʰ- or *h₃wegʷʰ- . [ 3 ] However, Kloekhorst prefers to reconstruct a singular root *h₁wegʷʰ- and treats the Hittite form as unrelated. [ 4 ] 

 Alternative reconstructions
 [ edit ] 

 *h₁ewgʷʰ- [ 3 ]
