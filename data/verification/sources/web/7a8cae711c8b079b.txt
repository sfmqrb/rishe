 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Inherited from Ottoman Turkish طولمق ( “ to fill, become full; swell ” ) , from Proto-Turkic *tōl- ( “ to be full ” ) .

 Verb
 [ edit ] 

 dolmak ( third-person singular simple present dolar ) 

 ( intransitive ) to fill up
