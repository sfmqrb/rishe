 Verb

 مَسَكَ • ( masaka ) I ( non-past يَمْسُكُ ( yamsuku ) or يَمْسِكُ ( yamsiku ) , verbal noun مَسْك ( mask ) ) 

 to grab , to grasp , to clutch , to clasp , to seize , to take hold 

 to hold 

 to stick , to cling , to adhere , to hang on

 Etymology 3
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian [script needed] ( mwšk' /⁠mušk⁠/ , “ musk ” ) ( Persian مشک ( mošk , “ musk ” ) ), from Sanskrit मुष्क ( muṣka , “ testicle, scrotum ” ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 مِسْك • ( misk )  m or f 

 musk 
 609–632 CE , Qur'an , 83:25-26 : يُسۡقَوۡنَ مِن رَّحِيقٍ مَّخۡتُومٍ ۝ خِتَـٰمُهُۥ مِسْكٌۭ ۚ [ … ] They will be given to drink of full-flavoured sealed wine ۝ and its seal is of musk.
