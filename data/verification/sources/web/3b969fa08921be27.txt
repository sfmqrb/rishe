 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian [script needed] ( kwlʾp̄ /⁠kulāf⁠/ , “ cap, bonnet ” ) . Compare Middle Armenian սաքուլայ ( sakʻulay ) and Northern Luri کلۏ ( kelö ) .

 This etymology is missing or incomplete. Please add to it, or discuss it at the Etymology scriptorium . 
Particularly: “Is this related to کل ( kal , “ bald ” ) somehow? Doesn't seem to be mentioned in Edelman.”

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 کلاه • ( kulāh / kolâh ) ( plural کلاه‌ها ( kulāh-hā / kolâh-hâ ) , Tajik spelling кулоҳ ) 

 hat 

 ( archaic ) crown 
 10th Century CE , Ferdowsi , Shahnameh 
 به ایوان خرامید و بنشست شاد
 کلاه کیانی به سر بر نهاد be ivân xarâmid o benšast šâd
 kolâh e kiyâni bar sar nehâd (He) gingerly approached and delightedly acceded to the throne
and put on the kingly crown 

 Synonyms: تاج , افسر 

 cap 

 bonnet
