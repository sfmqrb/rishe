 Persian

 Etymology 1
 [ edit ] 

 ارگ بم 
 From Middle Persian [script needed] ( arg ) , from Proto-Indo-European *h₂erk- . Cognates include Old Armenian արգել ( argel , “ obstacle ” ) and Ancient Greek ἀρκέω ( arkéō ) , Latin arx ( “ citadel ” ) .
