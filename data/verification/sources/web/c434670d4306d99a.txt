 Ancient Greek
 [ edit ] 

 Alternative forms
 [ edit ] 

 δᾰρχμή ( dărkhmḗ ) — Epic 

 δρᾰχμᾱ́ ( drăkhmā́ ) — Doric , Cretan 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Typically derived as a verbal noun (“holding”) of δρᾰ́σσομαι ( drắssomai , “ to hold, to seize ” ) . Beekes, however, argues that the existence of the variants δαρχμά ( darkhmá ) and δαρχνά ( darkhná ) means δραχ- ( drakh- ) and δαρχ- ( darkh- ) do not continue the zero-grade of Proto-Hellenic *dr̥kʰ- and instead derive from an otherwise unknown Pre-Greek substrate term. [ 1 ]
