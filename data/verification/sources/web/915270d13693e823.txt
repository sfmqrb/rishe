 Avestan
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Proto-Iranian *paridayjah . Equivalent to 𐬞𐬀𐬌𐬭𐬌 ( pa i ri , “ around ” ) + 𐬛𐬀𐬉𐬰𐬀 ( daēza , “ wall ” ) . Cognate with Persian فردوس ( ferdaws , “ paradise, heaven ” ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 𐬞𐬀𐬌𐬭𐬌 ⸱ 𐬛𐬀𐬉𐬰𐬀 • ( pa i ri.daēza )  m 

 walled enclosure , encompassing
