 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Traditionally derived from चन्द् ( cand , “ to shine ” ) , but probably a Dravidian borrowing: compare Tamil சாந்து ( cāntu , “ Santalum tree ” ) , சாந்தம் ( cāntam , “ sandalwood ” ) , Malayalam ചാന്ത് ( cāntŭ , “ sandal paste ” ) .
