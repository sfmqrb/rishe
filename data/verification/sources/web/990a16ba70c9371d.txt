 Middle Persian dywʾl 

 From Old Persian 𐎮𐎡𐎭𐎠 ( di-i-d-a /⁠didā-⁠/ , “ wall, fortress ” ) + *-wāra . [ 1 ] 

 The first part is from Proto-Iranian *dáyjah , from Proto-Indo-European *dʰeyǵʰ- ( “ to form, knead ” ) , [ 2 ] and the second part is from Proto-Iranian *wāra- , ultimately from Proto-Indo-European *wer- ( “ to watch, keep guard, cover ” ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 dywʾl • ( dēwār ) 

 wall
