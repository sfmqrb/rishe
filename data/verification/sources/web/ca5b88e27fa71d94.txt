 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Proto-Indo-European *(s)reg- ( “ to dye ” ) . Cognate with Proto-Iranian *ra(n)ǰ- ( “ to colour, dye ” ) , whence Persian رخش ( raxš , “ red ” ) , Khwarezmian [script needed] ( rxtk /⁠raxtak⁠/ , “ red ” ) , Mazanderani رش ( raš , “ red (of cattle) ” ) , Northern Kurdish reş ( “ black ” ) and Sogdian [script needed] ( rɣš- /⁠raxš⁠/ , “ Rakhsh ” ) , as well as Ancient Greek ῥέζω ( rhézō , “ to dye, paint ” ) .
