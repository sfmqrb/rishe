 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Borrowed from Classical Persian پنیر ( panir , “ cheese ” ) , from Middle Persian pnyl ( panīr ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 پنیر • ( penir ) ( definite accusative پنیری ( peniri ) , plural پنیرلر ( penirler ) ) 

 cheese , a dairy product made from curdled milk 
 Synonym: جبن ( cübn )
