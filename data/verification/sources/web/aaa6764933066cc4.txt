 Hindi

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 ( Standard Hindi ) IPA ( key ) : /pəʈ/ , [pɐʈ] 

 Etymology 1
 [ edit ] 

 Uncertain, maybe connected with पट ( paṭ , “ flat surface ” ) .

 Adjective
 [ edit ] 

 पट • ( paṭ ) ( indeclinable , Urdu spelling پٹ ) 

 lying flat or on the face

 overturned 

 falling flat , ineffective ( as the blow of a sword ) 

 flat , bare ( terrain ) , uncultivated land , deserted ( a village ) 

 slack , inactive ( as trade ) 

 ruined 

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 पट • ( paṭ )  ? ( Urdu spelling پٹ ) 

 tails ( of a coin ) 
 Antonym: चित ( cit )

 Somehow from Sanskrit पट् ( paṭ , “ sudden noise ” ) , whence also पटकना ( paṭaknā , “ to dash against ” ) and पटाका ( paṭākā , “ firecracker ” ) .

 sound of falling ( as of rain ) or of beating 

 ( by extension ) a beating

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Of uncertain origin.

 Usually derived from Proto-Indo-Aryan *paḷṭas , undergoing Fortunatov's law from Proto-Indo-Iranian *paltas (whence Persian پرده ( parde , “ curtain, veil ” ) ), from Proto-Indo-European *pol-to-s , from *pel- ( “ to cover, wrap; hide, cloth ” ) . This would make the term cognate with Proto-Slavic *poltьno ( “ linen, cloth ” ) , Ancient Greek πέλτη ( péltē , “ a small leather shield ” ) . [ 1 ] 

 Alternatively, borrowed from a presumably Southeast Asian substrate . [ 2 ] 

 Likely related to पटल ( paṭala , “ roof ” ) and पट्ट ( paṭṭa , “ slab, cloth ” ) .
