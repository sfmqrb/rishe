 Etymology 1
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian [script needed] ( kōδ ) . Related to Sanskrit कूट ( kūṭa , “ heap, multitude ” ) , Romani kidao ( “ to heap ” ) , Old Armenian loanword կոյտ ( koyt , “ heap, mass, pile ” ) and possibly Classical Syriac ܩܘܛܝ ( qawṭī , “ to heap ” ) .
