 Etymology 2
 [ edit ] 

 from Proto-Iranian *ǰántī , from Proto-Indo-Iranian *ǰʰánti , from Proto-Indo-European *gʷʰénti ( “ to slay , strike ” ) . Cognate with Persian زدن ( zadan ) .

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 IPA ( key ) : /d͡ʒə.nəɡ/ [d͡ʒə.nəɡ] 

 Verb
 [ edit ] 

 جنگ • ( janag ) ( past stem جت ) 

 to hit 
 آئیءَ منا پہ سرگ جت! Áí'a maná pa sarag jat ! He hit me on the head ! 

 a light verb that derives verbs from nouns and adjectives
