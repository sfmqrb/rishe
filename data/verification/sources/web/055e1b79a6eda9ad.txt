 Persian
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian 𐭭𐭩𐭰𐭪𐭩 ( nycky ) / nyck' ( /⁠nēzag⁠/ ) , from Old Persian *naizakam , borrowed from Old Median *naizakam , from Proto-Iranian *náyjakam , derived from Proto-Iranian *náyjam , from Proto-Indo-Iranian *náyȷ́žʰam . Compare Avestan 𐬥𐬀𐬉𐬰𐬀 ( naēza , “ tip, especially of a needle ” ) and borrowings from Iranian: Classical Syriac ܢܝܙܟܐ ( nayzkāʾ ) , Old Armenian նիզակ ( nizak ) , and Arabic نَيْزَك ( nayzak ) .
