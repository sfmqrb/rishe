 This Proto-Indo-European entry contains reconstructed terms and roots . As such, the term(s) in this entry are not directly attested , but are hypothesized to have existed based on comparative evidence.

 Proto-Indo-European
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Perhaps an extension of *ten- . [ 1 ] 

 Root
 [ edit ] 

 *temp- [ 2 ] [ 3 ] 

 to stretch , exert an effort on

 Reconstruction notes
 [ edit ] 

 According to the EIEC, if the root is connected with tempus ( “ time ” ) , then it may indicate that the Proto-Indo-European people viewed time as a linear construct. [ 1 ]
