 Arabic

 Etymology 1.1
 [ edit ] 

 Root 

 ء ج ر ( ʔ j r ) 

 14 terms 

 Compare Hebrew אגר ( agár , “ to gather, to harvest ” ) .

 Verb
 [ edit ] 

 أَجَرَ • ( ʔajara ) I ( non-past يَأْجُرُ ( yaʔjuru ) or يَأْجِرُ ( yaʔjiru ) , verbal noun أَجْر ( ʔajr ) or إِجَارَة ( ʔijāra ) ) 

 to reward , to pay , to compensate 

 to hire , to rent out 

 to serve for hire 

 to let ( someone, e. g. a slave ) for hire
