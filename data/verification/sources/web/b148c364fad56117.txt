 Ottoman Turkish
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Borrowed from Classical Persian رَوْغَن ( rawġan ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 روغن • ( revgan or rugan ) ( definite accusative روغنی ( revganı, ruganı ) , plural روغنلر ( revganlar, ruganlar ) ) 

 oil , grease , ointment , pomade , any greasy substance 
 Synonym: یاغ ( yağ ) 

 butter , a fatty foodstuff made by churning the cream of milk 
 Synonym: یاغ ( yağ ) 

 varnish , a paint with a solvent that evaporates to leave a film 
 Synonym: ورنیك ( vernik )

 [ edit ] 

 روغن آردیج ( rugan-ı ardıç , “ juniper oil ” ) 
 روغن زیت ( rugan-ı zeyt , “ olive oil ” ) 
 روغن ساده ( rugan sade , “ melted butter ” ) 
 روغن سورمك ( rugan sürmek , “ to apply varnish ” ) 
 روغن نفت ( rugan-ı neft , “ turpentine ” ) 
 روغنفروش ( ruganfuruş , “ seller of oil or butter ” ) 
 روغنلاتمق ( ruganlatmak , “ to make or let be varnished ” ) 
 روغنلامق ( ruganlamak , “ to varnish ” ) 
 روغنلو ( ruganlı , “ varnished ” ) 
 روغنی ( rugani , “ oily, syrupy ” )

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian [Book Pahlavi needed] ( lwkn' /⁠rōγn⁠/ , “ oil; butter ” ) , from Proto-Iranian *ráwgnah , from Proto-Indo-Iranian *ráwgʰnas , perhaps from Proto-Indo-European *réwgʰ-no-s ( “ cream ” ) , from *rewgʰ- +‎ *-nos , from *rew- ( “ to tear, dig, churn ” ) . Cognate with Mazanderani راغون ( rağun , “ oil ” ) , Central Kurdish ڕۆن ( ron , “ oil ” ) , Shughni рӯған ( rūɣan , “ butter, grease ” ) , Pashto روغن ( roğán ) , Zazaki ruwen .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 Dari

 روغن , تیل 

 Iranian Persian

 روغن 

 Tajik

 равған 

 روغن • ( rōġan, rawġan / rowġan ) ( plural روغن‌ها ( rōġan-hā, rawġan-hā / rowġan-hâ ) , Tajik spelling равған ) 

 oil (liquid fat); ( of mammals ) butter ; ghee 
 روغن آرگان ― rowġan -e ârgân ― argan oil 
 روغن زیتون ― rowġan -e zeytun ― olive oil 
 روغن نخل ― rowġan -e naxl ― palm oil 
 روغن گاو ― rowġan -e gâv ― clarified butter (literally, “ cow oil ”)

 روغن زن ( rowġan zan , “ oil press machine ” ) 
 روغن فروش ( rowġan foruš , “ oil seller ” ) 
 روغندان ( rowġan-dân , “ oil container, oilcan ” )

 Urdu
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Borrowed from Classical Persian رَوْغَن ( rawġan ) .

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 ( Standard Urdu ) IPA ( key ) : /ɾɔː.ɣən/ , /ɾoː.ɣən/ 

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 رَوغَن • ( rauġan )  m ( Hindi spelling रोग़न ) 

 oil ( liquid fat ) 

 butter ; ghee 

 the oily or fatty gravy of stew 

 varnish , polish ; sleekness , glossiness 

 Derived terms
 [ edit ] 

 رَوغنِی ( rauġnī , “ oily ” ) 
 رَوغَنِ اَرَنْڈ ( rauġan-i aranḍ , “ castor oil ” ) 
 رَوغَنِ زَرْد ( rauġan-i zard , “ ghee ” ) 
 رَوغَنِ زَیتُون ( rauġan-i zaitūn , “ olive oil ” ) 
 رَوغَنِ نارِیَل ( rauġan-i nāriyal , “ coconut oil ” ) 
 رَوغَنی روٹی ( rauġanī roṭī , “ buttered roti ” )
