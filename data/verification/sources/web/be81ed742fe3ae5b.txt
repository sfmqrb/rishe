 Gawar-Bati
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Borrowed from Pashto اسفنج or Urdu اسفنج , from Persian اسفنج , Arabic إِسْفَنْج ( ʔisfanj ) , ultimately Ancient Greek σπόγγος ( spóngos ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 اسفنج ( isfanj ) 

 sponge 

 foam
