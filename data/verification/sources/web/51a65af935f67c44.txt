 Proper noun
 [ edit ] 

 नील • ( nīl )  m 

 ( Hinduism ) Nila , a vanara , brother of Nala and son of Agni in Ramayana 
 रामायण में नल और नील ने समुद्र पर सेतु का निर्माण किया था। rāmāyaṇ mẽ nal aur nīl ne samudra par setu kā nirmāṇ kiyā thā. In Ramayana, Nala and Neela built the bridge over the ocean. 

 a male given name , Neel , from Sanskrit

 Etymology 2
 [ edit ] 

 Borrowed from Classical Persian نیل ( nīl ) , from Arabic النِيل ( an-nīl ) , from Ancient Greek Νεῖλος ( Neîlos ) .

 Proper noun
 [ edit ] 

 नील • ( nīl )  m ( Urdu spelling نیل ) 

 Nile (a large river in Africa , usually considered to be the longest river in the world; its basin covers Burundi , Rwanda , Tanzania , Kenya , Uganda , the Democratic Republic of the Congo , Ethiopia , Eritrea , South Sudan , Sudan , and Egypt , flowing north through Khartoum and Cairo into the Mediterranean Sea )

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Possibly from Proto-Indo-European *neyH- ( “ to be excited, shine ” ) , i.e. "the shining color"; see also Welsh nwyfiant ( “ splendor, gleam ” ) , Old Irish níab ( “ strength ” ) , noíb ( “ holy ” ) , Middle Irish níam ( “ splendor, gleam ” ) , níamda ( “ shining ” ) , Proto-Germanic *nīþą ( “ envy ” ) , Latin niteō ( “ to shine ” ) . [ 1 ] [ 2 ]
