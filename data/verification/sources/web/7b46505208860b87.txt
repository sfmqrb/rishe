 Persian
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Borrowed from Arabic كَتَّان ( kattān ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 کتان • ( katân ) 

 flax ( the plant or fibers of Linum usitatissimum ) 

 linen ( thread or cloth made from flax fiber ) 
 شلوارِ کتان ― šalvâr-e katân ― linen pants, linen trousers
