 Arabic
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Aramaic and post-Biblical Hebrew מרים ( Maryam ) , from Biblical Hebrew מִרְיָם ( Miryám ) , q.v.

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 IPA ( key ) : /mar.jam/ 

 Proper noun
 [ edit ] 

 مَرْيَم • ( maryam )  f 

 a female given name , Maryam , from Biblical Hebrew, equivalent to English Mary or Miriam , Latin Marīa , or French Marie 
 Mary , mother of Jesus 

 Miriam , sister of Moses and Aaron 

 The 19th sura (chapter) of the Qur'an .
