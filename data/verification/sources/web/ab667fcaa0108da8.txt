 Ottoman Turkish
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Borrowed from Persian سپرز ( seporz ) , a variant of اسپرز ( esporz , “ spleen, milt ” ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 سپرز • ( süpürz ) 

 spleen , milt , a vascular gland located in the left upper abdomen 
 Synonyms: طالاق ( dalak ) , طحال ( tahal )
