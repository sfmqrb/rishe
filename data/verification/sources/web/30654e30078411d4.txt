 Proto-Indo-European *peh₂- 
 Sanskrit पा ( pā ) 

 Sanskrit गोपा ( gopā ) bf. 
 Sanskrit गुप् 

 Back-formation from गोपा ( gopā́ , “ protector of cows; protector, guardian ” ) , whence also गोपायति ( gopāyáti , “ to guard ” ) .

 Root
 [ edit ] 

 गुप् • ( gup ) 

 to guard , defend , protect , preserve 

 to hide , conceal 

 ( in the desiderative ) to shun , detest

 Adjective
 [ edit ] 

 गुप् • ( gup ) stem 

 (at the end of a compound) defending , protecting 
 धर्म ( dharma , “ law, morality ” ) + गुप् ( gup ) → धर्मगुप् ( dharmagup , “ protecting or observing the law ” ) 

 being on one's guard or preserving oneself from something
