 Verb

 رَدَّ • ( radda ) I ( non-past يَرُدُّ ( yaruddu ) , verbal noun رَدّ ( radd ) ) 

 ( transitive ) to return , to bring back , to put back 
 رَدَدْتُ ٱلْمَالَ فِي جَيْبِي. radadtu l-māla fī jaybī. I put the money back into my pocket. 

 609–632 CE , Qur'an , 62:8 : قُلۡ إِنَّ ٱلۡمَوۡتَ ٱلَّذِي تَفِرُّونَ مِنۡهُ فَإِنَّهُۥ مُلَٰقِيكُمۡۖ ثُمَّ تُرَدُّونَ إِلَىٰ عَٰلِمِ ٱلۡغَيۡبِ وَٱلشَّهَٰدَةِ فَيُنَبِّئُكُم بِمَا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡمَلُونَ. "Say: 'Lo! the death from which ye shrink will surely meet you, and afterward ye will be returned unto the Knower of the Invisible and the Visible, and He will tell you what ye used to do.'" 

 to reply , to answer , to respond 

 to repel 

 to resist 

 to refuse 

 to reject 

 to restrain , to keep ( someone مِن ( min ) something ) 

 to refute 

 to dissuade 

 to reinstate

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Inherited from Arabic رَدَّ ( radda ) .

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 IPA ( key ) : /rad/ , [rad] 

 Audio ( al-Lidd ) : ( file ) 

 Verb
 [ edit ] 

 ردّ • ( radd ) I ( present بردّ ( birodd ) ) 

 to reply , to respond , to answer [ with على ]
 بسلّم عليه و ما بردّ . basallem ʕalēh w-mā birodd . I greet him and he doesn't respond . 

 ردّ على سؤال ي ، وين كنت مبارح ؟ rodd ʕala suʔāli: wēn kunt mbāreḥ? Answer my question: Where were you yesterday? 

 ردّ ع التلفون . ― radd ʕal-telfōn. ― He answered the phone. 

 to listen to, to follow ( advice , instructions )

 to obey , to listen to [ with على ]
 Synonym: سِمِع الكِلمة ( simiʕ l-kilme ) 
 الولاد لازم يردّو على أهل هم . l-ūlād lāzem yroddu ʕala ʔahl-hom. Children must do as they are told by their parents. (literally, “ Children must obey their parents. ”) 

 to close 
 Synonym: سكّر ( sakkar ) 
 ردّ الباب بسرعة ! ― rodd il-bāb bsurʕa! ― Close the door quickly!
