 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 بِكْر • ( bikr )  m ( plural أَبْكَار ( ʔabkār ) ) 

 firstborn child

 male or female virgin , celibate 
 609–632 CE , Qur'an , 56:36 : فَجَعَلْنَاهُنَّ أَبْكَارًا fajaʕalnāhunna ʔabkāran And [we] made them virgins 

 “ يَا مَرْيَمُ الْبِكْرُ فُقْتِ [ O Virgin Mary Ye Surpassed ]”‎ [1] : يَا مَرْيَمُ الْ بِكْرُ فُقْتِ الشَّمْسَ وَالْقَمَرَ وَكُلَّ نَجْمٍ بِأَفْلَاكِ السَّمَاءِ سَرَى yā maryamu l- bikru fuqti š-šamsa wālqamara wakulla najmin biʔaflāki s-samāʔi sarā O Virgin Mary, ye surpassed the sun and the moon and all the stars that orbited the heavenly spheres by night.

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Borrowed from Arabic بِكْر ( bikr , “ firstborn; virgin ” ) . Doublet of بكار ( bekâr , “ bachelor ” ) .

 Adjective
 [ edit ] 

 بكر • ( bikr or bikir ) 

 virgin , chaste , in a state of virginity , not having had sexual intercourse 

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 بكر • ( bikr or bikir ) ( definite accusative بكری ( bikri ) , plural ابكار ( ebkâr ) ) 

 firstborn , firstling , the first child born to in the birth order of a couple through childbirth 
 Synonym: نخست زاد ( nuhust zad ) 

 virgin , maid , vestal , a woman or female animal who has never had any sexual intercourse 
 Synonyms: بتول ( betul ) , دوشیزه ( duşize ) 

 ( of a woman ) virginity , maidenhood , the state or characteristic of being a virgin or a maiden 
 Synonyms: بكارت ( bekâret ) , قیزلق ( kızlık ) 

 hymen , maidenhead , a membrane which occludes the vaginal opening in human females 
 Synonyms: بكارت ( bekâret ) , قیزلق ( kızlık )
