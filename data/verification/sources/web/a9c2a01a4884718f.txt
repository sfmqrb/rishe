 Latin beryllus 

 Borrowed from Ancient Greek βήρυλλος ( bḗrullos ) , borrowed from Prakrit वेरुलिअ ( vērulia ) , from Proto-Dravidian *weḷ-ūr ( name of a city ) + Prakrit -इय ( -iya ) .
