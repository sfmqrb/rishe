 Etymology 1
 [ edit ] 

 Inherited from Middle Persian *gazar . Compare Sanskrit गार्जर ( gārjara ) , Classical Syriac ܓܙܪܐ ( gezzārāʾ ) , Hebrew גֶּזֶר ( gézer ) , Pashto ګازره ( gāzara ) , Alviri-Vidari گزر ( gazer ) , Baluchi گزرک ( gizirk, gazirk ) , Kurdish gêzer , گێزەر ( gêzer ) , Northern Luri گزر ( gezer ) . Compare also the Turkic borrowings: Bashkir кишер ( kişer ) , Tatar кишер ( kişer ) , Turkmen käşir .
