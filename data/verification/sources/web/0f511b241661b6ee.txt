 This Proto-Indo-European entry contains reconstructed terms and roots . As such, the term(s) in this entry are not directly attested , but are hypothesized to have existed based on comparative evidence.

 Proto-Indo-European
 [ edit ] 

 Root
 [ edit ] 

 *dʰebʰ- ( perfective ) [ 1 ] [ 2 ] [ 3 ] [ 4 ] 

 to make small , belittle , lessen , diminish 

 to strike , harm , injure 

 Alternative reconstructions
 [ edit ] 

 *debʰ- [ 5 ] [ 6 ] ( connecting Old Norse tefja ( “ to delay, hinder ” ) ) 

 Reconstruction notes
 [ edit ] 

 Mallory suggests that the meaning of the root could simply have been "harm", though—if the term were connected with Lithuanian dóbti ( “ to strike, beat kill ” ) —it could also describe physical violence. [ 7 ]
