 Persian
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Or زیفتن . Ultimately a derivation of Proto-Iranian *zay- ( “ to equip, to adorn ” ) , with no apparent cognates outside of Iranian. Related to زیور ( zivar , “ ornament ” ) . Compare Baluchi [script needed] ( ázit , “ to embellish, adorn ” ) . [ 1 ] 

 This etymology is missing or incomplete. Please add to it, or discuss it at the Etymology scriptorium . 
Particularly: “Cheung separates this root from the homophonous *zay- ( “ to set in motion, impel ” ) (the latter from Proto-Indo-European *ǵʰey- ( “ to drive, propel ” ) ); that said, the "equip" sense seems like it could be related to the "drive" sense, as equipping could be thought of as "driving decorative items onto oneself or something else". In addition, note strong semantic and formal similarities to Proto-Balto-Slavic *źibḗˀtei ( “ to shine; to strike; to wear (clothes) ” ) , the latter whence Lithuanian žibė́ti ( “ to glow, shine ” ) , Latvian zibens ( “ lightning ” ) .”

 Verb
 [ edit ] 

 زیبیدَن • ( zēbīdan / zibidan ) ( present stem زیب ( zib ) , Tajik spelling зебидан ) [ 2 ] [ 3 ] 

 ( archaic ) to adorn , embellish , beautify 
 Synonyms: آراستَن ( ârâstan ) , زیبا کَردَن ( zibâ kardan )
