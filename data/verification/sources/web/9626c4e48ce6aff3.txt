 This Proto-Indo-European entry contains reconstructed terms and roots . As such, the term(s) in this entry are not directly attested , but are hypothesized to have existed based on comparative evidence.

 Proto-Indo-European
 [ edit ] 

 Reconstruction
 [ edit ] 

 Sometimes also reconstructed as *dʰuHmós . The reconstruction of root-final laryngeal *h₂ is based on Hittite 𒀭𒌅𒉿𒀪𒄩𒀸 ( antuwaḫḫaš , “ human ” ) and 𒈭𒄩𒀀𒄑𒍣 ( tuḫḫāizzi , “ to produce smoke ” ) .

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From *dʰewh₂- ( “ to smoke ” ) +‎ *-mós .
