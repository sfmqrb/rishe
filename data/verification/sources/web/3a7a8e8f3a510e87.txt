 Etymology 1
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian [script needed] ( sʾn' /⁠sān⁠/ ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 سان • ( sân ) 

 kind , manner 
 c. 1260s , Jalāl ad-Dīn Mohammad Rūmī , translated by Reynold A. Nicholson , مثنوی معنوی [ Masnavi-ye-Ma'navi ], volume I, verse 3842: مر جفاگر را چنینها می‌‌دهم
پیش پای چپ چه سان سر می‌‌نهم mar jafā-gar rā čunin-hā mē-diham
pēš-i pāy-i čap či sān sar mē-niham Such things I am giving to the doer of iniquity:
(thou seest) in what fashion I lay my head before the left foot.
