 Persian
 [ edit ] 

 Alternative forms
 [ edit ] 

 پیام ( payâm ) 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Inherited from Middle Persian pgtʾm , compare Manichaean Middle Persian pygʾm , Sogdian [script needed] ( patγām ) , Northern Kurdish pêxemet ( “ sake, in the name of ” ) , pêxem ( “ barrier, obstacle; from the sense of "come against" ” ) , Central Kurdish پێخەم ( pêxem , “ barrier, hindrance; answer ” ) . Ultimately from Old Iranian , from Proto-Iranian *pati- ( “ against ” ) + a formation derived from Proto-Iranian *gam- ( “ to come ” ) , the latter from Proto-Indo-Iranian *gam- , from Proto-Indo-European *gʷem- ( “ to come, step ” ) . Compare also Iranian borrowings: Old Armenian պատգամ ( patgam ) , Aramaic פִּתְגָמָא ( piṯḡāmā ) , Classical Syriac ܦܬܓܡܐ ( peṯəḡāmāʾ ) , Classical Mandaic ࡐࡅࡂࡃࡀࡌࡀ ( pugdāmā ) , Hebrew פִּתְגָּם ( piṯgām ) .
