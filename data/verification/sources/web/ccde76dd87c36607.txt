 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 کینه • ( kīna / kine ) ( Tajik spelling кина ) 

 alternative form of کین ( kīn  / kin , “ hatred , rancour , malevolence ” ) 
 c. 1260s , Jalāl ad-Dīn Mohammad Rūmī , translated by Reynold A. Nicholson , مثنوی معنوی [ Masnavi-ye-Ma'navi ], volume II, verse 274: اصل کینه دوزخ است و کین تو
جزو آن کل است و خصم دین تو asl-i kīna dōzax ast u kin-i tū
juzw-i ān kul ast u xasm-i dīn-i tū The origin of malice is Hell, and your malice
is a part of that whole and is the enemy of your religion.
