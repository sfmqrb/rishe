 Pahari-Potwari

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Borrowed from Classical Persian سَفید ( safēd ) , from earlier Early New Persian سَپید ( sapēd ) , from Middle Persian spyt' , from Proto-Iranian *cwaytáh , from Proto-Indo-Iranian *ćwaytás , from Proto-Indo-European *ḱweyt- .

 Adjective
 [ edit ] 

 سَفید ( safed ) 

 white

 Persian سفید 

 From earlier Early New Persian سَپید ( sapēd ) , from Middle Persian spyt' , from Proto-Iranian *cwaytáh , from Proto-Indo-Iranian *ćwaytás , from Proto-Indo-European *ḱweyt- . Compare Avestan 𐬯𐬞𐬀𐬉𐬙𐬀 ( spaēta , “ white ” ) , Sogdian 𐼰𐼼𐼾𐼰𐼷𐽂 ( ʾspʾyt /⁠əspēt⁠/ ) , Sanskrit श्वेत ( śvetá , “ white, bright ” ) .
