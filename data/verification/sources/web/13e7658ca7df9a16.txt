 Persian Wikipedia has an article on: افشین 

 Wikipedia fa 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Possibly borrowed from Bactrian Αφϸιιανο ( Afšiiano ) , related to Middle Persian [script needed] ( Pišīn ) or its cognate in another Middle Iranian language, especially Sogdian , of unknown ultimate origin. Akin to Avestan 𐬞𐬌𐬯𐬌𐬥𐬀𐬵- ( pisinah- ) and Old Armenian Ափշին ( Apʻšin ) and Օշին ( Ōšin ) (earlier Աւշին ( Awšin ) ).
