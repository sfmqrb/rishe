 Noun

 چرم ( çirm ) 

 ( dialectal , Erbil ) alternative form of چڵم ( çillm , “ snot ” ) 

 Persian
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian [script needed] ( clm /⁠čarm⁠/ , “ skin, hide, leather ” ) , from Old Persian 𐎨𐎼𐎶𐎴 ( c-r-m-n /⁠carman-⁠/ , “ skin, hide, parchment ” ) , from Proto-Iranian *čárma . Compare Pashto څرمن ( carman , “ skin ” ) , Sanskrit चर्मन् ( carman , “ skin, hide, leather ” ) , etc.

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 چرم • ( čarm ) ( Tajik spelling чарм ) 

 leather 
 1010 , Ferdowsi , شاهنامه [ Shahnameh ]: بپوشید تن را به چرم پلنگ
که جوشن نبود و نه آیین جنگ bipōšīd tan rā ba čarm -i palang
ki jawšan na-būd u na āyīn-i jang He dressed his body with leopard skin ,
For there were no breastplates and no devices of war. (romanization in Classical Persian)
