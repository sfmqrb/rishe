 beryllium ( countable and uncountable , plural berylliums ) 

 ( chemistry , uncountable ) The chemical element ( symbol Be) with an atomic number of 4; a soft silvery-white low density alkaline earth metal with specialist industrial applications.
 ( countable ) An atom of this element.

 Beryllium sample.
