 Proper noun
 [ edit ] 

 طهمورث • ( tahmuras or tahmures ) 

 ( Iranian mythology ) Tahmooras/Tahmoorath, a mythical king mentioned in the Shahnameh .

 ( archaic ) a male given name , Tahmooras and Tahmoores
