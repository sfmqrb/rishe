 Etymology
 [ edit ]

 Proper noun
 [ edit ] 

 فروردین • ( farwardīn  / farvardin ) ( Tajik spelling фарвардин ) 

 Farvardin ( the first solar month of the Persian calendar ) 

 Name of the nineteenth day of any month of the solar Persian calendar.
