 Arabic
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Literally meaning from the city of الْكُوفَة ( al-kūfa , “ Kufa ” ) or in the Kufan style.

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 IPA ( key ) : /kuː.fij.ja/
