 This Proto-Iranian entry contains reconstructed terms and roots . As such, the term(s) in this entry are not directly attested , but are hypothesized to have existed based on comparative evidence.

 Proto-Iranian
 [ edit ] 

 Alternative forms
 [ edit ] 

 *parādáyjah 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From *pari- ( “ around ” ) , from Proto-Indo-Iranian *pari- , from Proto-Indo-European *péri ( “ around ” ) , + *dáyjah ( “ wall ” ) , from Proto-Indo-Iranian *dʰáyȷ́ʰas , from Proto-Indo-European *dʰóyǵʰos ( “ something formed ” ) , derivative of Proto-Indo-European *dʰeyǵʰ- ( “ to knead, form ” ) .

 ( garden ) : Compare typologically Ancient Greek περίβολος ( períbolos ) (the same PIE prefix); English garden , Russian огоро́д ( ogoród ) , Latin hortus .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 *paridayjah 

 circular boundary wall ; place enclosed by a circular boundary wall

 garden , orchard
