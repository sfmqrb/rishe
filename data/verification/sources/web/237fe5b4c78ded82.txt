 Etymology 1
 [ edit ] 

 This section or entry lacks references or sources. Please help verify this information by adding appropriate citations . You can also discuss it at the Tea Room . 
Particularly: “Are we certain the term was borrowed from Avestan and not inherited from a common ancestor?”

 Inherited from Middle Persian twlkʾ ( /⁠tōlag⁠/ , “ baby animal, whelp ” ) , from Avestan 𐬙𐬀𐬎𐬭𐬎𐬥𐬀 ( ta u runa , “ young ” ) , Northern Luri تیله ( tilə ) . Cognate with Sanskrit तरुण ( taruṇa , “ juvenile ” ) . Cf. تور ( tōr  / tur , “ uncivilized ” ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 توله • ( tōla / tule ) 

 whelp

 Etymology 2
 [ edit ] 

 Inherited from Middle Persian *tōlag , whence also Arabic طَوْلَق ( ṭawlaq , “ mallow ” ) . Probably a variation of ترشه ( turša  / torše , “ sorrel, dock ” ) , تور ( tōr  / tur , “ rosy dock ” ) 

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 توله • ( tōla / tule ) 

 mallow 

 Etymology 3
 [ edit ] 

 Borrowed from Hindustani   तोला / تولا ( tolā ) . The weight corresponds to the weight of an Indian silver rupee during the whole Mughal Empire .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 توله • ( tōla / tule ) 

 alternative form of تولا ( tōlā  / tulâ , “ weight measure for especially silver and gold of about 11.6 grams ” )
