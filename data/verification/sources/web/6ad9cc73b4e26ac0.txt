 Arabic
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology 1
 [ edit ] 

 Onomatopoeic .

 Verb
 [ edit ] 

 مَهْ or مَهٍ • ( mah or mahin ) 

 ( defective ) Stop!
 مَهْ عَنْ إِطْلَاقِ ٱلشَّائِعَاتِ الخَبِيثةِ. mah ʕan ʔiṭlāqi š-šāʔiʕāti l-ḵabīṯati. Stop starting malicious rumors.

 Etymology 2
 [ edit ] 

 Inherited from Proto-Iranian *HmíčaH , from Proto-Indo-Iranian *Hmíkš , from Proto-Indo-European *h₃meygʰ- .

 Cognates with English mist , Vedic Sanskrit मिह् ( mih , “ fog, mist ” ) , Ancient Greek ὀμίχλη ( omíkhlē , “ fog, mist ” ) , Polish mgła ( “ fog, mist, haze ” ) , and Lithuanian miglà ( “ mist, haze ” ) . Related to میغ ( mēġ  / miġ ) .

 Etymology 4
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian 𐫖𐫏𐫆 ( myẖ /⁠meh⁠/ ) , from Proto-Iranian *majā́ , from Proto-Indo-Iranian *maȷ́ʰā́ , from Proto-Indo-European *méǵh₂s ( “ great ” ) . Cognate with English mickle , much , Latin magnus , Ancient Greek μέγας ( mégas ) .
