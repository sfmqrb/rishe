 Ancient Greek
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Proto-Indo-European *spewd- ( “ to press, hurry ” ) . [ 1 ] Cognate with Albanian punë , Lithuanian spáusti , Old Armenian փոյթ ( pʻoytʻ ) .

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 IPA ( key ) : /spěu̯.dɔː/ → /ˈspeβ.ðo/ → /ˈspev.ðo/ 

 ( 5 th BCE Attic ) IPA ( key ) : /spěu̯.dɔː/ 

 ( 1 st CE Egyptian ) IPA ( key ) : /ˈspew.do/ 

 ( 4 th CE Koine ) IPA ( key ) : /ˈspeβ.ðo/ 

 ( 10 th CE Byzantine ) IPA ( key ) : /ˈspev.ðo/ 

 ( 15 th CE Constantinopolitan ) IPA ( key ) : /ˈspev.ðo/ 

 Verb
 [ edit ] 

 σπεύδω • ( speúdō ) 

 ( transitive ) 
 to set going , urge on , hasten 
 460 BCE – 430 BCE , Herodotus , Histories 1.38 : πρὸς ὧν τὴν ὄψιν ταύτην τόν τε γάμον τοι τοῦτον ἔσπευσα pròs hôn tḕn ópsin taútēn tón te gámon toi toûton éspeusa It is because of that vision that I hurried your marriage 

 c. 800 BCE – 600 BCE , Homer , Odyssey 19.137 : οἱ δὲ γάμον σπεύδουσιν hoi dè gámon speúdousin They urge a marriage 

 c. 445 BCE , Sophocles , Ajax 804 : καὶ σπεύσαθ’ , οἱ μὲν Τεῦκρον ἐν τάχει μολεῖν, kaì speúsath’ , hoi mèn Teûkron en tákhei moleîn, Hurry , some of you, to speed Teucer's coming; 

 to procure quickly , get ready 
 413 BCE , Euripides , Iphigenia in Tauris 1352 : κλίμακας σπεύδοντες klímakas speúdontes making haste to let down the ladders 

 to seek eagerly , strive after 
 6th century BC , Theognis of Megara , Elegies 335 : Μηδὲν ἄγαν σπεύδειν Mēdèn ágan speúdein be not overly eager in any matter 

 ( intransitive ) to press on , hasten 
 c. 800 BCE – 600 BCE , Homer , Iliad 11.119 : σπεύδουσ’ ἱδρώουσα κραταιοῦ θηρὸς ὑφ’ ὁρμῆς speúdous’ hidrṓousa krataioû thēròs huph’ hormês hasting and sweating before the onset of the mighty beast 

 c. 370 BCE , Xenophon , Anabasis 3.4.49 : καταλιπὼν τὸν ἵππον ἔσπευδε πεζῇ katalipṑn tòn híppon éspeude pezēî he left his horse behind and hurried forward on foot 

 ( with infinitive ) to be eager to
 522 BCE – 443 BCE , Pindar , Olympian Ode 4.22 : κῦδος ὄρσαι σπεύδει Καμαρίνᾳ kûdos órsai speúdei Kamarínāi hurries to rouse glory for Camarina 

 to be troubled in mind 
 300 BCE – 200 BCE , Septuagint, Exodus 15.15 : τότε ἔσπευσαν ἡγεμόνες Ἐδώμ tóte éspeusan hēgemónes Edṓm Then the chiefs of Edom were dismayed .
