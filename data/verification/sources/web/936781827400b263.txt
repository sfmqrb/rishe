 This Proto-Germanic entry contains reconstructed terms and roots . As such, the term(s) in this entry are not directly attested , but are hypothesized to have existed based on comparative evidence.

 Proto-Germanic
 [ edit ] 

 Alternative forms
 [ edit ] 

 *lambą 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Inherited from Proto-Indo-European *h₁l̥h₁-on-bʰ-os- (related to Scottish Gaelic lon ( “ elk ” ) , Ancient Greek ἔλᾰφος ( élăphos , “ red deer ” ) ), enlargement of *h₁elh₁én , from *h₁el- ( “ deer, elk ” ) . [ 1 ] 

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ]

 Rhymes: -ɑm.bɑz
