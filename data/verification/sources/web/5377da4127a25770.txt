 Persian
 [ edit ] 

 Alternative forms
 [ edit ] 

 گیوه ( giva ) ( dialectal , Sistan ) 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian [script needed] ( wdpk' /⁠wēwag⁠/ , “ widow ” ) , from Proto-Iranian *HwidáwaH , from Proto-Indo-Iranian *HwidʰáwaH , from Proto-Indo-European *h₁widʰéwh₂ , a derivative of the root *h₁weydʰh₁- ( “ to separate ” ) .

 Cognates include Northern Kurdish bî , Ossetian и́дӕдз ( ídæʒ ) (<*widawačī-), Zazaki viyā , Sanskrit विधवा ( vidhavā ) and Younger Avestan 𐬬𐬌𐬜𐬀𐬎𐬎𐬀 ( viδauua ) , English widow , Old Irish fedb , Latin vidua , and Old Church Slavonic въдова ( vŭdova ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 بیوه • ( bēwa / bive ) ( plural بیوه‌ها ( bēwa-hā / bive-hâ ) , or بیوگان , Tajik spelling бева ) 

 widow 

 ( rare ) widower
