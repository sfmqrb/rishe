 Alternative forms

 کوشه ( kôša ) 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian kwšk' ( kōšk , “ pavilion, palace, kiosk ” ) . Compare Aramaic קושקא ( qōšqāʾ ) / ܓܘܫܩܐ ( gawšāqāʾ ) , Iranian borrowings. Doublet of کیوسک .
