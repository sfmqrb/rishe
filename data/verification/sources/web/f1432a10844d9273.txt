 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Russian Кавказ ( Kavkaz ) , from Ancient Greek Καύκασος ( Kaúkasos ) , ultimately of Iranian origin.

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 IPA ( key ) : [käf.ˈkäz] 

 Hyphenation: Кав‧каз 

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 Кавказ • ( Kawkaz ) 

 Caucasus (a region in Eurasia)
 Кавказда ир бала тыуыуҙы мылтыҡтан атып хəбəр итəлəр. Kawkazda ir bala tıwıwðı mıltıqtan atıp xəbər itələr. In the Caucasus , they would fire a rifle to signal that a boy is born.

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

From Ancient Greek Καύκασος ( Kaúkasos ) , ultimately of Iranian origin.

 Russian Wikipedia has an article on: Кавказ 

 Wikipedia ru 

 Was already attested in Old East Slavic as Кавкасийскыѣ горы ( Kavkasijskyjě gory ) , a calque of Καυκάσια ὄρη , however according to Vasmer and Trubachyov the current form Кавказ ( Kavkaz ) stems from French Caucase and German Kaukasus .
