 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Russian самова́р ( samovár , “ self-boiler ” ) ; from само́ ( samó , “ self ” ) + вари́ть ( varítʹ , “ to boil; to cook ” ) .

 Apparently mediated or influenced by Persian سماور ( samâvar , “ samovar ” ) .

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Russian самова́р ( samovár ) , literally “self-boiler”; from само́ ( samó ) “self” + вари́ть ( varítʹ ) “to boil” or “to cook”. Northern Luri سںمۋںر ( səmüər ) .
