 Persian
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology 1
 [ edit ] 

 From Proto-Iranian *āmaH- , from Proto-Iranian *ā- + *maH- ( “ to measure ” ) , from Proto-Indo-Iranian *maH- , from Proto-Indo-European *h₂éd ( “ at ” ) + *meh₁- ( “ to measure ” ) . [ 1 ] Compare آماده ( âmâde , “ ready ” ) , Avestan 𐬁𐬨𐬁𐬙𐬀- ( āmāta- ) , Sanskrit माति ( māti , “ measure, knowledge ” ) , Ancient Greek μῆτῐς ( mêtĭs , “ skill, counsel, plan ” ) .

 Verb

 آمادن • ( âmâdan ) ( present stem آما ( âmâ ) ) 

 to prepare
