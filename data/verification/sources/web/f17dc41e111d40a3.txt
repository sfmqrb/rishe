 Persian
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Inherited from Middle Persian [Book Pahlavi needed] ( hndwhtn' /⁠handōxtan⁠/ , “ to gain, acquire, amass ” ) .

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 ( Classical Persian ) IPA ( key ) : /ʔan.doːx.ˈtan/

 Verb
 [ edit ] 

 اندوختن • ( andōxtan  / anduxtan ) ( present stem اندوز ( anduz ) , Tajik spelling андӯхтан ) ( transitive ) 

 to collect , to amass , to gather 
 Synonyms: جمع کردن ( jam' kardan ) ; ( archaic ) الفختن ( alfaxtan ) 
 c. 1520 , Selim I of the Ottoman Empire , edited by Benedek Péri, The Persian Dīvān of Yavuz Sulṭān Selīm , Budapest, Hungary: Research Centre for the Humanities, Eötvös Loránd Research Network, →ISBN , page 256 : لاله و گل را رخت با یکدیگر آمیخته
فتنه و آشوب را چشمت به سحر اندوخته lāla u gul rā ruxat bā yakdīgar āmēxta
fitna u āšōb rā čašmat ba sihr andōxta Your cheeks have mixed the tulip and rose with each other,
Your eyes have amassed temptation and tumult with sorcery. (Classical Persian transliteration)
