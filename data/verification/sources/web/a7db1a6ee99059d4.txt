 Etymology

 Root

 7 terms 

 Cognate with Arabic ح ن ن ( ḥ n n ) .

 Verb
 [ edit ] 

 חָנַן • ( khanán ) ( pa'al construction , infinitive לחון / לָחֹן , future יחון / יָחֹן , imperative חון / חֹן ) 

 to pardon , grant amnesty 

 to pity , have mercy , deal kindly 
 Tanach , Numbers 6:25 , with translation of the Jewish Publication Society The value tsrc=JPS is ambiguous and deprecated. After checking the translation, please replace it with JPS1917 , JPS1985 , or JPS2023 . See the documentation . : יָאֵר יְהוָה פָּנָיו אֵלֶיךָ וִיחֻנֶּךָּ ׃ ya'ér YHVH panáv elékha vikhunéka .
yāʾēr YHWH pānāw ʾēléḵā wīḥunnékkā . The LORD make His face to shine upon thee, and be gracious unto thee ; 

 Avinu Malkeinu :
 אָבִינוּ מַלְכֵּנוּ חָנֵּנוּ וַעֲנֵנוּ כִּי אֵין בָּנוּ מַעֲשִׂים עֲשֵׂה עִמָּנוּ צְדָקָה וָחֶסֶד וְהוֹשִׁיעֵנוּ. avínu malkénu khonénu va'anénu kí 'én bánu ma'asím asé imánu ts'daká vakhésed v'hoshi'énu.
ʾāḇī́nū malkḗnū ḥonnḗnū waʿănḗnū kī ʾēn bā́nū maʿăśīm ʿăśē ʿimmā́nū ṣəḏāqā wāḥésed wəhōšīʿḗnū. Our Father, our King, be gracious to us and answer us, for we have no meritorious deeds; deal charitably and kindly with us and deliver us.
