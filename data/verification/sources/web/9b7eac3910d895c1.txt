 This Proto-Germanic entry contains reconstructed terms and roots . As such, the term(s) in this entry are not directly attested , but are hypothesized to have existed based on comparative evidence.

 Proto-Germanic
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Proto-Indo-European *h₂émsus , from *h₂ems- ( “ to engender, beget ” ) . Compare Hittite 𒈗𒍑 ( ḫa-aš-šu-uš /⁠ḫaššuš⁠/ , “ king ” ) , Old Avestan 𐬀𐬵𐬎 ( ahu ) / Younger Avestan 𐬀𐬢𐬵𐬎 ( aŋhu , “ lord ” ) , Sanskrit असुर ( ásura , “ godlike, powerful ” ) .

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 IPA ( key ) : /ˈɑn.suz/ 

 Rhymes: -ɑn.suz 

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 *ansuz   m [ 1 ] 

 deity , god 
 Synonyms: *gudą , *tīwaz 

 ( Runic alphabet ) name of the rune ᚨ ( a )
