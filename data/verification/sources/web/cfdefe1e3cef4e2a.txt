 Adjective
 [ edit ] 

 سفت • ( seft ) 

 hard ( resistant to pressure, not soft ) 
 بالش سفتی ― bâleš-e seft-i ― a hard pillow 

 tough , hard ( of food, difficult to cut or chew ) 

 thick ( of a liquid, having a viscous consistency ) 
 Synonym: غلیظ ( ġaliz ) 

 taut ( of a rope, length of material, bow string; tight, under tension ) 
 Antonym: شل ( šol )
