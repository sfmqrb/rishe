 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Turkish zurna , from Ottoman Turkish زورنا ( zurna ) , from Classical Persian سرنا ( surnā ) ; see there for more. Cognate with English horn , Latin cornu ( “ horn ” ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 zurna ( plural zurnas ) 

 ( music ) A double-reed outdoor wind instrument , usually accompanied by a davul (bass drum) in Anatolian folk music .

 Synonyms
 [ edit ]
