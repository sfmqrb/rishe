 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle English niter , nitere , nitre , nytre , from Old French nitre , from Latin nitrum ( “ native soda, natron ” ) , from Ancient Greek νίτρον ( nítron ) . Ultimately from Egyptian nṯrj ( “ natron ” ) . Doublet of natron and trona . Compare also natrium , nitrogen . Compare reconstructed Proto-Germanic *lauþrą ( “ nitre ” ) related to Modern English lye .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 niter ( countable and uncountable , plural niters ) 

 ( US , inorganic chemistry ) A mineral form of potassium nitrate ( saltpetre ) used in making gunpowder .

 ( obsolete ) Native sodium carbonate ; natron .
