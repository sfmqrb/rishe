 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian [script needed] ( ʾyš /⁠ēš⁠/ , “ plough ” ) ( Manichaean Middle Persian [script needed] ( hyš /⁠hēš⁠/ ) ).

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ]
