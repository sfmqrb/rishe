 Ottoman Turkish
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Borrowed from Persian دخمه ( daxme ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 دخمه • ( dahme ) 

 vault , an underground burial chamber for the dead 

 mausoleum , a large stately tomb housing various graves 

 ( Zoroastrianism ) dakhma , tower of silence 

 Roman candle , a traditional type of firework 

 Descendants
 [ edit ] 

 Turkish: dahme

 Persian
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Inherited from Middle Persian [Book Pahlavi needed] ( dhmk' /⁠daxmag⁠/ ) , borrowed from Avestan 𐬛𐬀𐬑𐬨𐬀 ( daxma ) , from Proto-Indo-European *dʰm̥bʰ- ( “ to dig ” ) . Compare Old Armenian դամբան ( damban , “ tomb ” ) , Ancient Greek τᾰ́φος ( tắphos , “ tomb ” ) . Attested in New Persian since the Shāhnāma , 1010.

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 دخمه • ( daxma / daxme ) ( Tajik spelling дахма ) 

 ( Zoroastrianism ) tower of silence ( a low , cylindrical , open-topped tower where Zoroastrians place the bodies of deceased people to disintegrate from exposure and consumption by carrion birds such as vultures , the remaining bones being kept in an ossuary ) 

 catacomb , underground vault , crypt 
 فقیران در دخمه زیرزمینی زندگی می‌کنند. faġirân dar daxme -ye zirzamini zendegi mi-konand. The poor live in underground crypts .
