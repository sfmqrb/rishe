 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian [script needed] ( plwyc /⁠Parwēz⁠/ ) in Pazand texts 𐬞𐬀𐬭𐬬𐬍𐬰 ( parvīz ) . Compare with Avestan 𐬞𐬀𐬊𐬌𐬭𐬌𐬌𐬀𐬈𐬥𐬌 ( pao i riiaeni ) , Northern Kurdish pêwir , Central Kurdish پێروو ( pêrû ) and Pashto پرېوونې ( perwǔne ) .
