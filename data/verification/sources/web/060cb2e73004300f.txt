 Persian
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian [script needed] ( HNHTWN-tn' /⁠nihādan⁠/ ) , from Proto-Iranian *ni- + *dádaHtī ( from Proto-Indo-Iranian *dʰádʰaHti , from Proto-Indo-European *dʰédʰeh₁ti ) . Compare Northern Kurdish nan , niyan , nihan ( “ to put ” ) , Central Kurdish نان ( nan , “ to put ” ) , Zazaki naene . Northern Luri نیاٸه ( nyae ) , Bakhtiari نݖاڊن ( naaḍen ) .

 Iranian Persian

 Tajik

 نهادن • ( nahâdan or nehâdan ) ( present stem نه ( nah or neh ) ) 

 to put ; to place ; to lay ; to set 
 Synonyms: گُذاشتَن ( gozâštan ) , قَرار دادَن ( qarâr dâdan ) 
 c. 1390 , Hafez , “Ghazal 8”, in دیوان حافظ [ The Divān of Hafez ]‎ [1] : ساغر می بر کفم نه تا ز بَر
برکشم این دلق ازرق‌فام را sâġar-i may bar kaff-am nih tâ zi bar
barkašam în dalq-i azraq-fâm râ Place the wine goblet in my palm, so that from my chest
I might pluck off this azure dervish cloak. (romanization in Classical Persian)
