 Persian

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian [script needed] ( spʾs /⁠spās⁠/ ) , from Proto-Iranian *spácati , from Proto-Indo-Iranian *spáćati , from Proto-Indo-European *speḱ- ; see there for cognates. Unrelated to Russian спасибо ( spasibo )
