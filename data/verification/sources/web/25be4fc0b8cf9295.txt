 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 رونق • ( rawnaq / rownaġ ) ( Tajik spelling равнақ ) 

 boom , heyday , flourishing 
 رونق بازار ― rawnaq-i  / rownaġ-e bāzār  / bâzâr ― market boom 
 رونق و رکود ― rawnaq  / rownaġ wa rukūd  / va rokud ― boom and bust 

 ( poetic ) 
 beauty , glamour , splendor 

 c. 1260s , Jalāl ad-Dīn Mohammad Rūmī , translated by Reynold A. Nicholson , مثنوی معنوی [ Masnavi-ye-Ma'navi ], volume IV, verse 1734: رونق دنیا برآرد زو کساد، زانک هست از عالم کون و فساد rawnaq-i dunyā bar-ārad z-ō kasād, z-ānki hast az ālam-i kawn u fasād The splendour of the present life soon produces unsaleableness [i.e. becomes of no account], inasmuch as it belongs to the world of generation and corruption. ( Classical Persian transliteration ) 

 c. 1585 , عرفی شیرازی ( 'Orfi Shirazi ), “Poem 56”, in قصیده‌ها : تو از ملک عراقی واژگون کن عادت پیشین، اگر خواهی که حسن رونق هندوستان بینی. tu az mulk-i irāqī wāžgūn kun ādat-i pēšīn, agar xwāhī ki husn-i rawnaq-i hindūstān bīnī. You are from the Iraqi realm ( Persian Iraq , not modern Iraq); turn your old ways upside-down, if you wish to see the beauty of the splendor of India. 

 radiance , glow , luster

 Urdu
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology tree

 Arabic رَوْنَق ( rawnaq ) bor. 
 Classical Persian رَوْنَق ( rawnaq ) bor. 
 Urdu رَونَق 

 Borrowed from Classical Persian رَوْنَق ( rawnaq ) , borrowed from Arabic رَوْنَق ( rawnaq ) . First attested in c. 1564 as Middle Hindi رونق ( rvnq /⁠raunaq⁠/ ) . [ 1 ] 

 Cognate with Punjabi رَوݨَق ( rauṇaq ) .

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 ( Standard Urdu ) IPA ( key ) : /ɾɔː.nəq/ 

 ( colloquial ) IPA ( key ) : [ɾoː.nəq] 

 Rhymes: -əq 

 Hyphenation: رَو‧نَق 

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 رَونَق • ( raunaq )  m ( Hindi spelling रौनक़ ) 

 splendour , brilliance 

 glow , lustre ( of a face etc. ) 
 Synonym: شان ( śān ) 
 اس شہر میں رونق ہی نہیں ہے۔ is śahar mẽ koī raunaq nahī̃ hai. There is no splendour in this city. 

 liveliness , bustlingness ; flourishing state; vibe ( of a gathering , party , city etc. ) 
 وہ ہر محفل کی رونق ہے۔ voh har mahfil kī raunaq hai. They are the life of every party . 

 crowded ; busy ( of a market etc. ) 

 ( by extension ) progress ; enhancement
