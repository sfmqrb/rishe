 Proper noun
 [ edit ] 

 قیصر • ( qaysar  / ġeysar ) ( Tajik spelling Қайсар ) 

 Caesar , a Roman gens whose most notable member was Julius Caesar
 Synonym: ( more common in Iran ) سزار ( sezâr ) 

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 قیصر • ( qaysar / ġeysar ) ( plural قیاصره ( qayāsira / ġayâsere ) , Tajik spelling қайсар ) 

 Roman or Byzantine emperor 
 c. 1100 , Amīr Mu'izzī, “Qaṣīda 226”, in دیوان امیر معزی ‎ [1] : تو به بغدادی و در روم از هجوم لشکرت
هست قیصر مستمند و لشکر او سوگوار tu ba baġdād-ī u dar rūm az hujūm-i laškar-at
hast qaysar mustmand u laškar-i ō sōgwār You are in Baghdad, and in Byzantium, due to your army's attack,
The Emperor is in distress and his army is mournful. (Classical Persian transliteration) 

 Kaiser ; Holy Roman or German emperor
