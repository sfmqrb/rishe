 Alternative forms
 [ edit ] 

 میهمان ( mihmân ) 

 مهمون ( mehmun ) ( colloquial ) 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Inherited from Middle Persian [Book Pahlavi needed] ( m(ʾ)hmʾn' /⁠mehmān⁠/ , “ inhabitant; guest ” ) , ultimately from Proto-Indo-European *meyth₂- ( “ to change, exchange ” ) . Cognate with Northern Luri مؽمۏ ( mêmö ) , Pashto مېلمه ( melma ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 مهمان • ( mihmān / mehmân ) ( plural مهمانان ( mihmānān / mehmânân ) , or مهمان‌ها ( mihmān-hā / mehmân-hâ ) , Tajik spelling меҳмон ) 

 guest 
 Synonym: ( archaic ) سپنج ( sepanj ) 
 Antonym: میزبان ( mizbân , “ host ” ) 
 c. 1380 , Jahān Malik Khātūn , “Ghazal 379”, in دیوان جهان [ Divān-i Jahān ]‎ [1] : در فراقت دلم به جان آمد
آمدم یک شبی به مهمانت dar firāq-at dil-am ba jān āmad
āmadam yak šabē ba mihmān -at Out of parting from you, my heart came to death's verge;
Then one night, I came to you as a guest . (Classical Persian romanization) 

 patron of a hotel or restaurant
