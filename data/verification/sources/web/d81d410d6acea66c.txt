 Māori

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Proto-Polynesian *kapi ( “ to hold firmly between ” ) , [ 1 ] [ 2 ] from Proto-Central Pacific *kapi , *kavi , from Proto-Oceanic *kapit ( “ tongs ” ) , [ 3 ] from Proto-Eastern Malayo-Polynesian *qapit , from Proto-Central-Eastern Malayo-Polynesian *qapit , from Proto-Malayo-Polynesian *qapit ( “ to pinch, to squeeze ” ) . Cognates include Tahitian ʻapi ( “ to fold ” ) .

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 IPA ( key ) : /ˈkapi/ [ˈkɐpi] 

 Verb
 [ edit ] 

 kapi 

 to close 
 Synonym: kati 

 to cover , to shut 

 to occupy a surface or space
