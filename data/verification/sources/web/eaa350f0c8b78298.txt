 Origin and history of shish kebab 

 shish kebab (n.) 

 pieces of lamb or other meat grilled on skewers, 1913, from Armenian shish kabab , from Turkish siskebap , from sis "skewer" + kebap "roast meat."
 also from 1913 

 Entries linking to shish kebab 

 kebab (n.) 

 "pieces of meat roasted on a skewer," 1783 (compare shish kebab ).
