 Ottoman Turkish
 [ edit ] 

 Tire district 
 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Derived from Greek Θείρα ( Theíra ) .

 Proper noun
 [ edit ] 

 تیره • ( tire ) 

 Tire (a town and district of İzmir Province , Turkey )

 Descendants
 [ edit ] 

 Turkish: Tire 

 → Armenian: Թիրէ ( Tʻirē )

 [ edit ]

 Adjective
 [ edit ] 

 تیره • ( tēra / tire ) 

 dark ; dim 
 Synonym: تاریک ( tārīk  / târik ) 
 شبی تیره ― šabi tire ― a dark night 
 c. 1520 , Selim I of the Ottoman Empire , edited by Benedek Péri, The Persian Dīvān of Yavuz Sulṭān Selīm , Budapest, Hungary: Research Centre for the Humanities, Eötvös Loránd Research Network, →ISBN , page 75 : چو روز حشر سر از خاک تیره بردارم
بخاک پای تو خواهم نهاد روی چو زر čū rōz-i hašr sar az xāk-i tēra bardāram
ba-xāk-i pā-yi tu xwāham nihād rō-yi čū zar When I raise my head from the dark earth on Resurrection Day,
I will place my golden face on the earth below your feet. (Classical Persian transliteration) 

 turbulent (of water)
 آبِ تیره ― âb-e tire ― turbulent water 

 ( figurative ) gloomy ; despondent

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 تیره • ( tīra / tire ) 

 sect , division 
 section of a nomadic tribe ; sometimes synonymous with طایفه ( tāyifa  / tâyefe , “ tribal subdivision ” ) , sometimes a subsection within a طایفه . 

 religious sect 

 ( taxonomy ) family ; generally used for plants and birds. 

 Usage notes
 [ edit ] 

 خانواده ( xānawāda  / xânevâde ) is generally used for animal families.
