 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Borrowed from Spanish Venezuela , from Italian Veneziola , diminutive of Venezia ( “ Venice ” ) , after the stilt houses reminiscent of Venetian architecture.

 Proper noun
 [ edit ] 

 Venezuela 

 A country in South America . Official name: Bolivarian Republic of Venezuela . Capital and largest city: Caracas .
 2025 January 22, Clare Foran, Haley Talbot, Priscilla Alvarez, “Congress passes immigrant detention bill in first legislative win for Trump”, in CNN ‎ [1] , archived from the original on 21 April 2025 : The legislation is named after Laken Riley, a 22-year old Georgia student who was killed last year while out for a run. An undocumented migrant from Venezuela was convicted and sentenced to life without parole in the case that reignited a national debate over immigration and crime. 

 2025 December 20, Rob Schneider , AmericaFest 2025 speech ‎ [2] : You know, Venezuela . It is kind of next to Argentina, I think. I am not exactly sure, but it is definitely below Mexico. Probably. Like all Americans, I pride myself on not being very good at geology or geography, whatever it is called.
