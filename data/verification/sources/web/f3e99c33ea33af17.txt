 Persian Wikipedia has an article on: سرخ 

 Wikipedia fa 

 A سرخ ( surx ) pomegranate. 
 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From an earlier metathesis of سهر ( suhr ) , Southern Luri سوهر ( sohr ) (cf. سهراب ( suhrāb , “ literally a person who has red face ” ) ), from Middle Persian [Book Pahlavi needed] ( swhr /⁠suhr⁠/ , “ red ” ) , from Manichaean Middle Persian 𐫘𐫇𐫍𐫡 ( swhr /⁠suhr⁠/ ) , from Old Persian *θuxrah ( “ red, bright ” ) , from Proto-Iranian *cuxráh from Proto-Indo-Iranian *ćukrás . Compare Sanskrit शुक्र ( śukra , “ bright, resplendent ” ) , Parthian 𐫘𐫇𐫟𐫡 ( swxr /⁠suxr⁠/ , “ red ” ) , Northern Kurdish sor ( “ red ” ) , Ossetian сырх ( syrx , “ red ” ) and Avestan 𐬯𐬎𐬑𐬭𐬀 ( suxra ) .
