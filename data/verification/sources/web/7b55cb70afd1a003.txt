 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Old Persian 𐎠𐎱𐎭𐎠𐎴 ( a-p-d-a-n /⁠apadāna⁠/ ) . Doublet of iwan .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 apadana ( plural apadanas ) 

 a large hypostyle hall in ancient Persian palaces
