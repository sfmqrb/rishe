 Ottoman Turkish
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Borrowed from Persian روده ( rude , “ intestines ” ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 روده • ( rude ) 

 intestine , bowel , the alimentary canal of an animal through which food passes
 Synonyms: باغرساق ( bağırsak ) , معی ( miʿa ) 

 Descendants
 [ edit ] 

 Turkish: rude
