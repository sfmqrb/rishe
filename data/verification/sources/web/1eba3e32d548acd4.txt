 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology tree

 Old Persian *aivacadaθa

 Middle Persian yʾcdẖ 
 Persian یازده 

 From Middle Persian 𐫏𐫀𐫉𐫅𐫍 ( yʾzdh ) , [Book Pahlavi needed] ( yʾcdẖ /⁠yāzdah⁠/ ) , from earlier *yvāzdah , from Old Persian *aivacadaθa .
