 This Proto-Germanic entry contains reconstructed terms and roots . As such, the term(s) in this entry are not directly attested , but are hypothesized to have existed based on comparative evidence.

 Proto-Germanic
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Uncertain. Often explained as derived from the Proto-Indo-European root *dʰegʷʰ- ( “ to burn ” ) , [ 1 ] but the loss of the labialization would be irregular; *dawaz would be expected. Kroonen posits instead a root *dʰeǵʰ- ( “ day ” ) , from which he says a heteroclitic noun *dʰṓǵʰ-r/n- ( “ day ” ) is also derived, the source of Proto-Germanic *dōgera- / *dōgena- (see *dōg- ) and Proto-Indo-Iranian *Háȷ́ʰr̥ (from which Sanskrit अहर् ( ahar ) ) with irregular loss of the initial consonant, possibly in the zero grade. [ 2 ] For this root, see also *gēz , Gothic 𐌲𐌹𐍃𐍄𐍂𐌰𐌳𐌰𐌲𐌹𐍃 ( gistradagis ) and Proto-West Germanic *gesteran ( “ yesterday ” ) , all deriving from Proto-Indo-European *dʰǵʰyés .
