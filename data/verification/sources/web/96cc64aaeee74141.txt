 Wikipedia fa 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Inherited from Middle Persian 𐭱𐭲𐭥𐭩𐭥𐭥 ( Šahrēwar , “ mere rule ” ) ; name of the third Amahraspand (divine deity) in Zoroastrianism . Compare شهر ( šahr , “ city ” ) .

 شهریور • ( šahrēwar  / šahrivar ) ( Tajik spelling шаҳревар or шаҳривар ) 

 ( Iran ) Shahrivar ( the sixth solar month of the Persian calendar ) 

 Name of the fourth day of any month of the solar Persian calendar.
