 English Wikipedia has an article on: jupe (jacket) 

 Wikipedia 

 Etymology 1
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle English jupe , from Middle French jupe . Doublet of jubbah .

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 enPR : zho͞op , IPA ( key ) : /ʒuːp/ 

 Audio ( Southern England ) : ( file ) 

 Rhymes: -uːp 

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 jupe ( plural jupes ) 

 A style of skirt .

 A style of short jacket , usually for a woman or child .
 1936 , Norman Lindsay , The Flyaway Highway , Sydney: Angus and Robertson, page 55 : [T]he people all wore hosen and shoon and smocks and jupes and jerkins and wimples.

 Etymology 2
 [ edit ] 

 Apparently named after an EFnet user called Jupiter who did this to NickServ 

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 enPR : jo͞op , IPA ( key ) : /d͡ʒuːp/ 

 Audio ( Southern England ) : ( file ) 

 Rhymes: -uːp 

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 jupe ( plural jupes ) 

 ( IRC ) A block placed on a server , nickname or channel .

 Verb
 [ edit ] 

 jupe ( third-person singular simple present jupes , present participle juping , simple past and past participle juped ) 

 ( IRC ) To block a server (from joining the network ) or a nickname or channel (from being used).

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 jupe   f ( plural jupes ) 

 skirt 
 1994 , Michel Houellebecq , Extension du domaine de la lutte : On était une bonne trentaine, rien que des cadres moyens âgés de vint-cinq à quarante ans. À un moment donné il y a une connasse qui a commencé à se déshabiller. Ella a ôté son T-shirt, puis son soutien-gorge, puis sa jupe , tout ça en faisant des mines incroyables. There were a good thirty of us, all middle-ranking executives aged between twenty-five and forty. At one point, some stupid bitch started undressing. She took off her T-shirt, then her bra, then her skirt , all while pulling the most incredible faces.
