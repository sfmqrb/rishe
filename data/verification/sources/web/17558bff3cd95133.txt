 Kalami
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Possibly from Sanskrit वाम॑ ( vā́ma , “ left side ” ) .

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 Tone class: H

 IPA ( key ) : /báːm/ 

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 بام ( bām )  m 

 side , direction 

 References
 [ edit ] 

 Baart, Joan L. G. ( 1997 ), “ bām ”, in The sounds and tones of Kalam Kohistani: with wordlist and texts (Studies in Languages of Northern Pakistan; 1)‎ [1] , National Institute of Pakistan Studies, Quaid-i-Azam University; Summer Institute of Linguistics, page 79 

 Pashto
 [ edit ] 

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 IPA ( key ) : /bɑm/ 

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 بام • ( bām )  m 

 top of the house 

 roof 
 "Sandara"
 ته چې په بام بندې ولاړه وې ته چا ته کتل. خلک زما په ننداره وو چې ما تا ته کتل When you [feminine] were standing on the roof, who were you looking at ? People were observing my spectacle that I was looking at you.

 Etymology 1
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology tree

 Proto-Indo-European *wes- der. 
 Middle Persian bʾn 
 Persian بام 

 From Middle Persian [script needed] ( bʾn /⁠bān⁠/ , “ roof, rooftop ” ) , ultimately from Proto-Indo-European *wes- ( “ to clothe, wear, put on ” ) . Compare Avestan 𐬬𐬀𐬢𐬵𐬀𐬥𐬀 ( vaŋhana ) , 𐬬𐬀𐬢𐬵𐬁𐬥𐬀 ( vaŋhāna , “ cover ” ) .

 Etymology 2
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology tree

 Proto-Indo-European *bʰeh₂- 

 Proto-Indo-European *-mos 

 Proto-Indo-Iranian *bʰáHmas 
 Middle Persian bʾm ( /⁠bām⁠/ ) 
 Persian بام 

 Inherited from Middle Persian [Book Pahlavi needed] ( bʾm /⁠bām⁠/ ) , [Manichaean needed] ( bʾm /⁠bām⁠/ ) , from Proto-Iranian *báHmas , from Proto-Indo-Iranian *bʰáHmas ( “ light ” ) , ultimately from Proto-Indo-European *bʰeh₂- . [ 1 ] 

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 Dari

 بام 

 Iranian Persian

 Tajik

 бом 

 بام • ( bām / bâm ) ( Tajik spelling бом ) 

 dawn 

 gleam , sheen , shimmer , tinge , hue , color 
 Alternative forms: فام ( fâm ) , پام ( pâm ) , وام ( wâm ) 
 سپید بام ― sepid bâm ― of a whitish colour 
 گل‌فام ― gol-fâm ― rosy 
 ستاره بام ― setâre bâm ― Lucifer; the morning star
