 Sanskrit गोप 

 From गो ( go , “ cow; earth ” ) +‎ -प ( -pa , “ protector ” ) .

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 ( Vedic ) IPA ( key ) : /ɡɐu̯.pɐ́/ 

 ( Classical Sanskrit ) IPA ( key ) : /ɡoː.pɐ/ 

 Hyphenation: गो‧प 

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 गो॒प • ( gopá ) stem ,  m ( female equivalent गोपी ) 

 cowherd , herdsman , milkman 
 Synonym: गोपाल ( gopāla ) 
 c. 800 CE – 950 CE , Nārāyaṇa , Hitopadeśa 2.112.15: सा च गोपी तेन गोपेन पुनः पृष्टोवाच अरे पाप को मां महासती विरूपयितुं समर्थः । sā ca gopī tena gopena punaḥ pṛṣṭovāca are pāpa ko māṃ mahāsatī virūpayituṃ samarthaḥ. And that cowherdess, the highly virtuous woman, asked the cowherd , O wicked one, who has the power to defile me? 

 protector , guardian 
 c. 1500 BCE – 1000 BCE , Ṛgveda 5.62.9 : यद्बंहि॑ष्ठं॒ नाति॒विधे॑ सुदानू॒ अच्छि॑द्रं॒ शर्म॑ भुवनस्य गोपा ।
 तेन॑ नो मित्रावरुणावविष्टं॒ सिषा॑सन्तो जिगी॒वांसः॑ स्याम ॥ yádbáṃhiṣṭhaṃ nā́tivídhe sudānū ácchidraṃ śárma bhuvanasya gopā .
 téna no mitrāvaruṇāvaviṣṭaṃ síṣāsanto jigīvā́ṃsaḥ syāma. Bountiful guardians of the world! the shelter that is impenetrable, strongest, flawless, Aid us with that, O Varuṇa and Mitra, and when we long to win may we be victors.
