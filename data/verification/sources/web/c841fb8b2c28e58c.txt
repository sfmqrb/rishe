 Ancient Greek
 [ edit ] 

 Alternative forms
 [ edit ] 

 σᾶμᾰ ( sâmă ) — Doric 

 σῆμ’ ( sêm’ ) — apocopic 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Proto-Indo-European *dʰyéh₂mn̥ , from *dʰyeh₂- ( “ to notice ” ) (see Proto-Indo-European *dʰeyh₂- ), although Beekes is skeptical of the semantics. Cognate with Sanskrit ध्यायति ( dhyāyati , “ think of, imagine ” ) , ध्यान ( dhyāna , “ meditation ” ) . [ 1 ]
