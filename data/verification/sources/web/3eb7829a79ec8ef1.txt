 Persian
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology 1
 [ edit ] 

 Perhaps ultimately related to the root of Old Armenian ճաղատ ( čałat , “ bare, bald ” ) . [ 1 ] Of dubious connection to Sanskrit कुण्ठ ( kuṇṭhá , “ blunt, dull ” ) .
