 This Proto-Germanic entry contains reconstructed terms and roots . As such, the term(s) in this entry are not directly attested , but are hypothesized to have existed based on comparative evidence.

 Proto-Germanic
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Proto-Indo-European *ǵyewh₁- ( “ to chew ” ) ; compare Tocharian B śuwaṃ ( “ to eat ” ) , Polish żuć ( “ to chew ” ) , Persian جویدن ( javidan ) , Pashto [script needed] ( žovạl , “ to bite, gnaw ” ) . [ 1 ] 

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 IPA ( key ) : /ˈkew.wɑ.nɑ̃/ 

 Rhymes: -ew.wɑ.nɑ̃ 

 Verb
 [ edit ] 

 *kewwaną [ 1 ] [ 2 ] 

 to chew
