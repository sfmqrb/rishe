 This Proto-Germanic entry contains reconstructed terms and roots . As such, the term(s) in this entry are not directly attested , but are hypothesized to have existed based on comparative evidence.

 Proto-Germanic
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Cognate with Proto-Italic *haidos , but cannot be traced back to Proto-Indo-European. [ 1 ] According to Bjørn, from the same source as Proto-Semitic *gady- , Proto-Berber *e-ɣăyd , Proto-Nakh *gaaʒaᶰ . [ 2 ] 

 See also *kittīną ( “ young animal ” ) and *kidją ( “ goatling ” ) .
