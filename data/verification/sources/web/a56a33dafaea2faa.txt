 Persian
 [ edit ] 

 Alternative forms
 [ edit ] 

 اومدن ( umadan ) ( dialectal , Tehrani ) 

 آمادن ( âmâdan ) ( dialectal , Herati , Hazaragi ) 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Proto-Iranian *ā- + *gam- ( “ to come ” ) , from Proto-Indo-European *h₂éd ( “ to, at ” ) + *gʷem- ( “ to walk, to step, to come ” ) ; [ 1 ] [ 2 ] [ 3 ] exact cognate with English advent , and partial cognate with English come . The present stem comes from Proto-Indo-European *h₁ey- ( “ to go ” ) .

 Verb
 [ edit ] 

 آمدن • ( āmadan  / âmadan ) ( present stem آ or آی , Tajik spelling омадан ) 

 to come , to arrive 
 فردا به تهران می‌آییم. fardâ be tehrân mi-âyim . We' re coming to Tehran tomorrow. 

 to become
