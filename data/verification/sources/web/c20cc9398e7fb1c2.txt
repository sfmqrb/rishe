 Ottoman Turkish
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Borrowed from Classical Persian مُژْدَه ( mužda , “ good news ” ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 مژده • ( müjde ) ( definite accusative مژده‌یی ( müjdeyi ) , plural مژده‌لر ( müjdeler ) ) 

 good news , glad tidings , something or someone pleasant , fortunate , or otherwise positive 
 Synonym: تباشیر ( tebâşir )

 [ edit ]

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Borrowed from Parthian 𐫖𐫇𐫋𐫅𐫃 ( mwjdg /⁠muždag⁠/ , “ (good) news, message ” ) , from Proto-Iranian *miždám , from Proto-Indo-Iranian *miždʰám , from Proto-Indo-European *misdʰós . Related to مُزْد ( muzd ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 مژده • ( mužda / možde ) ( Tajik spelling мужда ) 

 good news ; joyful tidings 
 Synonyms: نوید ( nawīd  / navid ) , بشارت ( bišārat  / bešârat ) 
 1975 , “ منتظر [ Awaiter ]”, performed by Googoosh: درا رو وا می‌کنم، پنجره‌ها رو می‌شکنم
 مژده دیدنشو، تو کوچه‌ها جار می‌زنم darâ ro vâ mikonam, panjere-hâ ro mišekanam
 možde -ye didanešo, tu kuče-hâ jâr mizanam I'll open the doors, I'll break the windows
The good news of seeing him I will proclaim in the alleys 

 gladness ; joy

 Proper noun
 [ edit ] 

 مژده • ( mužda  / možde ) ( Tajik spelling Мужда ) 

 a female given name , Mojdeh , Mozhdeh , Muzhda , and Mozhda , from Parthian
