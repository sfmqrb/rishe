 Alternative forms
 [ edit ] 

 افریدون ( afridun ) , آفریدون ( âfridun ) ( archaic ) 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian plytwn' ( Frēdōn ) , from Proto-Iranian *Θraitauna- , from Proto-Indo-Iranian . Cognate to Avestan 𐬚𐬭𐬀𐬉𐬙𐬀𐬊𐬥𐬀 ( θraētaona ) , Lurish فرێدۆ ( ferıdø ) . Akin to Old Armenian Հրուդէն ( Hrudēn ) , a Parthian loanword.

 Proper noun
 [ edit ] 

 فریدون • ( feraydun ) 

 ( Iranian mythology ) Fereydun / Fredon , Thraitauna / Thraetaona 

 a male given name , Faredun , Fareydun , Feridun , Firedun , Faredoon , and Firedoon , from Middle Persian 
 c. 977-1010 , ابوالقاسم فردوسی توسی [ Abū l-Qāsim Firdawsī Tūsī ], “Zahhák”, in Arthur George Warner and Edmond Warner, transl., شاهنامه [ šāhnāma , Shahnameh ]‎ [1] , published 1905 , The Birth of Farídún: بر آمد برین روزگار دراز
که شد اژدهافش به تنگی فراز
خجسته فریدون ر مادر بزاد
جهان را یکی دیگر آمد نهاد
 [translit?] Years passed away, calamity approached
The dragon-king, the blesséd Farídún 
Was born, the fashion of the world was changed. 

 a surname 

 Name of several cities in Iran .
