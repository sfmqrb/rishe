 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Inherited from Middle Persian [script needed] ( swkndl /⁠Skandar ~ Sokandar⁠/ ) , [ 1 ] [ 2 ] from Arabic إِسْكَنْدَر ( ʔiskandar ) , from Ancient Greek Ἀλέξανδρος ( Aléxandros ) .

 Proper noun
 [ edit ] 

 اِسْکَنْدَر • ( iskandar  / eskandar ) ( Tajik spelling Искандар ) 

 a male given name , Eskandar and Iskandar , from Arabic [in turn from Ancient Greek], equivalent to English Alexander 
 اسکندر مقدونی ― iskandar-i maqdūnī  / eskandar-e maġduni ― Alexander the Great (literally, “ Alexander the Macedonian ”)
