 Reconstruction notes
 [ edit ] 

 Appears like an s -stem from *neh₂- , but there is little other evidence for such a root.

 If the stem-final *-s- is considered part of the root, this noun is a root noun and its inflection is amphikinetic . If on the other hand the root is seen as *neh₂- and *-(e)s- as the suffix, one may reconstruct either proterokinetic *néh₂-s-s ~ *n̥h₂-és-s or amphikinetic/hysterokinetic *néh₂-s-s ~ *n̥h₂-s-és ; the latter is formally identical to the root noun as presented here. Compare *HóHs ( “ mouth ” ) for a similar problem.
