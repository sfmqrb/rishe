 This Proto-Germanic entry contains reconstructed terms and roots . As such, the term(s) in this entry are not directly attested , but are hypothesized to have existed based on comparative evidence.

 Proto-Germanic
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Uncertain. Appears to stem from a Proto-Indo-European *(s)krewk- ( “ heap, hill; back, spine? ” ) , with potential cognates listed at Latin crux ( “ cross (frame, symbol) ” ) , though this is highly speculative. [ 1 ] Whether the hypothetical root is a derivative of *(s)ker- ( “ to turn, bend ” ) is far from clear.
