 Persian
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Inherited from Middle Persian ʾylmʾn' ( ērmān ) , derived from Avestan 𐬀𐬌𐬭𐬌𐬌𐬇𐬨𐬁 ( a i riiə̄mā , “ fellow tribesman; name of a divinity ” ) , from Proto-Iranian *aryamā́ , ultimately from Proto-Indo-Iranian *aryamā́ ( “ fellow, companion; member of one's own tribe/clan; name of a deity ” ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ]

 guest 

 religious leader , cleric
