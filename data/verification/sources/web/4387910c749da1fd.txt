 Etymology
 [ edit ]

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 رفو • ( rafu ) 

 finedrawing , darning 

 mending 
 c. 1260s , Jalāl ad-Dīn Mohammad Rūmī , translated by Reynold A. Nicholson , مثنوی معنوی [ Masnavi-ye-Ma'navi ], volume I, verse 3882: چون شکسته بند آمد دست او
پس رفو باشد یقین اشکست او čōn šikasta band āmad dast-i ō
bas rafū bāšad yaqīn aškast-i ō Inasmuch as His hand binds what is broken,
it follows that His breaking is assuredly mending .
