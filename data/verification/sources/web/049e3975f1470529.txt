 Persian
 [ edit ] 

 Alternative forms
 [ edit ] 

 گروس ( gorus ) 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian [Book Pahlavi needed] ( wls /⁠wars⁠/ , “ hair ” ) , from Proto-Iranian *wálśah , cognate with Avestan 𐬬𐬀𐬭𐬆𐬯𐬀 ( var ə sa , “ hair (on the head) ” ) , Sogdian 𐼴𐽀𐼼 ( wrs /⁠wars⁠/ , “ hair ” ) and Northern Kurdish pirç ( pirč , “ hair on the head ” ) ; from Proto-Indo-Iranian *wálćas , cognate with Sanskrit वल्श ( válśa , “ twig, sprout ” ) ; from Proto-Indo-European *wólḱ-o-s , cognate with Proto-Slavic *volsъ ( “ hair ” ) . Akin to Old Armenian վարս ( vars , “ hair ” ) and վարշամակ ( varšamak , “ handkerchief; headband ” ) , which are Iranian borrowings.

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 گرس • ( gors ) 

 curl , ringlet 

 braid
