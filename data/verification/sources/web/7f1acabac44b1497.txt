 Arabic
 [ edit ] 

 بَاب 

 Root 

 ب و ب ( b w b ) 

 5 terms 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Usually considered a borrowing from Aramaic בָּבָא ( bāḇā , “ gate ” ) , from Akkadian 𒆍 ( bābum , “ gate ” ) , particularly since the Arabs, living in tents, did not need doors. However, Hans Bauer considers all of these words native, explaining the formation as an ancient univerbation of etyma equivalent to Arabic بَاءَ ( bāʔa ) بِ ( bi ) “he has entered into (the house)”.

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 بَاب • ( bāb )  m ( plural أَبْوَاب ( ʔabwāb ) or بِيبَان ( bībān ) ) 

 door , gate 
 609–632 CE , Qur'an , 2:58 : وَادْخُلُوا الْبَابَ سُجَّدًا وَقُولُوا حِطَّةٌ wādḵulū al-bāba sujjadan waqūlū ḥiṭṭatun [And] enter the gate with humility [...] 

 opening , gateway 

 entrance 

 gullet , weasand 
 c. 1200 , يحيى بن محمد بن أحمد بن العوام [ yaḥyā ibn muḥammad ibn ʔaḥmad ibn al-ʕawwām ], edited by José Antonio Banqueri , كتاب الفلاحة [ Book on Agriculture ], volume 2, Madrid: Imprenta Real, published 1802 IA , Cap. 33, Art. 10 , page 677 line 6 : وقد تخرج للدابة من حنكها ويسمي التحنيك وصفة العمل في ذلك إذا أحنكت الدابة فأفتح لها من الدرجة الثالثة والرابعة ولا تفتح لها قرب الباب لأن الدم ربما عسر إمساكه فتلف الدابة. One may extract the blood from the jaw of a beast and it is called taḥnīk and the operation consists in that you have bound the beast with a chinstrap and now cut it open to the third or fourth degree but don’t open it close the throat as it may be difficult to contain the blood and the beast will succumb. 

 opportunity , a chance , opening 

 military tactic , maneuver , an opening to use a strategy 

 chapter , section , column 

 group , class , category 

 ( astronomy ) appearance of an astronomical body , a juncture , an opening to observe 

 ( figurative ) domain , field

 chapter (of a written work)

 ( archaic , original sense ) door , gate 
 Synonym: در ( dar ) 
 c. 1260s , Jalāl ad-Dīn Mohammad Rūmī , translated by Reynold A. Nicholson , مثنوی معنوی [ Masnavi-ye-Ma'navi ], volume I, verse 3763: چون تو باب ی آن مدینه‌‌ی علم را
چون شعاعی آفتاب حلم را čūn tū bāb -ī ān madīna-yi ilm rā
čūn šu'ā'-ī āftāb-i hilm rā Since thou art the gate of the city of Knowledge,
since thou art the beams of the sun of Clemency.

 a transliteration of the English male given name diminutive Bob

 باب دیلن ― bâb dilen ― Bob Dylan
