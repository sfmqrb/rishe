 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 शिफा • ( śiphā ) stem ,  f 

 a fibrous or flexible root (used for making whips )

 a lash or stroke with a whip or rod 

 a branch 

 a river 

 a tuft of hair on the crown of the head 

 the root of a waterlily 

 spikenard 

 turmeric 

 a sort of dill or fennel
