 This Proto-Indo-European entry contains reconstructed terms and roots . As such, the term(s) in this entry are not directly attested , but are hypothesized to have existed based on comparative evidence.

 Proto-Indo-European

 [ edit ] 

 *per- 

 before , in front 

 first

 Root
 [ edit ] 

 *per- [ 9 ] ( perhaps related to previous root, as "front" > "go forth" or vice versa ) 

 to go through 

 ? to pierce 

 to carry forth , fare 

 Reconstruction notes
 [ edit ] 

 See also *perh₃- ( “ to beget ” ) , often confused with this root, and perhaps conflated with or related to it.
