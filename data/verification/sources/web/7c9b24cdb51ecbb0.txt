 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian 𐭰𐭩𐭡𐭠 ( CYBA /⁠ēzm⁠/ ) from Proto-Indo-European *h₂eydʰ- ( “ to burn; fire ” ) . Compare Talysh ایزم ( izəm ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 هیزم • ( hēzum / hizom ) ( plural هیزم‌ها ( hēzum-hā / hizom-hâ ) , Tajik spelling ҳезум ) 

 firewood 
 Synonym: هیمه ( heyme ) 
 c. 1260s , Jalāl ad-Dīn Mohammad Rūmī , translated by Reynold A. Nicholson , مثنوی معنوی [ Masnavi-ye-Ma'navi ], volume I, verse 107: رنجش از صفرا و از سودا نبود
بوی هر هیزم پدید آید ز دود
 ranj-aš az safrā u az sawdā nabūd. bōy har hēzum padīd āyad z dūd. Her pain was not from yellow or black bile: the smell of every firewood appears from the smoke. ( Classical Persian transliteration )
