 Arabic
 [ edit ] 

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 نُمْرُق or نِمْرِق or نَمْرَق • ( numruq or nimriq or namraq )  m ( plural نَمَارِق ( namāriq ) ) 

 alternative form of نمرقة ( “ cushion , bolster for sitting ” )
