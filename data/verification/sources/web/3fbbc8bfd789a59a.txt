 Persian انجیل 

 Borrowed from Arabic إِنْجِيل ( ʔinjīl ) . Doublet of انگلیون ( angalyūn  / angalyun , “ Gospel ” ) , borrowed directly from Syriac, which this Arabic word displaced.
