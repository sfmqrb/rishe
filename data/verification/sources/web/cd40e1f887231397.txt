 Arabic بلور 

 Borrowed from Classical Persian بِلّور ( billōr ) , from Middle Persian bylwl ( /⁠*bēlūr⁠/ ) , borrowed from Pali veḷuriya , from Proto-Dravidian *weḷ-ūr ( name of a city ) + Pali -iya .
