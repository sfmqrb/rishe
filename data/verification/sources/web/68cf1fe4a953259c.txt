 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 IPA ( key ) : /za.ka.rij.jaː/ 

 Proper noun
 [ edit ] 

 زَكَرِيَّا • ( zakariyyā )  m 

 ( Islam ) Zakariya , father of John the Baptist as an Islamic prophet 
 609–632 CE , Qur'an , 19:2 : ذِكْرُ رَحْمَتِ رَبِّكَ عَبْدَهُۥ زَكَرِيَّا ḏikru raḥmati rabbika ʕabdahū zakariyyā ˹This is˺ a reminder of your Lord’s mercy to His servant Zachariah , 

 ( biblical ) Zechariah , Zachariah , father of John the Baptist in biblical contexts 

 Zakariya , a male given name , equivalent to English Zachary
