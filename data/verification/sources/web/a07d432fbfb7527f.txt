 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Inherited from Middle Persian [Book Pahlavi needed] ( gc /⁠gač⁠/ , “ plaster, gypsum ” ) , ultimately from Akkadian 𒅎𒌓 ( IM.BABBAR /⁠gaṣṣu⁠/ , “ gypsum ” ) . Compare Khotanese [script needed] ( gatsä , “ gypsum ” ) , Arabic جَصّ ( jaṣṣ , “ plaster ” ) , Classical Syriac ܓܨܐ ( gaṣṣā , “ plaster ” ) from the same source.

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 گچ • ( gač ) ( plural گچ‌ها ( gač-hā / gač-hâ ) , Tajik spelling гач ) 

 plaster , gypsum , chalk , stucco 

 ( orthopaedics ) plaster cast , orthopaedic cast
