 Ottoman Turkish
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology tree

 Middle Persian yʾmk' 
 Classical Persian جامه ( jâme ) bor. 
 Ottoman Turkish جامه 

 Borrowed from Classical Persian جامه ( jâme ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 جامه • ( came ) 

 a cloth, any woven material; a length of cloth

 a sheet

 a wrapper, or a wrap

 a garment, raiment

 a cup, bowl, goblet

 Related terms
 [ edit ] 

 جامه‌شوی ( cameşuy , “ laundry; laundryman ” ) 

 جماشیر ( çamaşır , “ underclothes; linen; laundry ” ) 

 پاجامه ( pacame ) 

 Further reading
 [ edit ] 

 Redhouse, James W. ( 1890 ), “ جامه ”, in A Turkish and English Lexicon ‎ [1] , Constantinople: A. H. Boyajian, page 637 

 Persian
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Inherited from Middle Persian yʾmk' ( jāmag ) , from Proto-Iranian *yām- ( “ to hold ” ) , from Proto-Indo-Iranian *yámas , *yam- ( “ to hold ” ) , from Proto-Indo-European *yém-o-s , from *yem- ( “ to hold ” ) , Northern Luri جۆمںە ( cømə ) , see also Sanskrit यम् ( yam , “ to sustain ” ) . Compare Old Armenian վարշամակ ( varšamak ) .
