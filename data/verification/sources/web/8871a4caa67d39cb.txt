 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian 𐭦𐭪𐭫 ( ZKL ) , 𐭭𐭫 ( nl /⁠nar⁠/ , “ male ” ) , from Proto-Indo-Iranian *Hnā́ , from Proto-Indo-European *h₂nḗr .

 Cognate with Pashto نر ( nar, nër ) , Ossetian нӕл ( næl ) , Avestan 𐬥𐬀𐬭 ( nar ) , and Sanskrit नर ( nara ) .
