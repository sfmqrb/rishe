 [ edit ]

 Proper noun
 [ edit ] 

 Latvia 

 A country in northeastern Europe . Official name: Republic of Latvia . Capital: Riga .
 Synonym: Lettland 
 2005 , Bill Clinton , My Life ‎ [1] , volume II, New York : Vintage Books , →ISBN , →OCLC , page 185 : In early July, I returned to Europe for the G-7 summit in Naples. On the way, I stopped in Riga, Latvia , to meet with the leaders of the Baltic states and celebrate the withdrawal of Russian troops from Lithuania and Latvia , a move we had helped to speed up by providing a large number of housing vouchers for Russian officers who wanted to go home.

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Internationalism , based on Latvian Latvija ( “ Latvia ” ) . The Latvian name is a 19th-century formation from latvis ( “ Latvian, Lett ” ) and the country-name suffix -ija . It is first recorded in a Latvian text in 1862 and became established later in the century.

 The underlying ethnonym is considerably older, but its deeper origin is uncertain. A connection with a local river name has been proposed.

 Compare Norwegian Nynorsk Latvia , English Latvia , Finnish Latvia , Lithuanian Latvija , Serbo-Croatian Latvija and Slovene Latvija ( “ Latvia ” ) .

 The official Norwegian long-form name is Republikken Latvia (“the Republic of Latvia”).
