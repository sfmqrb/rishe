 This Proto-Indo-European entry contains reconstructed terms and roots . As such, the term(s) in this entry are not directly attested , but are hypothesized to have existed based on comparative evidence.

 Proto-Indo-European
 [ edit ] 

 English Wikipedia has an article on: Indo-European copula 

 Wikipedia 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Probably related to *h₁eh₁s- ( “ to sit ” ) . According to the EIEC, the root *h₁es- may have originally meant "to sit" before eventually coming to mean "to be." For a semantic relationship between the meanings "to be" and "to sit," the EIEC compares Spanish ser ( “ to be ” ) , which has forms deriving from Latin esse ( “ to be ” ) and from Latin sedeō ( “ to sit ” ) . [ 1 ]
