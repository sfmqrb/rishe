 Etymology 1
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian kl ( karr , “ deaf ” ) . (This etymology is missing or incomplete. Please add to it, or discuss it at the Etymology scriptorium . Particularly: “OK, now explain the etymon of THAT”) Likely related to Sanskrit कर्ण ( karṇa , “ ear ” ) .
