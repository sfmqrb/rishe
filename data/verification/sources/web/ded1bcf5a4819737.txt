 Old Armenian
 [ edit ] 

 Alternative forms
 [ edit ] 

 տուրժ ( turž ) 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 An Iranian borrowing; compare Parthian 𐫤𐫇𐫋- ( twj- /⁠tōž-⁠/ , “ to pay back ” ) , Middle Persian [Book Pahlavi needed] ( twc- /⁠tōz-⁠/ , “ to atone, expiate ” ) , Persian توختن ( tuxtan , “ to pay a debt ” ) . Doublet of տուգան ( tugan ) . Related to Old Georgian ტოზიკი ( ṭoziḳi ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 տոյժ • ( toyž ) 

 sanction , penalty 

 Usage notes
 [ edit ] 

 Usually used in the plural.
