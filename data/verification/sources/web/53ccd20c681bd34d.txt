 Gojri
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Borrowed from Classical Persian امید ( umed, umīd, ummed, ummīd ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 اُمید ( umed )  f 

 hope , optimism , faith , anticipation 

 Derived terms
 [ edit ]

 hope

 Proper noun
 [ edit ] 

 امید • ( umēd  / omid or ummēd  / ommid ) ( Tajik spelling Умед or Уммед ) 

 a male given name , Omid , Umed , Umid , and Ummed , from Middle Persian
