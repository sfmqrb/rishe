 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Inherited from Middle Persian 𐭮𐭩𐭭𐭬𐭥𐭫𐭥 ( synmwlw /⁠sēn-murw⁠/ ) . [ 1 ] According to folk etymology a compound of سی ( sī  / si , “ thirty ” ) and مرغ ( murġ  / morġ , “ bird ” ) . See also The Speech of the Birds .
