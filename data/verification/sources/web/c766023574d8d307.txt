 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian (compare Manichaean Middle Persian nrymʾn ( narēmān ) ), from Avestan 𐬥𐬀𐬌𐬭𐬈⸱𐬨𐬀𐬥𐬀𐬢𐬵 ( na i re.manaŋh , “ manly-hearted ” ) , compound of 𐬥𐬀𐬌𐬭𐬌𐬌𐬀 ( na i riia , “ brave, manly ” ) and 𐬨𐬀𐬥𐬀𐬢𐬵 ( manaŋh , “ thought, mind ” ) .

 Proper noun
 [ edit ] 

 نریمان • ( narimân ) 

 a male given name , Nariman , Nareeman , Narreeman , Narriman , and Neriman , from Avestan 

 Nariman , character in the Shahnama , father of Sam, grandfather of Rostam
