 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Inherited from Ottoman Turkish كاغد ( ḱāğıd ) [ 1 ] , from Persian کاغذ ( kâġaz , “ paper ” ) [ 2 ] , from Proto-Common Turkic [Term?] (compare Karakhanid [script needed] ( kāɣïd , “ paper ” ) ), ultimately from Sogdian 𐼸𐼰𐼲𐼹𐼰 ( kʾγδʾ /⁠kāγaδā⁠/ ) ; further etymology is unknown, perhaps a borrowing from Old Chinese 紙 ( OC *kjeʔ ).

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 kâğıt ( definite accusative kâğıdı , plural kâğıtlar ) 

 paper 

 a piece of written paper, paper with writing on it

 exam paper 

 playing card 
 Synonyms: iskambil kâğıdı , iskambil , kart , oyun kâğıdı 

 document , official paper

 financial security (tradable instrument), e.g. bonds , shares 
 Synonym: menkul kıymetler 

 paper money 
 Synonyms: kâğıt para , banknot , kayme , ( dated ) kaime 
 Bu defter kaç kağıt ? How much does this notebook cost ?
