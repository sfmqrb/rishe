 Persian
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian [Book Pahlavi needed] ( SDKWN-tn', dl-ytn' /⁠darridan, darr-⁠/ , “ to split, to tear ” ) , [ 1 ] Northern Luri دڕنیێ ( derrnyı , “ to split ” ) from Proto-Indo-Iranian *dar- , ultimately from Proto-Indo-European *der- ( “ to tear, split ” ) . Cognate with English tear and Ancient Greek δέρω ( dérō , “ I skin, I flay ” ) .

 Verb
 [ edit ] 

 دریدن • ( daridan ) ( present stem در ( dar ) ) 

 to split , tear , rip 

 to bite (of an animal)

 to ravage , attack
