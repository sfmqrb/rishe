 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 IPA ( key ) : /hid͡ʒ.ra/ 

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 هِجْرَة • ( hijra )  f 

 migration , emigration , immigration 

 departure , exit 

 renunciation , abandonment 

 Hijra , Hegira : the flight of the prophet Muhammad and his followers from Mecca to Yathrib ( later Medina ) in 622 CE
