 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 فنجان • ( fincan ) ( definite accusative فنجانی ( fincanı ) , plural فنجانلر ( fincanlar ) ) 

 cup , a concave vessel for drinking, usually made of opaque material and with a handle 
 Synonym: طاس ( tas )
