 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Transliteration of Avestan 𐬵𐬀𐬊𐬨𐬀 ( haoma ) . Doublet of soma .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 haoma ( uncountable ) 

 ( Zoroastrianism ) A plant associated with the divinity Haoma .
 1999 , Karel van der Toorn, Bob Becking, Pieter Willem van der Horst, Dictionary of Deities and Demons in the Bible: DDD , page 384 : The offering of haoma was a common practice among Achaemenids; the Haumavarga Scythians probably owe their name to the fact that they did not press the plant but strewed it ritually (HOFFMANN 1975).
