 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian dmbʾwnd ( dumbāwand ) .

 This etymology is missing or incomplete. Please add to it, or discuss it at the Etymology scriptorium . 
Particularly: “Wikipedia gives the original interpretation as "the mountain from which smoke and ash arises", albeit without any references. In this case, the first component is from Proto-Indo-Iranian *dʰuHmás ( “ smoke ” ) . What would the second component be? A derivative of Proto-Indo-European *bʰewg- ( “ to release ” ) (where would the -nd come from?) or something else?. Compare Talysh band ( “ mountain ” ) .”
