 Old Anatolian Turkish
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology 1
 [ edit ] 

 Inherited from Proto-Turkic *nē- 

 Pronoun
 [ edit ] 

 نَه • ( nä ) 

 ( interrogative ) what 

 ( fused relative ) that which, the thing that.
 دَدَه قُرْقُوتْ صُويْلَامِشْ، كُرَلُمْ خَانُمْ نَه صُويْلَامِشْ: [...] dede ḳorḳut soylamış, görälüm ḫānum nä soylamış: [...] Dede Korkut said, let us see, my lord, what he said: [...]
