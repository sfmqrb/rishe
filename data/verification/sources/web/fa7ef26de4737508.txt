 This Proto-Germanic entry contains reconstructed terms and roots . As such, the term(s) in this entry are not directly attested , but are hypothesized to have existed based on comparative evidence.

 Proto-Germanic
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Proto-Indo-European *dʰrógʰ-e-ti , traditionally derived from *dʰregʰ- ( “ to pull, draw, drag ” ) (though Kroonen is skeptical and leaves the origin open). [ 1 ] 

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 IPA ( key ) : /ˈdrɑ.ɣɑ.nɑ̃/ 

 Rhymes: -ɑ.ɣɑ.nɑ̃ 

 Verb
 [ edit ] 

 *draganą [ 1 ] [ 2 ] [ 3 ] 

 to draw , to pull , to carry
