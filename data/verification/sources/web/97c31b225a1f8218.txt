 Persian
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology tree

 Proto-Indo-European *yúHs 
 Middle Persian [script needed] ( ašmā ) der. 
 Persian شما 

 From Middle Persian ʾšmʾ, ʿšmʾ ( ašmā, išmā , “ you ” ) , from Proto-Indo-European *yúHs ( “ you (plural) ” ) . Compare Southern Luri ایسا ( īsâ , “ you ” ) and Bakhtiari ایسا ( īsâ , “ you ” ) .
