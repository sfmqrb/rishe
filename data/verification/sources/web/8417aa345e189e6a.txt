 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From 𐭩𐭦𐭣𐭪𐭫𐭲𐭩 ( yzdklty ) / Middle Persian 𐭩𐭦𐭣𐭪𐭥𐭲𐭩 ( Yazdegerd, Yazdgird , literally “ made by God ” ) , from 𐭩𐭦𐭣 ( Yazd ) + 𐭪𐭥𐭲𐭩 ( -gird, -kirt ) .

 Proper noun
 [ edit ] 

 Yazdegerd 

 A transliteration of the Middle Persian male given name 𐭩𐭦𐭣𐭪𐭫𐭲𐭩 ( yzdklty ) or 𐭩𐭦𐭣𐭪𐭥𐭲𐭩 ( Yazdegerd, Yazdgird ) , notably borne by several Sassanid kings .
