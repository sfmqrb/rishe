 English
 [ edit ] 

 emblic 
 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Medieval Latin emblicus , from Arabic أَمْلَج ( ʔamlaj ) , from Sanskrit आमलक ( āmalaka ) . Doublet of amla .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 emblic ( plural emblics ) 

 The fruit of Emblica officinalis (now Phyllanthus emblica , the Indian gooseberry , once used as a remedy against dysentery )

 Synonyms
 [ edit ] 

 ( Emblica officinalis ) : amla , Indian gooseberry
