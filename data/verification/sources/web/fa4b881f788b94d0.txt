 Persian
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian [Book Pahlavi needed] ( nkwn' /⁠nigūn⁠/ ) , which may be akin to Sanskrit न्यञ्च् ( nyañc , “ directed downwards ” ) . [ 1 ] Akin to Old Armenian նկուն ( nkun ) , an Iranian borrowing.

 Adjective
 [ edit ] 

 نگون • ( negun ) 

 turned , inverted , hanging down
 c. 1260s , Jalāl ad-Dīn Mohammad Rūmī , translated by Reynold A. Nicholson , مثنوی معنوی [ Masnavi-ye-Ma'navi ], volume III, verse 1145: عقل جزوی گاه چیره گه نگون 
عقل کلی آمن از ریب المنون aql-i juzwī gāh čīra gah nigūn 
aql-i kullī āmin az raybu l-manūn The particular intellect is sometimes dominant, sometimes overthrown ;
 the Universal Intellect is secure from the mischances of Time. 

 upside down
