 Ottoman Turkish
 [ edit ] 

 سِپَر 
 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Borrowed from Classical Persian سِپَر ( sipar , “ shield ” ) , from Middle Persian spl ( spar , “ shield ” ) , from Old Persian *sparah .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 سپر • ( siper ) 

 shield , a broad piece of defensive armor held in hand 
 Synonyms: ترس ( türs ) , درقه ( daraka ) , قلقان ( kalkan ) 

 shield , anything that acts as a protection , or guard 

 ( printing ) side smudge of ink made by type 

 Derived terms
 [ edit ] 

 سپرجك ( sipercik , “ small shield ” ) 
 سپردار ( siperdar , “ shield-bearer ” ) 
 سپرلنمك ( siperlanmak , “ to become a shield ” ) 

 Descendants
 [ edit ]

 Inherited from Middle Persian spl ( spar , “ shield ” ) , from Old Persian *sparah . Akin to Old Armenian ասպար ( aspar ) , an Iranian borrowing.
