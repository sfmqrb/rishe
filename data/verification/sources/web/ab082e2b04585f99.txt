 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Vulgar Latin *smaralda , *smaraldus , *smaraudus , variant of Latin smaragdus ( “ emerald ” ) , from Ancient Greek σμάραγδος ( smáragdos ) , μάραγδος ( máragdos , “ emerald ” ) , from a Semitic language.
