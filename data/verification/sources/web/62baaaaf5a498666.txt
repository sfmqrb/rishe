 Persian
 [ edit ] 

 Alternative forms
 [ edit ] 

 افشانیدن ( afšānīdan  / afšânidan ) , فشاندن ( fašāndan  / fašândan, fišāndan  / fešândan ) , اوشاندن ( awšāndan  / owšândan ) 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian [script needed] ( ʾpšʾn-tn' /⁠afšāndan⁠/ ) , from Proto-Iranian *Hápa ( “ away ” ) + the Iranian root *šan (“to shake”).

 Compare Old Khotanese ṣāñ ( “ to shake down ” ) , Baluchi شانگ ( šánag , “ to vomit, throw, shake out ” ) , Northern Luri شںنێ ( şənı , “ to throw ” ) , Gilaki فشادن ( fišādən , “ to disperse ” ) , Northern Kurdish reşandin ( “ to sprinkle, throw, vomit ” ) ( with prefix *fra ) , Hindi छनना ( channā , “ to be restrained ” ) .

 Verb
 [ edit ] 

 اَفشاندَن • ( afšândan ) ( present stem اَفشان ( afšân ) ) 

 to scatter , disperse 
 Synonyms: ریختن ( rixtan ) , پاشیدن ( pâšidan ) 
 late 1300s , Xāja Shams-ud-Dīn Moḥammad Ḥāfeẓ-e Shīrāzī , “ اگر آن ترک شیرازی به دست آرد دل ما را ( Shirazi Turk )”, in دیوان حافظ [ Divān of Ḥāfeẓ ]: غزل گفتی و در سفتی بیا و خوش بخوان حافظ
که بر نظم تو افشاند فلک عقد ثریا را ġazal gofti o dorr softi, biyâ vo xoš be-xân hâfez
ke bar nazm-e to afšânad falak 'eqd-e sorayyâ-râ You have completed your poem and pierced the pearl; come and sing beautifully, Hafez,
That on your compositions Heaven may scatter the necklace of the Pleiades.
