 Northern Kurdish

 Central Kurdish

 کاروان ( karwan ) 

 کاروان ( karwan ) 

 caravan (a convoy or procession of travelers, their vehicles and cargo, and any pack animals)

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian kʾlwʾn' ( kārawān , “ caravan; military column ” ) . See descendants there.
