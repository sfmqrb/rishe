 Etymology 2
 [ edit ] 

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 *h₂eyǵ- 

 oak 

 Reconstruction notes
 [ edit ] 

 Displays unusual suffixing in most descendants. Kroonen notes that root nouns in Germanic are frequently of unexplained/substrate origin.
