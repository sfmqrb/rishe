 Etymology 1.1
 [ edit ] 

 If the noun goes so far as to mean justice , it is perhaps a semantic loan from Classical Syriac ܩܘܼܫܬܿܵܐ ( qūštā , “ truth, rectitude, justice ” ) , while others go even so far as to consider a borrowing from Latin iūstitia , whereas the use for a measure is Aramaic קִיסְטָא / ܩܸܣܛܵܐ ( qesṭā ) from Medieval Greek ξέστης ( xéstēs ) from Latin sextārius ( “ a cubic measure ” ) . The presence of a denominal verb from قِسْطَاس ( qisṭās , “ weigher ” ) is also possible.

 Etymology 1.2
 [ edit ] 

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 قِسْط • ( qisṭ )  m ( plural أَقْسَاط ( ʔaqsāṭ ) ) 

 a vague amount or time of
 خُذ قِسْطاً مِن الراحة! ― ḵuḏ qisṭan min ar-rāḥa! ― Get some rest! 

 equity , equitability 
 609–632 CE , Qur'an , 3:21 : إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ يَكۡفُرُونَ بِـَٔايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ وَيَقۡتُلُونَ ٱلنَّبِيِّـۧنَ بِغَيۡرِ حَقّٖ وَيَقۡتُلُونَ ٱلَّذِينَ يَأۡمُرُونَ بِٱلۡقِسۡطِ مِنَ ٱلنَّاسِ فَبَشِّرۡهُم بِعَذَابٍ أَلِيمٍ. Lo! those who disbelieve the revelations of Allah, and slay the prophets wrongfully, and slay those of mankind who enjoin equity : promise them a painful doom. 

 lot , portion , share , tranche 
 installment 

 meed , premium 

 payment , payout 

 ( obsolete ) a volume measure of 1.2158 liters in the small and 2.4336 liters in the large variant, 2.106 liters in Egypt

 Ottoman Turkish
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology 1
 [ edit ] 

 Borrowed from Arabic قِسْط ( qisṭ , “ equity; lot, portion, share ” ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 قسط • ( kıst ) ( definite accusative قسطی ( kıstı ) , plural اقساط ( eksât ) ) 

 justice , equity , fairness , impartiality , the state or characteristic of being just or fair 
 Synonyms: انصاف ( insaf ) , عدالت ( ʼadalet ) , عدل ( ʼadl ) 

 share , lot , a portion of something, especially a portion given or allotted to someone
 Synonyms: پای ( pay ) , حصه ( hisse ) , نصیب ( nâsib ) 

 ( finance ) installment , one member of a series of portions of a debt or sum of money
 Synonym: تقسیط ( tâksit ) 

 ( obsolete ) kist , a measure of 1.2158 liters in the small and 2.4336 in the large variant

 Etymology 2
 [ edit ] 

 Borrowed from Arabic قُسْط ( qusṭ , “ costus ” ) , ultimately from Sanskrit कुष्ठ ( kuṣṭha , “ crepe ginger ” ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 قسط • ( kust ) ( definite accusative قسطی ( kustu ) , plural قسطلر ( kustlar ) ) 

 costus , a composite growing on the Himalayas whose root has been used in traditional medicine ( Saussurea costus )
