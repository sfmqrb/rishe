 This Proto-Indo-European entry contains reconstructed terms and roots . As such, the term(s) in this entry are not directly attested , but are hypothesized to have existed based on comparative evidence.

 Proto-Indo-European
 [ edit ] 

 Root
 [ edit ] 

 *h₂eyḱ- [ 1 ] 

 to appropriate , possess 

 Reconstruction notes
 [ edit ] 

 The a- vowels in Tocharian and Germanic could be from a pervasive o -grade, but *h₂- on e -grade verbs is considered a more likely explanation. The Italic evidence, however, can only reflect initial *h₂ey- .

 Alternative reconstructions
 [ edit ] 

 *Heyḱ- [ 2 ]
