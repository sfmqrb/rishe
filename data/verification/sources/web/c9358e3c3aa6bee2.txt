 [ edit ] 

 Inherited from Middle Persian [script needed] ( pnc /⁠panǰ⁠/ ) , from Proto-Iranian *pánča , from Proto-Indo-Iranian *pánča , from Proto-Indo-European *pénkʷe . Compare Northern Kurdish pênc , Ossetian фондз ( fonʒ ) , Pashto پنځه ( pindzə ) , Avestan 𐬞𐬀𐬧𐬗𐬀 ( paṇca ) , Sanskrit पञ्च ( páñca ) , Sarikoli pundz .

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Ultimately inherited from Sanskrit पञ्च ( páñca ) , from Proto-Indo-Iranian *pánča , ultimately from Proto-Indo-European *pénkʷe ; but also unusually heavily influenced by Classical Persian پنج ( panj ) , an Indo-Iranian cognate. Compare with other descendants of Prakrit 𑀧𑀁𑀘 ( paṃca ) . Cognate with Assamese পাঁচ ( pãs ) , Baluchi پنج ( panj ) , Bengali পাঁচ ( pãc ) , English five , Hindustani ( Hindi पाँच ( pā̃c ) / Urdu پانچ ( pā̃c ) ), Persian پنج ( panj ) , Romani panʒ , Russian пять ( pjatʹ ) , Sylheti ꠙꠣꠌ ( fas ) .
