 Persian

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Uncertain. Related to Sanskrit कुञ्चित ( kuñcita , “ curved, bent, crooked ” ) , कुञ्चन ( kuñcana , “ contraction ” ) , Old Armenian կնճիռն ( knčiṙn , “ knot ” ) .
