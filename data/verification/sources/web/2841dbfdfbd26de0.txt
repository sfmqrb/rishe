 Chagatai
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Borrowed from Classical Persian خَاکِی ( xākī ) . By surface analysis , خاک ( xāk , “ soil, earth ” ) +‎ ـی ( -ī , “ nisbah suffix ” ) .

 Adjective
 [ edit ] 

 خاکی • ( xākī ) 

 khaki 

 of or related to earth or soil , earthy , soily 

 Descendants
 [ edit ] 

 Uzbek: xoki

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 (This etymology is missing or incomplete. Please add to it, or discuss it at the Etymology scriptorium .) 
From Middle Persian [Term?] . By surface analysis , خاک ( xāk  / xâk , “ earth, soil ” ) +‎ ـی ( -ī  / -i , suffix forming adjectives ) .
