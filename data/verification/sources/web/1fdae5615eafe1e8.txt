 Persian
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian [script needed] ( mdk' /⁠ma(y)ik⁠/ ) , from Proto-Iranian *madika , a variant of Proto-Iranian *madaxa ( “ locust, grasshopper ” ) , and thus related to ملخ ( malax ) ; see there for further information.

 The second component is from Middle Persian [Term?] ( /⁠-ūg⁠/ , “ -like ” ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 میگو • ( meygu ) ( plural میگوها ) 

 shrimp , prawn
