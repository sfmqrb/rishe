 Persian
 [ edit ] 

 Alternative forms
 [ edit ] 

 میون ( miyun ) ( Tehrani ) 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Middle Persian mdyʾn' ( mayān ) , from Old Persian *madyānaʰ , from Proto-Iranian *mádyānah , composed of *mádyah +‎ *-anah , from Proto-Indo-Iranian *mádʰyas , ultimately from Proto-Indo-European *médʰyos . Cognate with Avestan 𐬨𐬀𐬌𐬜𐬌𐬌𐬀 ( ma i δiia ) , Sanskrit मध्य ( madhya ) , Ossetian мидӕг ( midæg ) , Gilaki مئن (men) and Arabic مَيْدَان ( maydān ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 میان • ( miyān / miyân ) ( Tajik spelling миён ) 

 middle 
 در این میان ― dar in miyân ― in the midst of this 

 centre 
 Synonym: وسط ( vasat ) 

 ( poetic ) waist 

 ( Dari , Indo-Persian ) lord , sir
