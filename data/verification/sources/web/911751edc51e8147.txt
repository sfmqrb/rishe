 Wikipedia 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Borrowed from Latin Pasargadae , from Ancient Greek Πασαργάδαι ( Pasargádai ) , ultimately from Old Persian *Pāθragadā . Doublet of Pasargad .

 Proper noun
 [ edit ] 

 Pasargadae 

 ( historical ) An ancient city of Persia , a capital of the Achaemenid Empire .
