 Alternative forms

 Emmanuel ( figure whose birth is foretold ) 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Hebrew עִמָּנוּאֵל ( ʿimanuʾél ) , from עִמָּנוּ אֵל ( ʿimánu ʾél , literally “ God is with us ” ) .

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 IPA ( key ) : /ɪˈmænjuəl/ , /ɪˈmænjəl/ 

 Audio ( Southern England ) : ( file ) 

 Homophone : Emmanuel 

 Proper noun
 [ edit ] 

 Immanuel ( plural Immanuels ) 

 ( biblical ) A figure whose birth is prophesied in the Book of Isaiah in the Bible.
 1611 , The Holy Bible,   [ … ] ( King James Version ), London: [ … ] Robert Barker ,   [ … ] , →OCLC , Isaiah 7:14 : Behold, a virgin shall conceive, and bear a son, and shall call his name Immanuel . 

 ( Christianity ) This figure, regarded by Christians as the Messiah or Christ .

 A male given name from Hebrew : alternative form of Emmanuel .

 Alternative forms
 [ edit ] 

 Imanuel , Emanuel , Emmanuel 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Derived from Biblical Hebrew עִמָּנוּאֵל ( ʿImmānūʾēl , “ God is with us ” ) , from עִמָּנוּ ( ʿimmānū , “ with us ” ) and אֵל ( ʾēl , “ God ” ) .

 Compare Danish Immanuel , Swedish Immanuel , German Immanuel and English Immanuel .

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 IPA ( key ) : /ɪˈmɑː.nʉ.ɛl/ 

 Rhymes: -ɑːnʉɛl 

 Homophone : Imanuel 

 Hyphenation: Im‧ma‧nu‧el 

 Proper noun
 [ edit ] 

 Immanuel 

 ( biblical ) Immanuel ; the child whose birth is foretold in the Book of Isaiah and whose name is interpreted in the Gospel of Matthew as referring to Jesus 

 a male given name from Biblical Hebrew, equivalent to English Immanuel 

 Usage notes
 [ edit ] 

 Immanuel is rare as a first given name in Norway. Among Norwegian-born bearers recorded for the period 1900–2017, 87% had it as their second given name. [ 1 ] 

 Statistics
 [ edit ] 

 According to Statistics Norway , 28 men in Norway had Immanuel as their sole or first given name as of 31 December 2025. [ 2 ]

 Swedish
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Hebrew עמנואל ( ‘immánû ’Él ) , meaning God with us .

 Proper noun
 [ edit ] 

 Immanuel   c ( genitive Immanuels ) 

 ( biblical ) Immanuel 
 1917 , 1773 års bibelkommission , “Jesaja [ Isaiah ] 7:14”, in 1917 års kyrkobibel ‎ [1] , © Svenska Bibelsällskapet, accessed at Bible.com, archived from the original on 9 September 2025 : Se, den unga kvinnan skall varda havande och föda en son, och hon skall giva honom namnet Immanuel . Behold, the young woman will conceive and bear a son, and she will give him the name Immanuel . 

 2000 , 1973 års bibelkommission , “Matteusevangeliet [ Matthew ] 1:23”, in Bibel 2000 ‎ [2] , © Svenska Bibelsällskapet, accessed at Bible.com, archived from the original on 9 September 2025 : Jungfrun skall bli havande och föda en son, och man skall ge honom namnet Immanuel (det betyder: Gud med oss). The virgin will conceive and give birth to a son, and they will call him Immanuel (which means "God with us"). 

 ( rare ) a male given name from Hebrew : alternative form of Emanuel .
 1933 , Ivar Lo-Johansson , Godnatt, jord , Albert Bonniers förlag, published 1950 , pages 127–128 : —Du fick heta Mikael du, sade hon en gång, då hon gick förbi med grädden i en båtformad snäcka. Det var jag som föreslog det. Jag hade alltid tyckt om det namnet.
Mikael hörde hjärtat dunka under blusen och förblev stum.
—Pappa heter Immanuel , sade han äntligen stammande. Det har väl farmor också gett honom?
—Ja, Immanuel , det är också ett vackert namn... Då tycktes hon bli litet tankspridd. (please add an English translation of this quotation)
