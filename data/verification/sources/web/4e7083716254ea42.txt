 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Probably from a Proto-Indo-European *bʰeǵʰ- ( “ outside ” ) , which appears to be a formation from *bʰe ( instrumental particle ) + *-ǵʰs ( “ out ” ) , in which case cognate with Proto-Slavic *bez , Lithuanian bè , Latvian bez , Old Prussian bhe . [ 1 ] 

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 ( Vedic ) IPA ( key ) : /bɐ.ɦís/ 

 ( Classical Sanskrit ) IPA ( key ) : /bɐ.ɦis̪/ 

 Adverb
 [ edit ] 

 ब॒हिस् • ( bahís ) [ 2 ] 

 out , forth , outwards , outside 
 c. 1200 BCE – 1000 BCE , Atharvaveda 

 ( with ablative or at the beginning of a compound ) out of , apart from , except , beside
