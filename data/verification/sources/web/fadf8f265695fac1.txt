 This Proto-Indo-European entry contains reconstructed terms and roots . As such, the term(s) in this entry are not directly attested , but are hypothesized to have existed based on comparative evidence.

 See also: Reconstruction:Proto-Indo-European/h₂elh₂-

 Root

 *h₂el- ( imperfective ) [ 1 ] 

 to grow , nourish

 Etymology 3
 [ edit ] 

 Root
 [ edit ] 

 *h₂el- 

 all ; each , every ; entire , whole 

 Reconstruction notes
 [ edit ] 

 Largely nominal or adverbial, with determiners arising in Germanic and Celtic. Connected to 'Root 1' ("to grow") by Dunkel, [ 1 ] to 'Root 2' ("beyond") by Pokorny.
