 This Proto-Germanic entry contains reconstructed terms and roots . As such, the term(s) in this entry are not directly attested , but are hypothesized to have existed based on comparative evidence.

 Proto-Germanic
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From earlier *yunhós (from which /h/ > /g/ due to Verner's law ), from Proto-Indo-European *h₂yuHn̥ḱós ( “ young ” ) , from *h₂yuh₁en- ( “ young ” ) . [ 1 ] The shortening of the vowel from earlier *-uHun- is due to Osthoff's law .

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 IPA ( key ) : /ˈjuŋ.ɡɑz/ 

 Adjective
 [ edit ]
