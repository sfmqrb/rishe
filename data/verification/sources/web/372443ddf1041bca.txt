 English

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 pagode ( plural pagodes ) 

 ( obsolete ) Alternative spelling of pagod , an idol . 
 1583 , Ralph Fitch , Voyage to Ormus, and so to Goa in the East India , in John Pinkerton (ed.), A General Collection of the Best and Most Interesting Voyages and Travels in All Parts of the World , London: Longman, Hurst, Rees, Orme & Brown, 1811, [1] 
 And the house wherein his Pagode or idol standeth, is covered with tiles of silver, and all the walls are gilded with gold. 

 ( obsolete ) Alternative spelling of pagoda , various structures , a former coin , a kind of sleeve .

 pagode 
 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From Malayalam പകോതി ( pakōti , “ Durga temple ” ) or close cognates in Kannada and Tamil , all ultimately from Sanskrit भगवती ( bhagavatī , “ Holy One , used for goddesses ” ) .

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 ( Brazil ) IPA ( key ) : /paˈɡɔ.d͡ʒi/ 

 ( Portugal ) IPA ( key ) : /pɐˈɡɔ.dɨ/ [pɐˈɣɔ.ðɨ] 

 ( Portugal ) IPA ( key ) : /pɐˈɡɔ.dɨ/ [pɐˈɣɔ.ðɨ] 
 ( Southern Portugal ) IPA ( key ) : /pɐˈɡɔ.dɨ/ 

 Rhymes: ( Brazil ) -ɔd͡ʒi , ( Portugal ) -ɔdɨ 

 Hyphenation: pa‧go‧de 

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 pagode   m ( plural pagodes ) 

 ( Hinduism , obsolete ) Hindu temple 
 1720 , Rafael Bluteau , “pagode”, in Vocabulario Portuguez e Latino [ Portuguese and Latin Vocabulary ]‎ [2] , volume 6, Lisbon: Pascoal da Sylva, page 185 , column 1: Dizem que no Reyno de Golgonda ha hum pagode [ … ] onde os Gentios fazem a ſua oração It's said that in the Golkonda Kingdom there is a pagoda [ … ] where the gentiles pray 

 ( Hinduism , obsolete ) Hindu idol 

 ( Buddhism ) pagoda ( an East Asian tiered tower with multiple eaves ) 

 ( historical ) pagoda ( Indian unit of currency ) 
 1720 , Rafael Bluteau , “pagode”, in Vocabulario Portuguez e Latino [ Portuguese and Latin Vocabulary ]‎ [3] , volume 6, Lisbon: Pascoal da Sylva, page 185 , column 1: Tambem he moeda de ouro [ … ] que o Gentio da India fabrica, [ … ] foy chamada pagode . It is also (the name of) a gold coin [ … ] minted by the Indian gentiles, [ … ] it was named pagoda . 

 ( music ) pagoda ( a subgenre of samba )
