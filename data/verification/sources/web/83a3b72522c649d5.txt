 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology tree

 Proto-Indo-European *gʰrebh₂- der. 
 Old Persian [Term?] ( /⁠grab-⁠/ ) 
 Middle Persian [Term?] ( /⁠griftan⁠/ ) 
 Persian گرفتن 

 Inherited from Middle Persian [Term?] ( /⁠griftan⁠/ ) , from Old Persian [Term?] ( /⁠grab-⁠/ , “ to seize ” ) , from Proto-Indo-European *gʰrebh₂- ( “ to grab, sieze ” ) . Cognates include Central Kurdish گرتن ( girtin ) , Northern Luri گرتێ ( geretı ) , Baluchi گرگ ( girag ) , Sanskrit गृह्णाति ( gṛhṇāti , “ he seizes ” ) , Russian гра́бить ( grábitʹ ) , German greifen , and English grab .

 Verb
 [ edit ] 

 گرفتن • ( giriftan  / gereftan ) ( present stem گیر ( gīr  / gir ) , Tajik spelling гирифтан ) 

 to grab 

 to grip 

 to take 

 to get 

 to catch 
 گربه دو موش را گرفت . gorbe do muš râ gereft . The cat caught two mice. 

 to hold 

 to seize 

 to receive
