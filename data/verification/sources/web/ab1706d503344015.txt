 English
 [ edit ] 

 Painting of a woman playing a santur .

 Alternative forms
 [ edit ] 

 santūr , santour , santouri , santoor , santir 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 Borrowed from Classical Persian سَنْتُور ( santūr ) , of uncertain origin; sometimes suggested to be from Ancient Greek ψαλτήριον ( psaltḗrion , “ psaltery ” ) .

 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 santur ( plural santurs ) 

 ( music ) A type of hammered dulcimer played in Persian music
