 This Proto-Indo-European entry contains reconstructed terms and roots . As such, the term(s) in this entry are not directly attested , but are hypothesized to have existed based on comparative evidence.

 Proto-Indo-European
 [ edit ] 

 Root
 [ edit ] 

 *pekʷ- ( imperfective ) [ 1 ] [ 2 ] [ 3 ] 

 ? ( Anatolian , perhaps originally ) to crack , crush ( foodstuff ) , pulverize ( grain ) 

 to cook 
 Synonyms: *bʰeh₃g- , *bʰreǵ- , *lendʰ- ? , ( possibly ) *teyh₁- 

 to ripen 

 Reconstruction
 [ edit ] 

 It has been suggested [ 4 ] [ 5 ] that Hittite and Lydian terms which point to a root *pekʷ- ( “ to pound, crush, crack (grain) ” ) may preserve the original meaning of the PIE root, which later shifted in meaning to “to prepare food, cook” by the ancestor of the non-Anatolian branches. The semantic development “cook” → “ripen” occurred later and has parallels in many languages of the world.
