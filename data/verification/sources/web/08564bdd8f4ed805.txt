 Noun
 [ edit ] 

 شاه • ( šāh / šâh ) ( plural شاهان ( šāhān / šâhân ) , Tajik spelling шоҳ ) 

 king ; shah 

 ( chess ) king 

 ( in compounds ) Something that is the biggest or the best of its kind.

 ( archaic ) bridegroom , son-in-law , or brother-in-law 
 10 th Century CE , Ferdowsi , Shahnameh 
 مگر شاه را نزد ماه آوریم / به نزدیک او پایگاه آوریم magar šâh râ nazd-e mâh âvarim / be nazdik-e 'u pâygâh âvarim [... We shall not give up] unless we bring the groom ( Zāl ) before the Moon ( metaphor : Rudāba ), / and settle him by her side. 

 Synonyms: داماد ( dâmâd ) , شاه‌داماد ( šâh-dâmâd )
