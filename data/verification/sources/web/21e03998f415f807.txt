 This Proto-Germanic entry contains reconstructed terms and roots . As such, the term(s) in this entry are not directly attested , but are hypothesized to have existed based on comparative evidence.

 Proto-Germanic
 [ edit ] 

 Etymology
 [ edit ] 

 From earlier *senwō , [ 1 ] [ 2 ] from Proto-Indo-European *senh₁-weh₂- , a formation related to the more typical *snḗh₁wr̥ ( “ sinew, tendon ” ) found in other Indo-European languages. [ 3 ] 

 Pronunciation
 [ edit ] 

 IPA ( key ) : /ˈsin.wɔː/ 

 Noun
 [ edit ]
